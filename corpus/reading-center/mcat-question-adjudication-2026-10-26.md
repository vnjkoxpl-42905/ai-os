# Keep two questions out of learner practice

All three passage bodies, twelve questions, forty-eight choices and twelve supplied explanations in the [expanded original](../../raw/sources/clips/mcat-cars-questions-revision-2026-10-26/source.md) were read on 2026-10-26. This is a passage-grounded editorial review, not an official answer-key validation. Original wording and keys remain unchanged in that immutable source.

## Use the reasoning distinctions, not this captured bank

Recommendation: retain all source evidence and this adjudication for private editorial work; do not import or assign these twelve questions. Ten have a defensible best answer, including four with explanation/wording qualifications, and two require quarantine. No learner pilot was chosen.

The expanded capture attributes underlying adapted texts to Plato/Jowett, Thoreau and A. C. Seward with Gutenberg links. Those attributions do not establish the rights to this particular adaptation, questions or explanations. The commercial-host clue is a Shemmassian question-bank link; the originating page URL and permission remain unknown. No current terms, original editions or jurisdiction-specific rights were checked. Every question therefore has the same unresolved rights boundary even where its reasoning is sound. This review does not certify passage-only rights retroactively.

Immediate local adoption: use original examples in the [RC source adoption checklist](../../references/rc-source-adoption-checklist.md) to practice (1) separating an author from a person discussed, (2) identifying the function of an analogy, (3) preserving quantity and qualifier strength, and (4) distinguishing a rhetorical description from a recommendation. These reusable distinctions do not require copying the questionable questions into student material.
