# Check whether earlier paragraphs survive later reading

Private teacher method recovered from Ellen Cassidy's Decaf RC workout, captured 2026-08-27. Use it when observed recall loses earlier details as a passage continues. This is a practice protocol, not a universal timed-test prescription or evidence that any particular student needs it.

## Check recall cumulatively

Have the student read a paragraph, restate its information in their own words and react to its specific content. Looking away, they retell that paragraph. After the second paragraph, they retell it and then the first two together. Continue the cumulative retelling as later paragraphs are added.

The source's target is a full translation, including relevant names, dates, lists and qualifiers. A gist or main point is a different output. Keep that distinction explicit when checking the exercise. Success on a gist task does not establish success on a detailed-recall task.

Start aloud so the teacher can hear omissions, additions and changed relationships. Check those against the passage, and record the actual discrepancy. The source later permits silent practice, but silent confidence alone does not show the teacher what was retained.

## Choose it for an observed reading problem

Separate losing the passage from misunderstanding a question task or running out of time earlier in the section. The [tutoring diagnosis framework](../../../tutoring-diagnosis.md) explains those distinct hypotheses. For LR recall after analysis, the related Proto-Stimulus Rebound protocol retells the stimulus after CLIR, checks missing/added/changed information, and records the discrepancy; see the [method collection](../../../loophole-online/index.md).

When this method addresses an actual student observation, record the evidence in that student's teaching notes and link this shared page. Do not duplicate the entire drill or infer a learning problem merely because this source recommends it. [Student-record conventions](../../../../students/README.md).

Cassidy's stated workload, 12-minute aloud/8-minute silent goals and advice for 70-minute accommodations are historical vendor recommendations. They were not adopted as Joshua's timing targets or assigned to anyone.
