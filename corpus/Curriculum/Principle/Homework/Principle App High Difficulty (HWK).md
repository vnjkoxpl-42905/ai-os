# Principle-Strengthen | Application (mini-set)

## Debrief

Find the gap between a Rule and its Application on these 2 HARD Principle-Strengthen questions.

### Example

- **PRINCIPLE:** Lies are justified if they do more good than harm.
- **APPLICATION:** Jason falsely claimed that Sharon has a driver's license to spare her getting mocked by her friends. Since he did manage to spare her the mockery, he was justified in lying.

In order to use this Rule to prove that a lie was justified, we have to establish the trigger: the lie did more good than harm.

So the correct answer would need to convince us that "keeping Sharon from getting mocked = did more good than harm".

Try these two questions, untimed. Focus on predicting what you want before you look at answers.

- Think about each Principle as having some criteria that must be met to trigger the rule, and having an outcome that matches up with the Application's conclusion.
- The Application will conclude the right side of the Principle.
- We're just being tested on how the Evidence in the Application paragraph failed to clearly establish the trigger (left side) of the Principle.
- Think about which part(s) of the Principle's trigger wasn't clearly established, and keep that mantra in your head as you consider answer choices.
