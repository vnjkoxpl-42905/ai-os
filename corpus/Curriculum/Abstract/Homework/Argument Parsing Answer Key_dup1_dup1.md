# Argument Parsing - Answer Key

## Part 1: Conclusion & Evidence

1. **"Infers that X on the grounds that Y"** — Conclusion: X (what is being inferred). Evidence: Y (the grounds for the inference).
2. **"Doing X by doing Y"** — Conclusion (the goal): X. Evidence (the method): Y.
3. **"Using X to support Y"** — Conclusion: Y (what is being supported). Evidence: X (what is used for support).
4. **"Concludes, from the claim that X, that Y"** — Conclusion: Y. Evidence: X (the claim it is based on).

## Part 2: Premise & Conclusion

5. **"Concludes X on the basis of Y"** — Premise: Y. Conclusion: X.
6. **"Infers, on the grounds that X, that Y"** — Premise: X. Conclusion: Y.
7. **"Takes for granted that Y because X"** — Premise: X (the reason). Conclusion: Y (the thing taken for granted).
8. **"Y is true merely on the basis of X"** — Premise: X. Conclusion: Y.

## Part 3: Vocabulary

9. **Another name for "Supported Premise"?** — Intermediate Conclusion (or Sub-Conclusion).
