# Strengthen-Application

## Why?

We're given a Principle: "If X, then Y."

We're given an Application: "Blah blah blah. Therefore, Y."

Our job is to figure out where "blah blah blah" fell short of clearly establishing X.

## Set an 18-minute time limit

Attempt all the problems within that time limit, but feel free to revisit some of them after your time limit expires but before you confirm your answer.

Think about each Principle as having some **trigger** — criteria that must be met for the rule to apply — and having an **outcome** that will match up with the conclusion in the Application paragraph.

What we're being tested on is what language in the trigger we still haven't established.

The winning answer is the one that best delivers us that missing idea from the Trigger of the principle.
