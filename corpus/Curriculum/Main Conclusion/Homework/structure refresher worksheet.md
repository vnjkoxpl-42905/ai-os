probably have broken *Apatosaurus*'s legs.

before breaking. By taking into account the diameter and density of
*Apatosaurus* leg bones, it is possible to calculate that those bones
could not have withstood the strains of galloping.

**Question 18: Find the Conclusion**

In a recent study of more than 400 North American men and women whose
previous heart attack put them at risk for a second heart attack, about
half were told to switch to a "Mediterranean-type diet"---one rich in
fish, vegetables, olive oil, and grains---while the other half were
advised to eat a more traditional "Western" diet but to limit their fat
intake. Those following the Mediterranean diet were significantly less
likely than those in the other group to have a second heart attack. So,
a diet may not have to be extremely low in fat in order to protect the
heart.
