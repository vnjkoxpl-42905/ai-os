# Voice Calibration — Locked Profile

This profile applies across all LSAT U work, not just Main Conclusion. Saved as a permanent rule in Claude's memory: see `memory_snapshot/lsat_u_voice_and_content.md`.

## Two-register voice

### Register 1 — Structured / Decisive

Used for: Reference module, Question Simulator audits, drill instructions, procedural callouts, named-tool boxes.

**Traits:**
- **Verdict-style answer audits.** "Too strong." "Unsupported." "Stay narrow." "Out of scope."
- **Imperative procedural voice.** Short, single-imperative sentences. "Read piece. Cover piece. Rephrase from memory."
- **Naming convention for teaching tools.** Every method gets a memorable label.
- **Confidence calibration phrases** (used sparingly, once per module max). "The most salvageable question type ever." "The most lovable question type ever." "Don't over-complicate this."

**Decision:** preserve all four traits.

### Register 2 — Whimsical / Parodic

Used for: Lessons module prose, drill setup framing, voice-rich teaching moments.

**Traits:**
- **Concrete absurdist metaphors.** Cake-on-blocks, monsters called cluster sentences, beautiful starships valid conclusions, conclusions are worthless and ridiculous like this.
- **Counterfactual drill premises.** Microphones drink coffee at 8:36 AM, QWERTY-keyboard-prison, Sophie Spradlin cholesterol, fish are made of pure green light.
- **Mid-prose ALL-CAPS.** "HARK, A SIMPLE ARGUMENT" / "MEMORIZE (TATTOO IT IF YOU HAVE TO)" / "AND YOU WILL SOON FIND OUT EVEN RC SECTION STAY TUNED."
- **Onomatopoeia and reader-as-character asides.** "(aghhhhhhhhh)"
- **Personification of abstract concepts.** "Beautiful starships valid conclusions"; "conclusions are worthless and ridiculous like this; if left to their own devices, conclusions would turn into a hot mess on the floor."

**Decision:** preserve all five traits. ALL-CAPS tightened (used as character beats, not as shouting).
