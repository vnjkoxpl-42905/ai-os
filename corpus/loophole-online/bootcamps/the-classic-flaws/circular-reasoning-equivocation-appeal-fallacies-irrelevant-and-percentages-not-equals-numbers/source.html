Observed attempt 1: A Fishing equipment can pose a threat to dolphins, impacting up to one third of dolphins worldwide. Therefore, entanglement in nets have a major impact on the survival of many dolphins.

Question 5

Which one of the following is an example of Percentages ≠ Numbers?

Ah, not quite

Observed attempt 2: B Fishing gear is a major threat to dolphins, harming up to 50% of dolphins globally. We can conclude from this that at least half of the world's dolphins may not suffer at the hands of fishing gear.

Question 5

Which one of the following is an example of Percentages ≠ Numbers?

Ah, not quite

Observed attempt 3: C Roughly 10% of dolphins worldwide were entangled in fishing nets in 2020, down from 20% in 2000. Clearly, a greater number of dolphins globally are avoiding fishing nets in 2020 than did 20 years prior.

Question 5

Which one of the following is an example of Percentages ≠ Numbers?

Correct!

Percentages and numbers are not the same. Just because a raw number went up, that doesn't mean there was an increase in percentage. If the percentage changed, that doesn't mean the raw number changed.
