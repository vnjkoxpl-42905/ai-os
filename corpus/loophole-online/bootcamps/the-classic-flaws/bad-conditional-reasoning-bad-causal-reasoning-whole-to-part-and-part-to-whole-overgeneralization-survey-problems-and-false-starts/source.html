Question 2

Which one of the following is an example of Bad Causal Reasoning?

Observed attempt 1: A Dolphins sleep with only one eye closed and are acutely aware of their surroundings at all hours of the day. It follows that sleeping with one eye open produces such awareness in dolphins.

Question 2

Which one of the following is an example of Bad Causal Reasoning?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

On the LSAT, we cannot infer a causal relationship from mere correlation. Bad Causal Reasoning arguments have premises about two things being correlated, and conclude one of the things causes the other. Remember your three Omitted Options:

1. What if a new factor caused one or both these things? 

2. What if there’s no relationship here at all?

3. What if there's backwards causation?

Question 5

Which one of the following is an example of an Overgeneralization flaw?

Correct!

Overgeneralization is the part-to-part(s) flaw. In a stimulus with an Overgeneralization flaw, the premises will talk about something having a property, and the conclusion will go on to say a bunch of other things also have that property.

Question 6

Which one of the following is an example of Survey Problems?

Observed attempt 1: A A survey of a large, representative sample of Florida residents found that most respondents admitted to having a pet dolphin. We can conclude that most Americans have made tangible contact with a water mammal.

Question 6

Which one of the following is an example of Survey Problems?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

Survey Problems can take the following forms:

- Biased samples

Suppose a survey only interviews college students, and then draws a conclusion about the average American's opinion.

- Biased questions

Suppose a survey question said, "Only malcontents like ice cream. Do you like ice cream?"

- Other contradictory surveys

Suppose a survey concluded that 100% of Americans like ice cream, and another survey said 90% of Americans despise all cold desserts.

- Survey liars

Suppose the police are conducting a survey about how often people have engaged in illegal behavior.

- Small sample size

Suppose a survey reports that 100% of Americans like ice cream, but the survey included just one person.

Question 8

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

False Starts arguments will always assume that the two groups in the stimulus are the same in all respects except the ones called out as part of the study.
