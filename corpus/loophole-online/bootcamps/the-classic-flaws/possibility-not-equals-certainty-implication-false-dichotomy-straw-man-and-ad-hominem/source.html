Which one of the following is an example of an Implication flaw?

Observed attempt 1: A Dolphins think graham crackers are the tastiest snack. Graham crackers are made by Nabisco, so dolphins think that Nabisco makes the tastiest snacks.

Question 2

Which one of the following is an example of an Implication flaw?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

In an Implication flaw, someone will have a belief. The argument will then supply a necessary implication of that belief and claim that the speaker must believe the logically required implication of their belief. But remember, the person in question may not be aware of the logical implications of their beliefs.
