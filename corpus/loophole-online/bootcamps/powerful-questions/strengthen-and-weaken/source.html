Question 6

Which one of the following is a Strengthen question stem?

Correct!

Strengthen questions ask you to make the conclusion more likely to be true. Stems that ask you to prove or justify the conclusion are Sufficient Assumption questions. When you have a "most" thrown in the stem, it's a Strengthen.

Question 9

On a Strengthen question, can the answer bring in new information to make the conclusion more likely to be true?

Correct!

Strengthen correct answers can introduce new information to help the conclusion. 

Imagine you have a conclusion claiming that smoking caused heart disease in a study. A correct Strengthen answer choice might say that study participants maintained a heart-healthy diet. This new information makes the conclusion more likely to be true because it rules out other potential causes of heart disease besides smoking.
