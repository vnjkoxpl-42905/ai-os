Question 2

How difficult is it to prove the statement above?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

All cats have stripes is a Powerful statement; it would take checking every single cat to prove it and you could disprove it in literally one cat. Powerful statements like this one take a ton of examples to prove and very few examples to disprove.

Powerful statements are fragile and easily damaged. They are easy to disprove.
