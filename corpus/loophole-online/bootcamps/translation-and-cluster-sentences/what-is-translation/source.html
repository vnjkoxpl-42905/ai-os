Question 3

Initial observed state

Question 3

Here are a few key takeaways about Translation, just so you remember.

1. You must translate every LSAT stimulus.

If you can't translate the stimulus, you don't understand it well enough to answer the question.

2. Translating is the opposite of skimming.

Translation is not "getting the gist." It's something you do after you have more than the gist.

3. At first you will have to translate consciously.

It will quickly become subconscious with practice.

Soon, you will "just know" everything the stimulus says from reading it. That's the goal, but you've got to stay ultra-disciplined until it becomes that automatic.
