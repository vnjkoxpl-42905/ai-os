Question 7

Using middle-out, let's identify which parts of the following sentence are core elements and which parts are optional elements.

The book, which was a gift, sat on the shelf.

Is "which was a gift" an optional element or a core element?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

When you have two or more commas in a sentence, use middle-out. If the middle piece of the sentence can be a complete thought by itself, both outer pieces are optional. If the middle piece can’t be a complete thought, it's an optional element.
