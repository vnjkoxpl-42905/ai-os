Question 3

If it is sunny, then we will go to the dog park.

Which one of the following is a correct diagram and contrapositive of the above statement?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

The contrapositive statement is implicit in our original statement. When taking the contrapositive of a conditional statement, you reverse and negate the statement.

Every time it is sunny outside, we head straight to that dog park. So, if we're not going to the dog park, that must mean it's not sunny.

Original: S --> DP

Contrapositive: ~DP --> ~S
