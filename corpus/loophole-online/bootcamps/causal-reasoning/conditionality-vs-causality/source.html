Question 5

Based only on the statement above, what do we not know about obedience and flossing?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

According to this conditional statement, obedient children consistently floss. But we can't say why obedient children consistently floss. Obedience here is presented as a sufficient indicator, not a cause that produces an effect.

Question 10

Are there any temporal elements to the relationship between causal reasoning and conditional reasoning?

Observed attempt 1: A In conditional reasoning, there are no necessary temporal requirements. In causal reasoning, there is a necessary temporal requirement.

Question 10

Are there any temporal elements to the relationship between causal reasoning and conditional reasoning?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

Conditionality allows for no necessary temporal requirements, but causality requires the cause to come at the time of the effect or before.
