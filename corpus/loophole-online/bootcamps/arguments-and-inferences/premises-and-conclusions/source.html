Question 8

Initial observed state

Question 8

Here is an example of a premise indicator showing us which parts of the argument are which:

Red velvet cake is the best kind of cake given that it is moist and cream cheese-y.

"Given that" precedes "it is moist and cream cheese-y." This tells us that "it is moist and cream cheese-y" is a premise and the other half of the sentence is the conclusion.

Premise: Red velvet cake is moist and cream cheese-y.

Conclusion: Red velvet cake is the best kind of cake.
