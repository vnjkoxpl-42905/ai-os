Question 5

Is this argument valid or invalid?

Premise 1: The only type of dye ever found in red velvet cake is red food dye.

Premise 2: Only food that is always made with green food dye is low in sodium.

Conclusion: Red velvet cake is not low in sodium.

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

Low sodium food must contain green food dye, and red velvet cake does not fit in this category since it contains only red food dye. So we know for sure that red velvet cake is not low in sodium.
