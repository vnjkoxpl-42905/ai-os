Question 3

How can you tell if a statement is conceptually Provable?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

Remember how I asked you to write down the conceptual Powerful-Provable standard at the start of the Chapter 9 Bootcamp? If you haven’t already made note of this standard, here it is again to note down: 

If you need more evidence to prove something than to disprove it, you have a Powerful claim.

If you need more evidence to disprove it than to prove it, you have a Provable claim. 

Apply this standard throughout your prep to understand whether a statement is Powerful or Provable on a deeper level, beyond just keywords.
