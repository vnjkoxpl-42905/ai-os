Question 16

Could a Crazy Nonsense answer choice ever be correct?

Correct!

We can only discern whether an answer is Crazy Nonsense on the basis of the entire answer’s conceptual Translation and that Translation’s relationship to the ideas presented in the stimulus as a whole. We cannot determine whether an answer choice is Crazy Nonsense simply on the basis of word matching or lack thereof.

Correct answers often use words that aren’t in the stimulus. Here are a few examples of correct answers that can look like Crazy Nonsense: answers that point to a Loophole in the argument, an Omitted Option on Bad Causal Reasoning, a member of a category discussed in the stimulus, or a Grouped Opposite.
