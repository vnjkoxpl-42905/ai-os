Question 7

All major players in the animation industry have a special interest. Only major players in the animation industry lack concern for human anatomical proportions. But all and only those with a special interest have known true joy. If one occupies a rich internal world, then one will invariably lack concern for human anatomical proportions.

Which one of the following is an example of a Provable Conditional answer choice for the stimulus above?

Correct! You got it right on the first try!

Here’s the whole chain again: RIW → ~CHAP → MPA → SI ←> KTJ

“Only if someone does not occupy a rich internal world will they never know true joy” diagrams to ~KTJ → ~RIW. This activates the contrapositive of our chain above and properly reads from ~KTJ to ~RIW along the contrapositive chain, negating as we go. This is a provable reading of our conditional chain. 

“Only if someone occupies a rich internal world will they know true joy” diagrams to KTJ → RIW. This attempts to read our chain backwards without negating. It is not a valid reading of our conditional chain, so it is not provable. 

“Only if someone has a special interest will they fail to occupy a rich internal world” diagrams to ~RIW→ SI. This attempts to read our chain forwards from RIW, but given that this statement has negated RIW, it doesn't activate our chain. We don’t know anything about what happens when ~RIW is true, as we cannot read our conditional chain forwards with a negated sufficient condition. This is not a valid reading of our conditional chain, so it is not provable.
