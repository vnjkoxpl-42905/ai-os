Question 4

What does Could signify?

Correct!

Check out how this technical definition of Could appears in our crawfish example:

Though cooked crawfish appears red, there is a 1% chance the live animals are also found in a variety of colors. Because there is a 1% chance they are close relatives of lobsters, there is a 1% chance blue and green crawfish walk the ocean floors.
