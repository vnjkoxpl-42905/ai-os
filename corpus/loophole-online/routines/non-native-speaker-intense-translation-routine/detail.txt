Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

It often takes a fair amount of time for non-native English speakers to reach the Translation + CLIR time goal.

So we have many Routine progressions while you're still working on that Translation + CLIR time goal. In this one, we add MemAC to the mix.

Non-native speakers, especially those who currently live in China or South Korea, tend to excel on MemAC, as the education systems in these nations build exceptional memory skills. 

I'm adding MemAC earlier for you than I do for native English speakers because y'all tend to have a great capacity for it.

Don't despair if it's taking you longer to meet those Translation + CLIR time goals. That's expected; it's part of the plan. When you do meet those time goals (and you WILL), you are going to be unstoppable with accuracy. 

You should be finding fewer words in your Vocabulary Translation Drills these days, and your Vocab Training should be getting simpler.

In addition, Caffeinated RC Translation will not take nearly as long as Cappuccino so you'll have more time for MemAC. But if you haven't met the Cappuccino time goals on the previous Routine, feel free switch out the Caffeinated RC Translation for Cappuccino. There's no pressure to only do Caffeinated here.

How This Should Be Done

The same Translation time goals advice I shared in the Non-Native Speaker Intermediate Translation Routine about meeting the Translation time goals applies here. 

You will likely need to pair this Routine with Translation Marathons once or twice per week to bring your Translation + CLIR time down to the time goal.

Just to review in case you missed it anywhere else, here are the Translation + CLIR Goals:

The time goal for Translation + CLIR Drills is 20 minutes.

The accommodated time goals for Translation + CLIR Drills are 25 minutes for 53-minute accommodations and 30 minutes for 70-minute accommodations.

Meeting this Translation + CLIR Drill time goal is only valid under two conditions:

You have a CLIR for every stimulus.

You have translated and quipped every stimulus in the section out loud.

MemAC takes a fair number of hours to master, but it is something you can cram and get better really fast. 

If you're willing to spend 6 hours on MemAC (and man, it is only my non-native speaker students who ever will do this! ❤️), you could take half of it out in one single day. You just have to be strong and get through it.

How to Move On

When you hit the Translation + CLIR time goal, proceed to the Non-Native Speaker Accuracy Routine.

Congratulations on beating Translation as a non-native English speaker. You're an absolute champion. I admire you.

If you haven't beaten MemAC by the time you hit the Translation + CLIR time goal, you can continue your same MemAC practice schedule in the Non-Native Speaker Accuracy Routine, even though that Routine is going to say to move MemAC to a 5 question warmup.
