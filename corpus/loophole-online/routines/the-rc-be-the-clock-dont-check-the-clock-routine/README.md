### Step 1: Time Awareness Training

1

#### Time Awareness Training

##### Instructions

**NOTE**: Checking the time excessively during the exam is a major cause of accuracy issues. This Workout is designed to help you overcome the need to check the time by enhancing your intuitive understanding of how much time has passed.

1. Look at the clock and note the exact time with seconds.

2. Aim to look at the clock again exactly when your Time Goal has elapsed.

   1. Five minutes is the Level 1 Time Goal, so start here on your first Time Awareness Training.

   2. If you've done Time Awareness Training before, start at the last Time Goal you've earned.
3. When you think your Time Goal has elapsed, look at the clock.

   1. You may only look at the clock once per Time Awareness Training.
4. If you look at the clock within one minute of your Time Goal, progress your Time Goal to the next Level tomorrow.

   1. To progress to the next level of Time Awareness Training, you must check the clock within only one minute of your Time Goal.

   2. If you've checked the time within one minute of the Level 5 Time Goal, congratulations! You've graduated from Time Awareness Training.
5. If you look at the clock farther than one minute from your Time Goal, this round of Time Awareness Training is now over.

   1. Please restart Time Awareness Training with the same Time Goal tomorrow.

### Step 2: Cappuccino RC Translation Drill (35 Version)

2

#### Cappuccino RC Translation Drill (35 Version)

1 passage

##### Instructions

1. Read, translate, and quip every sentence* . Max every two sentences if they are really simple. Exactly how you would translate in LR.

2. At the end of each paragraph, pause to review the Translation while looking at the paragraph and confirm with yourself that you really know it.

   1. **In this step, you are not rereading from scratch.**

   2. You're confirming the Translation you already have is right.

   3. This style of confirming your Translation with the text applies to every confirmation you ever do on RC.
3. After each paragraph, relook at all the paragraphs that you've read so far to confirm they are placed in your memory.

4. Continue on until the end of the passage with the same process.

5. At the end of the passage, do a final review by relooking at the entire passage and reconfirming your Translation. This should take about 30 seconds.

6. Complete this same process on all remaining passages in the section.

### Step 3: Pacing Practice

3

#### Pacing Practice

##### Instructions

1. Choose an interval time goal.

   1. Your interval time goal is your target time for each question.

   2. For example, an interval time goal may be 90 seconds per question.

   3. Follow the procedures of How to Set a Realistic Interval Time Goal below.
2. Set an interval timer to repeatedly countdown from that time goal 30 times in a row.

   1. Do not incorporate rest intervals.

   2. We choose 30 times so we can have some extra intervals in case our timer messes up.
3. Start your interval timer when you begin an LR or RC section* .

   1. For RC, only use the interval timer on the questions, not during the passage.
4. You must select an answer on each question before the interval timer goes off.

   1. You cannot stay on a question longer than the interval timer permits, no matter how badly you want to.

### Ellen's Advice

#### Why Are We Doing This?

This Routine eases you in to timing with minimal traumatic events.

By doing this Routine *before* jumping into Test-Day Timed Sections, you enable yourself to more effectively transition your goal accuracy into a timed environment.

By studying and understanding timing on a deeper level, you train yourself to be fast as opposed to just expecting yourself to be fast.

#### How This Should Be Done

Gradually build up your Time Awareness Training as you progress through this Routine.

You have the option of overlapping your Time Awareness Training with other tasks as noted in the Time Awareness Training instructions, but make sure to go directly into the Cappuccino warmup and RC Pacing Practice as soon as the Time Awareness Training is completed.

This will enable you to more effectively and intuitively feel the time during your RC Pacing Practice.

#### How to Move On

You can move on from this Routine when you no longer feel the urge to check the clock to know how much time has passed.

I want you to trust yourself and your own internal clock so that you don't distract yourself on test day.

#### Ellen's Advice

##### If You Want to Win

- Pacing Practice trains you to pace yourself.

  - It is an ideal workout if you find yourself over-investing in a few questions throughout the section and, in doing so, causing yourself to run out of time.
- You can use Pacing Practice within a test-day timed section just to ensure that you're moving on when you should, or you can use Pacing Practice on stopwatch-timed sections to gradually introduce timing in a controlled environment.

  - It's a versatile workout with many applications, so enjoy adapting it to your needs.

##### Timing Tips

***To Set a Realistic Interval Time Goal***

- Start with your natural pace and divide that natural pace by the number of questions in a section. This result is your interval time per question.

  - Your natural pace is how long it takes you to complete a section at goal accuracy without worrying about the time.

  - For instance, if your natural pace is 50 minutes per section and you divide that by the number of questions in a given LR section (~25 questions), you would have an interval time goal of 2 minutes per question.
- If your natural pace time is above your test-day time, gradually lower your interval time by 3-10 seconds after every Pacing Practice that meets your accuracy goal

  - Keep lowering the interval time until your interval time goes below your test-day timing.

### Timing Failsafe Routine

#### Why are we doing this?

Timing Failsafe Routines are one of the only ways to effectively reduce time without Marathoning.

The upsides of Timing Failsafes are:

- They can be done in shorter periods of time than a Marathon

- They allow for more systematic gradual time reduction

- Review can be more targeted and frequent

This Routine works great for students who thrive on structure.

#### How to Pull This Off

Failsafe Routines work best when combined with other Routines to bring time down while maintaining accuracy.

Essentially, you have to address the reasons why the Failsafe breaks in order to get it to progress, so you'll want to consider alternating Failsafe Routines with the Accuracy Fix Routines that pertain to your mistakes.

#### How to move on

Timing Failsafe Routines can get you to test-day goal time and accuracy without progressing to any other Speed Routine.

However, if you have lost every Timing Failsafe you've attempted for three consecutive prep days, here are a few options for what you can try next:

1. Set a higher baseline natural pace to start your Timing Failsafe

2. Replace the Timing Failsafe in this Routine with a Light Failsafe

3. Replace the Timing Failsafe in this Routine with Pacing Practice

4. Return to the Accuracy Fix Routines

If you sometimes hit goal time and accuracy in your Timing Failsafes, progress to any of the following Speed Routines: Failsafe Marathon Routine, Mini-Marathon Routine, or Marathon Routine.

If you're hitting your goal time and accuracy in the majority of your sections, take a Practice Test (PT).

You can move to any of the other Speed Routines, if your Timing Failsafe Routine went well. You do not have to go in order.

## Stop Mental and Physical Self-Sabotage

### What problem are we solving?

Have you ever done a section when:

- you're falling asleep in the middle?

- someone else is taking a call a little too close to you?

- you're saying to yourself, "I can't get this wrong. Oh my god, I don't understand. I have to get it. I'll never get into Berkeley. My dad is right about me."?

- you just saw a previous score and you're going to cry?

- you have a stye on your left eye and also maybe COVID?

Doing the LSAT like this is mental and physical self-sabotage.

You are willfully engaging in behaviors that decrease your score.

I care about you and I care about your score, so I'm going to give you some real talk here.

Telling yourself you can't do something drastically increases the chances you won't be able to do that thing.

So stop it.

Doing LSAT in an environment or situation that pulls on your attention sets you up to fail.

So stop it.

Thinking about the past, the future, and what other people think while you're doing the LSAT ensures that you do not do your best.

So stop it.

This is your Olympics.

This Accuracy Fix teaches you how to be that your advocate.

Instructions

Choose an interval time goal.

Your interval time goal is your target time for each question.

For example, an interval time goal may be 90 seconds per question.

Follow the procedures of How to Set a Realistic Interval Time Goal below.

Set an interval timer to repeatedly countdown from that time goal 30 times in a row.

Do not incorporate rest intervals.

We choose 30 times so we can have some extra intervals in case our timer messes up.

Start your interval timer when you begin an LR or RC section*.

For RC, only use the interval timer on the questions, not during the passage.

You must select an answer on each question before the interval timer goes off.

You cannot stay on a question longer than the interval timer permits, no matter how badly you want to.
SEE FULL WORKOUT DETAILS
Ellen&#x27;s Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

This Routine eases you in to timing with minimal traumatic events. 

By doing this Routine before jumping into Test-Day Timed Sections, you enable yourself to more effectively transition your goal accuracy into a timed environment.

By studying and understanding timing on a deeper level, you train yourself to be fast as opposed to just expecting yourself to be fast.

How This Should Be Done

Gradually build up your Time Awareness Training as you progress through this Routine.

You have the option of overlapping your Time Awareness Training with other tasks as noted in the Time Awareness Training instructions, but make sure to go directly into the Cappuccino warmup and RC Pacing Practice as soon as the Time Awareness Training is completed.

This will enable you to more effectively and intuitively feel the time during your RC Pacing Practice.

How to Move On

You can move on from this Routine when you no longer feel the urge to check the clock to know how much time has passed.

I want you to trust yourself and your own internal clock so that you don&#x27;t distract yourself on test day.
