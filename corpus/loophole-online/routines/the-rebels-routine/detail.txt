Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

This Routine is a safe place to get your rebellious instincts out.

It shows you how good you are and also how the process affects your sections. For the right test taker, it can be a great way to get night-before-the-test jitters out.

Recently, one of my tutoring students did this Routine the night before his test and he got a 177 the next day.

Correlation or causation? You be the judge 😂

Often, the scores on DARE Sections and Anti-PTs don't go down nearly as much as the student thinks they will.

Other times, the scores will reduce significantly and it reminds students why they do the process in the first place. No matter what, it's surprisingly comforting.

How This Should Be Done

This is the Routine where you purposefully break all of my rules.

Enjoy living free. I don't want to give you too much advice on how this should be done because the whole point is to screw around. 

But on the DARE Section, make sure that you feel the pain of not being sure, but having to choose an early answer anyway.

This an exaggerated experience to show you how risky it is to choose an answer without reading them all.

How to Move On

This Routine is something you'll only do one or two times maximum.

There's no real need to move on from it since it's kind of a one-off anyway.
