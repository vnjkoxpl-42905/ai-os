Ellen's Advice

Why Are We Doing This?

We pair the depth of RC HIIT with the intuitive movement of the Reverse Shuffle to balance this advanced RC skill workout.

This Routine challenges your speed and agility across all your RC skills. 

Keeping the passage in your working memory while independently pivoting into a new question type every 30 seconds (or less) is no easy task.

Successfully completing this Routine means you're ready for anything, no passage phases you, difficulty doesn't bother you, and you have test day locked down.

How This Should Be Done

Go straight from the RC HIIT into the Reverse Shuffle.

It should feel kinda like a sauna + cold plunge (not that I've ever done that, but I imagine that's what this would feel like).

In the RC HIIT, you'll be burning hot, pulling every insight you can out of the passage. Then in the Reverse Shuffle you have to fly free, trusting your memory to guide you without overthinking. It's a really cool feeling.

Don't overdo this Routine; don't perform it more than once every other day at maximum.

How to Move On

You don't really need to move on from this Routine since it can be useful as an intermittent check-in all the way up through test day.
