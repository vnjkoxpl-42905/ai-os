### Step 1: Caffeinated RC Translation Drill

1

#### Caffeinated RC Translation Drill

1 passage

##### Instructions

1. Read, translate, and quip paragraph 1* . Do it just like LR.

2. Take a few seconds to confirm your Translation with the paragraph you just read.

   1. **In this step, you are not rereading from scratch.**

   2. You're confirming the Translation you already have is right.

   3. This style of confirming your Translation with the text applies to every confirmation you ever do on RC.
3. Repeat this pattern for all remaining paragraphs.

4. At the end of the passage, confirm your Translation with the entire passage. This should take 15-30 seconds.

5. **OPTIONAL**: Look away and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of the entire passage silently.

   1. This step is optional, but you should do it when you first begin Caffeinated RC Translation Drills and periodically afterward, just to confirm that you can.
6. Complete this same process on all remaining passages in the section.

### Step 2: RC HIIT (High Intensity Interval Training)

2

#### RC HIIT (High Intensity Interval Training)

##### Instructions

1. Translate the passage*

2. **RC HIIT Phase 1: Complete the following RC Thunderdome outputs in under 30 seconds each before beginning the questions.** These outputs may require looking at the passage.

   1. Outline the structure of the passage overall.

      1. You can use paragraphs as an organizing structure, but you can also delineate on a more fine-grained level, if desired.
   2. Parallel one piece of the passage.

      1. Pick an example, one line of reasoning, or a given person's point of view.
   3. Sub out one word in the passage and replace it with a new word/phrase that means the same thing.

   4. Pick one sentence out of the passage and identify its function.

   5. What is the Main Point?
3. **RC HIIT Phase 2:** **Complete the following RC Thunderdome outputs in under 30 seconds each. You have two options in how you complete the Phrase 2 outputs: You may complete all the additional RC Thunderdome outputs before beginning the questions or complete two to three additional RC Thunderdome outputs in between each of the questions.** These outputs do not require you to look at the passage.

   1. What is the tone of the passage?

   2. Who is the author?

   3. Who is the audience?

   4. Who are the players in the passage and what do they believe?

   5. Strengthen the author's opinion

      1. If the passage feels neutral, write a confirming piece of evidence for the information the author is presenting.
   6. Weaken the author's opinion

      1. If the passage feels neutral, write a disconfirming piece of evidence for the information the author is presenting.
   7. One Inference from the passage

   8. One thing the author is likely to believe

   9. Identify a principle underlying the passage as a whole or an argument within the passage

### Ellen's Advice

#### Why Are We Doing This?

We pair the depth of RC HIIT with the intuitive movement of the Reverse Shuffle to balance this advanced RC skill workout.

This Routine challenges your speed and agility across all your RC skills.

Keeping the passage in your working memory while independently pivoting into a new question type every 30 seconds (or less) is no easy task.

Successfully completing this Routine means you're ready for anything, no passage phases you, difficulty doesn't bother you, and you have test day locked down.

#### How This Should Be Done

Go straight from the RC HIIT into the Reverse Shuffle.

It should feel kinda like a sauna + cold plunge (not that I've ever done that, but I imagine that's what this would feel like).

In the RC HIIT, you'll be burning hot, pulling every insight you can out of the passage. Then in the Reverse Shuffle you have to fly free, trusting your memory to guide you without overthinking. It's a really cool feeling.

Don't overdo this Routine; don't perform it more than once every other day at maximum.

#### How to Move On

You don't really need to move on from this Routine since it can be useful as an intermittent check-in all the way up through test day.

### Strengthen Your Working Memory

#### What problem are we solving?

You know that feeling when your memory just goes blank in the middle of a question? And you kinda have no idea what you're supposed to do?

Yeah, that's not supposed to happen.

That's something to train out of you.

And this whole LSAT thing is going to feel so much better when you have the evidence you need to make the correct decisions in the moment.

I never knew memory was such privilege and advantage.

I used to think, "Of course *everyone* is **subconsciously and instantaneously interrelating every answer choice with the compete conceptual idea behind the stimulus.** That's the only way *anyone* would ever make a decision about an answer choice, right?"

WRONG.

This is the blindspot you have when you "just know" the answer to questions.

Choosing answers the way I just described feels so good that you can't imagine anyone would ever want to do it a different way.

But if you don't remember the complete conceptual idea behind the stimulus/passage while you're in the answer choices, you don't have the option of choosing answers this way.

That's the problem what we're here to solve.

This Accuracy Fix will train your memory to be able to enact this (much more fun and accurate) process in the answer choices.

Yes, the training is strenuous, but all good training is. And on the other side, you won't be able to believe you really chose answers without keeping the whole Translation in your working memory.

#### Instructions

1. Translate the passage*

2. **RC HIIT Phase 1: Complete the following RC Thunderdome outputs in under 30 seconds each before beginning the questions.** These outputs may require looking at the passage.

   1. Outline the structure of the passage overall.

      1. You can use paragraphs as an organizing structure, but you can also delineate on a more fine-grained level, if desired.
   2. Parallel one piece of the passage.

      1. Pick an example, one line of reasoning, or a given person's point of view.
   3. Sub out one word in the passage and replace it with a new word/phrase that means the same thing.

   4. Pick one sentence out of the passage and identify its function.

   5. What is the Main Point?
3. **RC HIIT Phase 2:** **Complete the following RC Thunderdome outputs in under 30 seconds each. You have two options in how you complete the Phrase 2 outputs: You may complete all the additional RC Thunderdome outputs before beginning the questions or complete two to three additional RC Thunderdome outputs in between each of the questions.** These outputs do not require you to look at the passage.

   1. What is the tone of the passage?

   2. Who is the author?

   3. Who is the audience?

   4. Who are the players in the passage and what do they believe?

   5. Strengthen the author's opinion

      1. If the passage feels neutral, write a confirming piece of evidence for the information the author is presenting.
   6. Weaken the author's opinion

      1. If the passage feels neutral, write a disconfirming piece of evidence for the information the author is presenting.
   7. One Inference from the passage

   8. One thing the author is likely to believe

   9. Identify a principle underlying the passage as a whole or an argument within the passage

#### Ellen's Advice

##### If You Want to Win

- **It is not important to look at the questions and answer choices during an RC HIIT.**

  - The point of this workout is not to answer questions, but if you want to combine it with answer-choosing, I'm not fully against it.
- Don't attempt to HIIT as a beginner.

  - If you haven't met the Translation time goal or don't have significant experience with full LR sections, wait to HIIT until you need it.
- For the right kind of plateaued, advanced test taker, HIIT offer a singular opportunity for a perspective shift.

- HIITs show you where you're weak.

  - If you find that you always struggle with one of the RC HIIT outputs, review the videos and written material on that topic.
- HIITs are great for when you start to run out of unseen PTs near the end of your prep.

  - HIITs can still be done on previously seen material.
- If you are an advanced student PTing at 172+, HIITing can really take your skills to the next level.

- Like Translation + CLIR Drills, HIITs are a great spot to use AI voice dictation to speed up the workout. My favorite dictation software is Flow ; it's free up to 2000 words per week.

### Don't Be Desperate in the Answers

#### What problem are we solving?

Sometimes you just want to get the question right so badly that every answer choice looks like an escape hatch.

"Is it right? Is it right!??

If it's right, then I can move on.

Then I won't waste any more time.

Then the stimulus must have meant this.

Then this must have been what they were looking for."

Notice the desperation dripping off this monologue.

You're looking for an answer choice to rescue you, but actually the roles need to be reversed. You have to be the strong one.

Be for real. You are better than 80% wrong.

Answer choices are designed to exploit your desire for them to be right and to prey on moments of inattention.

But you can channel your desire to get the question right into building an invulnerable fortress of analysis throughout every stage of the question. This fortress (Translation, CLIR, Mission, Back Up Plan) will allow you to see exactly why these answers are wrong without. any. drama.

The answer choices only gain manipulative power over you when you look to them to save you. But you are not the freaking damsel that answer C is here to rescue. You're the hero.

You're here to judge answer C, and you're going to judge it according to your vetted, bulletproof standards.

The answers need to impress *you*, not the other way around.

This Accuracy Fix trains you to build the fortress.

Because you are smart enough. You have the tools, the time, and the resources to never need any answer choice to save you again.

Sometimes you just want to get the question right so badly that every answer choice looks like an escape hatch.

&quot;Is it right? Is it right!??

If it&#x27;s right, then I can move on.

Then I won&#x27;t waste any more time.

Then the stimulus must have meant this.

Then this must have been what they were looking for.&quot;

Notice the desperation dripping off this monologue. 

You&#x27;re looking for an answer choice to rescue you, but actually the roles need to be reversed. You have to be the strong one.

Be for real. You are better than 80% wrong. 

Answer choices are designed to exploit your desire for them to be right and to prey on moments of inattention.

But you can channel your desire to get the question right into building an invulnerable fortress of analysis throughout every stage of the question. This fortress (Translation, CLIR, Mission, Back Up Plan) will allow you to see exactly why these answers are wrong without. any. drama.

The answer choices only gain manipulative power over you when you look to them to save you. But you are not the freaking damsel that answer C is here to rescue. You&#x27;re the hero.

You&#x27;re here to judge answer C, and you&#x27;re going to judge it according to your vetted, bulletproof standards.

The answers need to impress you, not the other way around.

This Accuracy Fix trains you to build the fortress.

Because you are smart enough. You have the tools, the time, and the resources to never need any answer choice to save you again.

### Disrupt Your Toxic Habits

#### What problem are we solving?

It's so easy to attribute a score you don't like to anything but your toxic habits. To say, "This one was just hard" or "I always have trouble with that question type." Ellen&#x27;s Advice
If You Want to Win

The Reverse Shuffle&#x27;s bark is a lot worse than its bite. Don&#x27;t be so intimidated by this workout that you don&#x27;t even try it. 

The Reverse Shuffle is a great way to prove to yourself that you don&#x27;t actually have to look back to the passage as much as you think you do.

For the most part, you look back at the passage to ease anxiety, not because it actually increases the number of correct answers per section. 

The Reverse Shuffle combines the best of Party Tricking with the best of Translation.

You know your Translation has to be really good because of the gap between translating the passage and actually answering its questions, so it automatically holds you to a higher standard. 

The steps between translating the passage and actually answering its questions produce a beneficial blur over the content of the passage.

This beneficial blur mimics the feeling of Party Tricking – it forces you to answer the questions conceptually, rather than relying on keywording to choose your answer.

This is why the Reverse Shuffle often yields an increase in accuracy. 

Just like the E Blackout, I&#x27;m sure you won&#x27;t believe that the Reverse Shuffle was actually used on a real LSAT to produce a 180. But it did, and I have the receipts ✅.

Why Are We Doing This?

We pair the depth of RC HIIT with the intuitive movement of the Reverse Shuffle to balance this advanced RC skill workout.

This Routine challenges your speed and agility across all your RC skills. 

Keeping the passage in your working memory while independently pivoting into a new question type every 30 seconds (or less) is no easy task.

Successfully completing this Routine means you&#x27;re ready for anything, no passage phases you, difficulty doesn&#x27;t bother you, and you have test day locked down.

How This Should Be Done

Go straight from the RC HIIT into the Reverse Shuffle.

It should feel kinda like a sauna + cold plunge (not that I&#x27;ve ever done that, but I imagine that&#x27;s what this would feel like).

In the RC HIIT, you&#x27;ll be burning hot, pulling every insight you can out of the passage. Then in the Reverse Shuffle you have to fly free, trusting your memory to guide you without overthinking. It&#x27;s a really cool feeling.

Don&#x27;t overdo this Routine; don&#x27;t perform it more than once every other day at maximum.

How to Move On

You don&#x27;t really need to move on from this Routine since it can be useful as an intermittent check-in all the way up through test day.
