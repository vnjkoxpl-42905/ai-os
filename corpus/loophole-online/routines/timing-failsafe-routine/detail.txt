Instructions

After you've Camoed your LR or RC section, begin the Wrong Answer Journaling process.

For each wrong answer, fill in the date you did the section, the question number that was wrong, and the answer you chose.

This information will already be filled in for you if you Camo using Loophole Online.

Fill in your Reason Missed column

In this column, write down what you did wrong that caused you to miss this question.

Ask yourself, "Why did I think that this wrong answer was right? Why did I think that this right answer was wrong?" Focus on changing the actions that enabled these mistaken judgments.

The first word of every entry in this column should be "I." 

Example: "I misidentified the question stem."

This will force you to reflect on what you did that caused you to choose the wrong answer and overlook the right answer.

Fill in your Solution column

In this column, write down what you will do differently to ensure you don't make this same mistake again.

Ask yourself, "What could I have done that would have made me realize in the moment that the wrong answer is wrong? What could I have done that would have made me realize in the moment that the right answer is right?"

The first words of every entry in this column should be "I will..." 

Example: "I will do Question Stem Training to improve my ability to correctly identify question types."

Frame your Solution Column as a direct response to the Reason Missed column.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

Timing Failsafe Routines are one of the only ways to effectively reduce time without Marathoning.

The upsides of Timing Failsafes are:

They can be done in shorter periods of time than a Marathon

They allow for more systematic gradual time reduction

Review can be more targeted and frequent

This Routine works great for students who thrive on structure.

How This Should Be Done

Failsafe Routines work best when combined with other Routines to bring time down while maintaining accuracy.

Essentially, you have to address the reasons why the Failsafe breaks in order to get it to progress, so you'll want to consider alternating Failsafe Routines with the Accuracy Fix Routines that pertain to your mistakes.

How to Move On

Timing Failsafe Routines can get you to test-day goal time and accuracy without progressing to any other Speed Routine.

However, if you have lost every Timing Failsafe you've attempted for three consecutive prep days, here are a few options for what you can try next:

Set a higher baseline natural pace to start your Timing Failsafe

Replace the Timing Failsafe in this Routine with a Light Failsafe

Replace the Timing Failsafe in this Routine with Pacing Practice

Return to the Accuracy Fix Routines

If you sometimes hit goal time and accuracy in your Timing Failsafes, progress to any of the following Speed Routines: Failsafe Marathon Routine, Mini-Marathon Routine, or Marathon Routine.

If you're hitting your goal time and accuracy in the majority of your sections, take a Practice Test (PT).

You can move to any of the other Speed Routines, if your Timing Failsafe Routine went well. You do not have to go in order.
