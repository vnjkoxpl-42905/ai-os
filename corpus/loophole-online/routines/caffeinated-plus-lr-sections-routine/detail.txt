Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

Hitting the Caffeinated time goal with high retention make the RC question so much easier within time.

RC section will feel feel very different after you meet this time goal.

How This Should Be Done

Caffeinated RC Translation is the endgame RC Translation Drill.

This is what you'll actually do in a 35-minute section, unless you really like Cappuccino RC Translation (35 Version), so we need to ensure we can complete Caffeinated RC Translation at a high quality standard within 4 minutes on average per passage.

In pursuing this 4-minute average time goal, try to not allow passages to deviate from this average time to a large extent.

It's undesirable to have 2-minute passages and equally undesirably to have 6-minute passages, even though these two numbers would average out to 4 minutes.

In general, your Caffeinated RC Translation timing range should be between 3-5 minutes per passage, but limiting the number of passages that get close to 5 minutes.

If you would like additional help meeting this time goal, incorporate a Caffeinated RC Translation Drill Failsafe into your Routine.

At this point in the RC Translation journey, you'll have to learn to trust your memory far more since there are fewer opportunities for recaps.

But you've earned this trust in your memory at this point through all your hard work with Decaf and Cappuccino. In those drills, we verified your memory through the paragraph-level and full-passage recaps.

Now we can trust that you know the passage when you translate it, but we will continue to check in through periodic full-passage recaps just to make sure our standard isn't slipping.
