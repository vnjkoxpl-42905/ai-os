Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

Cappuccino RC Translation brings a lighter, more time-efficient RC Translation process that can be used within more test-day timing scenarios.

This Routine ensures that there is no drop in passage retention when we move from Decaf to Cappuccino.

How This Should Be Done

Cappuccino is a much faster drill than Decaf, so you'll have more time to devote to Mini-Cycles and Acronyms in this Routine.

To meet the Cappuccino time goal, repeat at least three sections of Cappuccino RC Translation in a row in this Routine. Do not complete the questions.

To maximize your progress through Cappuccino RC Translation, hold to the recap standard that you built in Decaf RC Translation. If you lower your standard of comprehension and retention, our work is going to waste.

Yes, it's difficult to continue to push yourself to even more ambitious goals with your memory, but it's worth it when you're able to answer the questions in an RC section confidently from memory, without looking back at the passage.

If you feel confident in your Translation skills, feel free to skip straight to the 35-minute version of Cappuccino RC Translation without doing the regular version first.

However, if you feel your standard of comprehension and retention falter in the 35-minute version, return the regular version of Cappuccino RC Translation. Meet the time goal there and then return to the 35-minute version of the drill afterward.

Whenever I say to "alternate" workouts in a Routine, switch which Workout you do each day. For example, if the Routine says, "Alternate: X // Y (1:1)," that means that I should do X on day 1 and then switch to doing Y the next time I do the Routine. The (1:1) numbers signal how many times you should do each Workout before switching to other Workout.
