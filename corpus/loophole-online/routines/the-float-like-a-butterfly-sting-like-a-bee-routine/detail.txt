Instructions

NOTE: Do not attempt HIIT if you have never done a Thunderdome. Ideally, you've done several Thunderdomes and begun to feel confident while Thunderdoming before ever attempting HIIT.

After you finish your Translation + CLIR on a given stimulus at 20-minute pace, complete the stimulus' Thunderdome without looking at the stimulus again*.

Cap each Thunderdome output at 30 seconds outloud.

Use AI voice dictation to record your Thunderdome outputs while keeping the workout speedy. My favorite dictation software is Flow; it's free up to 2000 words per week.

If you go over 30 seconds per Thunderdome output, don't worry about it. Just aiming for 30 seconds will make you faster.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

This Routine challenges your speed and agility across the entire skill set of LR. 

Keeping the stimulus in your working memory while independently pivoting into a new question type every 30 seconds (or less) is no easy task.

Successfully completing this Routine means you're ready for anything, no question type phases you, difficulty doesn't bother you, and you have critical intuition locked down.

How This Should Be Done

We keep the warmup light to give HIIT room to breathe.

If you've done this Routine at least once before, you can experiment with adding the answer choices on each question after you're done with all your HIIT outputs.

Answering the questions should be relatively simple if you've mastered the HIIT outputs. 

Don't overdo this Routine; don't perform it more than once every other day at maximum.

How to Move On

You don't really need to move on from this Routine since it can be useful as an intermittent check-in all the way up through test day.
