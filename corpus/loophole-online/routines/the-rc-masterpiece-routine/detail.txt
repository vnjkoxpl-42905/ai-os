Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

There's a real-recognize-real quality to some students and the RC Masterpiece. Sometimes you just know that the RC Masterpiece's impulse control techniques were made for you (because they were ❤️‍🩹).

If nothing seems to improve your score, this Routine is for you.

How This Should Be Done

Since the RC Masterpiece takes different amounts of time on different days, you can contract or extend the Extracurricular Reading and Vocab Training time to fill your homework block.

For example, if you're on the third day of your RC Masterpiece and you don't actually have that many questions to complete, use that time as an opportunity to dive more deeply into your Extracurricular Reading and Vocab Training.

You can also layer this Routine with second RC Masterpiece section on a staggered start day. This means you would begin another RC Masterpiece section on day 2 or 3 of another section's RC Masterpiece. This would allow you to get more RC Masterpiece sections done throughout the week and potentially see a lot more improvement.

How to Move On

Move on from this Routine when your accuracy improves.

RC Masterpieces are great as temporary but intense Routines, but you don't need to stay on RC Masterpieces any longer than is necessary to move the accuracy.
