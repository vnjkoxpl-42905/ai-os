Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

Density Buckets and the Method Vocab Remix hold you accountable to using the complex language of LR in context.

If you notice that your internal monologue is vague and you tend to make remarks like "all these answers sound the same," this is the Routine for you.

This Routine gives you the verbal tools to raise your score, and gives you the chance to put those tools into action.

Make sure you know the Uncut Method Vocab List before you take on this Routine. The Method Vocab Remix will get you to start using this vocab in your own internal monologue. Once it's in your brain, you'll be able to master LR on a whole new level.

How This Should Be Done

Take your time with the Method Vocab Remix to really make your answer choices sound like actual LSAT answer choices. It's not worth rushing it and not really learning how to use the words. 

Don't be afraid to throw your Mission in the Low Density Column during your Density Buckets. It's so much better to be for real with yourself rather than pretending something is high density information when you know it's not. This is how you'll improve.

How to Move On

When two Missions or fewer are in the Low Density column on your Density Buckets and you've completed the Method Vocab Remix, you're safe to move on from this Routine. You can progress to any other Accuracy Fix Routine.
