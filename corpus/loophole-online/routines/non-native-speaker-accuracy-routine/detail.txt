Instructions

After you've Camoed your LR or RC section, begin the Wrong Answer Journaling process.

For each wrong answer, fill in the date you did the section, the question number that was wrong, and the answer you chose.

This information will already be filled in for you if you Camo using Loophole Online.

Fill in your Reason Missed column

In this column, write down what you did wrong that caused you to miss this question.

Ask yourself, "Why did I think that this wrong answer was right? Why did I think that this right answer was wrong?" Focus on changing the actions that enabled these mistaken judgments.

The first word of every entry in this column should be "I." 

Example: "I misidentified the question stem."

This will force you to reflect on what you did that caused you to choose the wrong answer and overlook the right answer.

Fill in your Solution column

In this column, write down what you will do differently to ensure you don't make this same mistake again.

Ask yourself, "What could I have done that would have made me realize in the moment that the wrong answer is wrong? What could I have done that would have made me realize in the moment that the right answer is right?"

The first words of every entry in this column should be "I will..." 

Example: "I will do Question Stem Training to improve my ability to correctly identify question types."

Frame your Solution Column as a direct response to the Reason Missed column.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

We've finally beaten Translation, which means we're finally in the Accuracy Prep Stage. 

This is where you shine because we've taken care of a lot of the English trouble, and now your analytical skills get to take full center stage.

Any Accuracy Fix or Speed Routine is now open to you, even though I have one more custom non-native speaker Routine after this one.

Now that you've gone through the intense non-native speaker Translation Process, your skills are going to be much more similar to a native speaker with the uplifted analytical ability that you bring to the table as a non-native speaker. This is often a huge win in the Accuracy Prep Stage.

Make sure to keep your fundamentals strong as you move into answering questions. It will feel different now that you have a much stronger understanding of what all the words mean. Keep up your Vocab Training to continue uplifting your English ability.

We move you into a Write In RC and Write In LR first to keep you grounded in the process and force you to make Missions.

It can feel tempting to jump into the questions excited and disorganized, but we need to make sure we fine-tune your analytical process just as much as you fine-tuned your English process.

How This Should Be Done

Try to stay focused throughout this entire Routine. Only take breaks after MemAC and before Vocab Training. 

You can omit the Cappuccino RC Translation section if you feel confident in your RC Translation skills and don't need the extra warmup.

Don't rush the Write In. They take as long as they take. We will work on speed later. Work on accuracy now.

How to Move On

Review your wrong answers and judge which Accuracy Fixes are relevant to your mistakes. Pursue those Workouts and Routines to address the mistakes you're seeing.

Camo Review and Wrong Answer Journaling will help you with this process. Also, consider making a Highlight Reel once per week. 

After you've hit goal time and accuracy using the Accuracy Fixes and Speed Routines, move on to the Non-Native Speaker Perform Routine.
