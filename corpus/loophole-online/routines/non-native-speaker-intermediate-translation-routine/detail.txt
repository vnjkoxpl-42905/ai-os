Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

Now you've been doing Vocabulary Translation Drills and Vocab Training long enough that we can switch our emphasis from Vocabulary Translation Drills to Translation + CLIR Drills in a focused attempt to meet our Translation + CLIR time goal silently. 

We're moving to Cappuccino in this Routine to bring down our RC Translation time, mimicking the same progression you would make in the Translation Core Routines.

We're adding a Proto-Stimulus Rebound to our Vocabulary Translation Drills in order to more deeply ingrain the memory of the stimulus in your subconscious.

Non-native speakers are typically good with these memory-powered workouts, so the Proto-Stimulus Rebound shouldn't be a time sink. If it is a massive challenge, that just proves that we really needed to do it. Keep it up, and you will improve. 

If any native English speakers are reading this:

Please do not take the silent accommodation I am making here for non-native speakers as a license to meet your Translation + CLIR time goals silently.

Non-native English speakers have a very different experience speaking English out loud, especially when they have never lived in English-speaking nations. The standard is different for a reason.

How This Should Be Done

You will likely need to pair this Routine with Translation Marathons once or twice per week to bring your Translation + CLIR time down to the time goal.

Just to review in case you missed it anywhere else, here are the Translation + CLIR Goals:

The time goal for Translation + CLIR Drills is 20 minutes.

The accommodated time goals for Translation + CLIR Drills are 25 minutes for 53-minute accommodations and 30 minutes for 70-minute accommodations.

Meeting this Translation + CLIR Drill time goal is only valid under two conditions:

You have a CLIR for every stimulus.

You have translated and quipped every stimulus in the section out loud.

How to Move On

Once you feel like the Proto-Stimulus Rebound on the Vocabulary Translation Drill is easy, move on to Non-Native Speaker Intense Translation Routine.
