Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

This Routine purposefully messes with the passage and the answer choices to get you to wake up and stop auto-piloting your way through them.

The Reverse Shuffle will hopefully show you how powerful your memory and intuition can be.

Since the E Blackout removes one of the answers, it forces you to trust your knowledge of the passage on a much deeper level. When you can't compare all the answers and settle, you have to really understand what makes an RC answer choice correct.

This Routine is built to break down every second-guessing, answer-choice-settling habit you have.

How This Should Be Done

Students are often desperate to break a plateau, but they aren't desperate to change their habits.

Take the process disruption in this Routine as an opportunity to get you out of any funks you've been in. This Routine is designed to make you re-examine the parts of the process you've taken for granted.

Don't be put off by the uncertainty inherent in the E Blackout or the Reverse Shuffle. The uncertainty is the point because it helps you grow your trust in yourself.

How to Move On

This is a good Routine to return to intermittently when your process starts to get sloppy.

There's no need to move on from this Routine permanently since it can be useful even in the week of your test to offer an answer choice tune-up. 

None of my tutoring students have ever gotten better than a -3 on an RC E Blackout. Hit me up on Tell Ellen if you beat them.
