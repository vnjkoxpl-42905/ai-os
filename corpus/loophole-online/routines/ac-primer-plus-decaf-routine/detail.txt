Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

The Answer Choice Primer arrives as soon as we hit the Translation + CLIR time goal to transfer our stimulus Translation skills into the answer choices.

The first half of the Primer is super tedious, so be ready. However, it's only tedious because it's stopping you from skipping over words in the answer choices.

The Primer is actually doing you a HUGE favor that you'll (hopefully) never recover from, even if it seems really annoying.

How This Should Be Done

Now that Translation + CLIR has moved to a warmup, lean into upping your Decaf RC Translation volume so that your RC Translation progression doesn't lag too far behind LR.

The more frequently you Decaf, the faster and easier it will become 🪄

Ideally, you are practicing 3 consecutive sections of Decaf RC Translation per day. If you don't practice Decaf every day or don't follow the Decaf instructions, it will stay difficult longer.

The Answer Choice Primer will take several days to complete.

It will feel very slow in the beginning, but make sure you're not trying to speed it up in the wrong ways. Write a real Translation in the Translation column, not just a near-copy/paste of the exact words of the answer choice.

The Written Answer Choice Translation Spreadsheet Drill is meant to slow you down and get you to acknowledge every word of every answer choice. That said, do not elongate filling in the spreadsheet longer than you have to.

How to Move On

First, complete 3 sections of the Written Answer Choice Translation Spreadsheet Drill.

After that, complete 3 sections of the Out Loud Answer Choice Translation Drill.

There is no time threshold on either drill; completing the sections is what moves you on.

Then move on to the Decaf + LR Translation Routine.
