Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

We're a little less scared of RC now, so we can move our Decaf to a warmup and let you choose what Translation style you want to use on your Light Inside Out. 

If you want to stick with Decaf, that's fine, but feel free to move to Cappuccino or Caffeinated if that's more comfortable for you. 

Light Inside Out is a little less work than Modified Inside Out, so you should still feel comfortable with RC without as much energy expenditure. It still spoils the passage, so there's no worries there. 

We add some Tone Training to get you used to feeling the author's emotions through the passage. That way you won't be as intimidated when you have to do Tone questions in a timed environment.

How This Should Be Done

This is another Routine where I really don't want you to rush.

This Routine might take a little less time than the Make RC Easy Routine, but slow and steady wins the race, especially if you're changing the RC Translation Drill away from Decaf.

Remember the point of the I'm Afraid of RC Routine Progression is to fix your RC mindset. If the Routine isn't helping your mindset, it's not doing its job.

How to Move On

If you feel less fear (but still a little bit of fear) toward RC, move on to the Learn to Love RC Routine, the next Routine in the I'm Afraid of RC Routine Progression.

If you don't feel any fear toward RC, move on to any of the I Just Want to Focus on RC Routine Progressions.
