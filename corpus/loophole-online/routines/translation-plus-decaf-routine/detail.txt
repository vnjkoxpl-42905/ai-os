Instructions

Highlight any word you wouldn't bet your LSAT score on during all LSAT sections and during all Extracurricular Reading.

These highlighted words are your vocab words.

Harvest all the vocab words you've highlighted after your section or reading is complete.

Harvesting your words means writing down each of your vocab words in a tracker. You can use my template or create a simple Google Sheet for this.

Always harvest your vocab words the same day that you highlight them.

To further optimize your vocab harvesting, watch the How to Harvest Vocab Words video.

Practice your vocab words daily for at least 10 minutes.

To practice, follow the procedures outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video or the How to Learn Vocab: AI Supercharged Edition video.

10 minutes of practice is a minimum, not a maximum. If you have a 100+ number of vocab words to learn, do not be afraid to practice for a longer period of time.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

Decaf is one of the most intense, difficult Workouts on the whole Prep Map. It's also magical.

By the end of Decaf, you can actually say the whole Translation of the passage out loud from memory. Doesn't that sound like a goal worth sweating for?

How This Should Be Done

Adding Decaf RC Translation to your Routine means you'll have less time for your Translation + CLIR Drills. This should be fine because your Translation + CLIR Drills are taking less time than they used to, so you should ideally be able to maintain the same Translation + CLIR Drill volume as you did in the Intro Translation Routine (Translation Routine 1).

Make sure to watch the Decaf video explanation and demo before trying Decaf on your own (all linked below). It really helps to see me do it before you try it yourself.

Your progression toward the Decaf RC Translation time and accuracy goals is primarily determined by two things: volume and following the instructions. Do at least 1 full section of Decaf RC Translation per day, if possible. Ideally, you would complete 3 consecutive sections of Decaf RC Translation per day.

If you don't practice Decaf every day or don't follow the Decaf instructions, it will stay difficult longer. To maximize your progress in this Routine, only do Decaf Translation Drills on whole sections at a time. Do not split up Decaf Translation Drills into individual passages or take breaks between passages. The number one way to improve quickly at Decaf RC Translation is volume, and if you only do one passage per day the volume will be too low to allow for progress.

Do not compromise the Translation and recap standard outlined in the Decaf RC Translation instructions. If you do not recap with all the details or do not quip, you are not completing Decaf RC Translation and should not progress to the next Routine.

How to Move On

Once you hit the Translation + CLIR time goal, ideally while maintaining accuracy of -5 or better against the CLIR Answer Key, you can move on to the AC Primer + Decaf Routine.

We move to the Answer Choice Primer next so we can bring your newfound Translation skills into the answer choices.

Translation + CLIR Goals

The time goal for Translation + CLIR Drills is 20 minutes.

The accommodated time goals for Translation + CLIR Drills are 25 minutes for 53-minute accommodations and 30 minutes for 70-minute accommodations.

Meeting this Translation + CLIR Drill time goal is only valid under two conditions:

You have a CLIR for every stimulus.

You have translated and quipped every stimulus in the section out loud.
