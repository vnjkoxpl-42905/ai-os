### Step 2: Party Tricking Jail

2

#### Party Tricking Jail

##### Instructions

**NOTE**: Party Tricking Jail is an advanced workout for those who want to push themselves to become true answer choice experts. Beginners can make good progress with other workouts before entering Party Tricking Jail.

1. Party Trick every question in an LR or RC section* .

   1. This means you will only read the question stem and answer choices on each question before you select an answer on that question.

   2. Do not look at the stimulus or passage.
2. Camo the section immediately after completing it.

   1. The Camo will display all the questions that are wrong plus an unknown number of decoy correct answers.

   2. Do not look at the stimulus when you Camo.

   3. Party Trick the returned questions again. Doubt your answers on the questions Camo displays and try to correct your answer.
3. Get as close to -0 on the Camo as you can.

4. **OPTIONAL**: Do not exit Party Tricking Jail until you reach -5 or better pre-Camo.

   1. You can customize this accuracy goal to your personal needs.

### Step 3: Camo Review

3

#### Camo Review

##### Instructions

1. When you finish an LR or RC section in LawHub, do not press Complete Section. Instead, write down your answers.

   1. Use Self-Paced Mode on all LR and RC sections. Never use Exam Mode.
2. At least 24 hours later, enter your answers into Camo Review.

   1. **Do not check your answers against the answer key before entering your answers into Camo Review.**
3. Camo Review will give you a list of questions to return to.

   1. This list includes all of your wrong answers and an unknown number of decoy correct answers.
4. Reattempt all of the questions in your Camo Review from scratch. Attempt to do a better job on the question now than you did the first time.

   1. Doing a better job means you should retranslate, requip, and re-CLIR the stimulus on LR or retranslate and requip the passage on RC. Retranslate every part of the question, consider every answer choice, and try to correct your process mistakes before choosing a new answer.
5. Enter all your new answers into Camo Review.

6. Camo Review will identify all your Misreads, Conceptual Gaps, Self-Doubt, and Self-Confidence answers.

7. Your Misreads, Conceptual Gaps, and Self-Doubt answers have been automatically added to your Wrong Answer Journal. Go to your Wrong Answer Journal now to immediately begin the Wrong Answer Journal process.

### Ellen's Advice

#### Why Are We Doing This?

We go to Party Tricking Jail when you've become good the LSAT, but not quite great at the LSAT.

I'll often assign Party Tricking Jail when my students hit a plateau of around -4 on either LR or RC. These students have tried everything, worked hard, but they aren't quite natural at the exam yet.

Often these students will be hardworking technicians who earnestly apply all the methodologies to the absolute best of their ability, but something just hasn't clicked. Party Tricking Jail is what makes the LSAT click (when you give it a real chance).

Yes, it's hard in the beginning. Yes, you have to show a little bit of resilience when you see low accuracy on the first few sections.

But on the other side, you'll have a hard time believing you ever did the LSAT without being able to Party Trick at goal level.

#### How This Should Be Done

Feel free to customize the Party Tricking Jail goal accuracy to suit your needs.

In general, you should set the Party Tricking Jail goal accuracy to allow for 3-4 more questions wrong than your test-day goal accuracy. For instance, if your goal is -5 on LR/RC on test day, you could set your Party Tricking goal accuracy at -8 or -9.

Take your Camo really seriously while you're in Party Tricking Jail.

The real challenge of Party Tricking Jail comes in Camo. Do not just stick with your original answer because you don't have any additional information. You do have additional information: You now know that the answer you chose is under suspicion because only a limited number of decoys will come up in Camo.

So if you have any doubt as to your answer, it's time to re-evaluate the answer choices with the added knowledge that you should be suspicious of your original Party Trick answer. You can improve a lot from revisiting the list of questions you've already Party Tricked before you've had the stimulus spoiled.

The goal of the Camo pass in Party Tricking Jail is always to get a -0.

If you're not changing your answers on enough questions to get a -0, you are not improving as much as you can from Party Tricking Jail.

#### How to Move On

Move on from Party Tricking Jail when you hit your accuracy goals.

If you do a section or two and have worse than 50% accuracy, that is not a reason to give up and move on from Party Tricking Jail.

It's completely expected that you will not have amazing accuracy the first time you try a full section of Party Tricking Jail. That's where the Workout starts.

Your RC Party Tricking accuracy will be better than your LR Party Tricking accuracy because RC is inherently easier to Party Trick (you gain clues from the other questions). This is not a sign that there is anything wrong with you or that you need to stop doing the Workout.

### Practice Occurs Under Full Test Conditions

#### What problem are we solving?

You should not take an official LSAT until you have practiced regularly in test conditions. This means you're practicing in a quiet environment, taking exactly the allocated time you'll have on test day (no more no less), and you are not taking breaks to go on your phone or talk to people. Practicing in chaotic, unplanned conditions causes more score chaos than you'd think.

#### Instructions

**NOTE**: Party Tricking Jail is an advanced workout for those who want to push themselves to become true answer choice experts. Beginners can make good progress with other workouts before entering Party Tricking Jail.

1. Party Trick every question in an LR or RC section* .

   1. This means you will only read the question stem and answer choices on each question before you select an answer on that question.

   2. Do not look at the stimulus or passage.
2. Camo the section immediately after completing it.

   1. The Camo will display all the questions that are wrong plus an unknown number of decoy correct answers.

   2. Do not look at the stimulus when you Camo.

   3. Party Trick the returned questions again. Doubt your answers on the questions Camo displays and try to correct your answer.
3. Get as close to -0 on the Camo as you can.

4. **OPTIONAL**: Do not exit Party Tricking Jail until you reach -5 or better pre-Camo.

   1. You can customize this accuracy goal to your personal needs.

#### Ellen's Advice

##### If You Want to Win

- Traditionally, Party Tricking Jail has been assigned when an advanced student reaches a score plateau around -3 to -6,

  - I'll assign Party Tricking Jail when the plateau results from inadequate answer choice processing.

  - When I assign Party Tricking Jail this way, I suspend all the student's other homework so their entire Routine is *only* Party Tricking Jail.

  - I find it effective to suspend all the other homework so the student is more able to break the old habits that have left them plateaued.

  - However, you do not have to complete Party Tricking Jail as the only Workout in your Routine.
- Party Tricking Jail should not be undertaken unless you can pass Level 5 of MemAC or have a true appetite for danger.

  - If you're not sensitive about being unsure, feel free to try Party Tricking Jail and learn about Party Tricking as you go.
- It's key to push past the first few questions of Party Tricking Jail where you will undoubtedly feel uncertain and potentially uncomfortable.

  - Feeling uncertain is the whole point.

  - This is a Workout that is designed to hone your intuition. Like Arya learning to fight with a blindfold on.

### Don't Be Desperate in the Answers

#### What problem are we solving?

Sometimes you just want to get the question right so badly that every answer choice looks like an escape hatch.

"Is it right? Is it right!??

If it's right, then I can move on.

Then I won't waste any more time.

Then the stimulus must have meant this.

Then this must have been what they were looking for."

Notice the desperation dripping off this monologue.

You're looking for an answer choice to rescue you, but actually the roles need to be reversed. You have to be the strong one.

Be for real. You are better than 80% wrong.

Answer choices are designed to exploit your desire for them to be right and to prey on moments of inattention.

But you can channel your desire to get the question right into building an invulnerable fortress of analysis throughout every stage of the question. This fortress (Translation, CLIR, Mission, Back Up Plan) will allow you to see exactly why these answers are wrong without. any. drama.

The answer choices only gain manipulative power over you when you look to them to save you. But you are not the freaking damsel that answer C is here to rescue. You're the hero.

You're here to judge answer C, and you're going to judge it according to your vetted, bulletproof standards.

The answers need to impress *you*, not the other way around.

This Accuracy Fix trains you to build the fortress.

Because you are smart enough. You have the tools, the time, and the resources to never need any answer choice to save you again.

THIS WILL HELP YOU IF

You don&#x27;t have expert-level intuition in the answer choices and you want to gain that intuition in order to up-level your speed and accuracy.

SKIP FOR NOW IF

You haven&#x27;t completed the Answer Choice Translation Primer, and you haven&#x27;t beaten Level Five of MemAC.

You aren&#x27;t bought into the Party Tricking, so you rush through the process, repeating that you don&#x27;t know. Go learn about Party Tricking to change your attitude.

Instructions

NOTE: Party Tricking Jail is an advanced workout for those who want to push themselves to become true answer choice experts. Beginners can make good progress with other workouts before entering Party Tricking Jail.

Party Trick every question in an LR or RC section*.

This means you will only read the question stem and answer choices on each question before you select an answer on that question.

Do not look at the stimulus or passage.

Camo the section immediately after completing it.

The Camo will display all the questions that are wrong plus an unknown number of decoy correct answers.

Do not look at the stimulus when you Camo.

Party Trick the returned questions again. Doubt your answers on the questions Camo displays and try to correct your answer.

Get as close to -0 on the Camo as you can.

OPTIONAL: Do not exit Party Tricking Jail until you reach -5 or better pre-Camo.

You can customize this accuracy goal to your personal needs.

Ellen&#x27;s Advice
If You Want to Win

Traditionally, Party Tricking Jail has been assigned when an advanced student reaches a score plateau around -3 to -6,

I&#x27;ll assign Party Tricking Jail when the plateau results from inadequate answer choice processing.

When I assign Party Tricking Jail this way, I suspend all the student&#x27;s other homework so their entire Routine is only Party Tricking Jail.

I find it effective to suspend all the other homework so the student is more able to break the old habits that have left them plateaued.

However, you do not have to complete Party Tricking Jail as the only Workout in your Routine.

Party Tricking Jail should not be undertaken unless you can pass Level 5 of MemAC or have a true appetite for danger.

If you&#x27;re not sensitive about being unsure, feel free to try Party Tricking Jail and learn about Party Tricking as you go.

It&#x27;s key to push past the first few questions of Party Tricking Jail where you will undoubtedly feel uncertain and potentially uncomfortable.

Feeling uncertain is the whole point.

This is a Workout that is designed to hone your intuition. Like Arya learning to fight with a blindfold on.

#### Instructions

1. When you finish an LR or RC section in LawHub, do not press Complete Section. Instead, write down your answers.

   1. Use Self-Paced Mode on all LR and RC sections. Never use Exam Mode.
2. At least 24 hours later, enter your answers into Camo Review.

   1. **Do not check your answers against the answer key before entering your answers into Camo Review.**
3. Camo Review will give you a list of questions to return to.

   1. This list includes all of your wrong answers and an unknown number of decoy correct answers.
4. Reattempt all of the questions in your Camo Review from scratch. Attempt to do a better job on the question now than you did the first time.

   1. Doing a better job means you should retranslate, requip, and re-CLIR the stimulus on LR or retranslate and requip the passage on RC. Retranslate every part of the question, consider every answer choice, and try to correct your process mistakes before choosing a new answer.
5. Enter all your new answers into Camo Review.

6. Camo Review will identify all your Misreads, Conceptual Gaps, Self-Doubt, and Self-Confidence answers.

7. Your Misreads, Conceptual Gaps, and Self-Doubt answers have been automatically added to your Wrong Answer Journal. Go to your Wrong Answer Journal now to immediately begin the Wrong Answer Journal process.

#### Ellen's Advice

##### If You Want to Win

- Never Camo before doing your sections for the day. Always Camo as the last LSAT thing you do for the day.

  - If you Camo immediately before completing another section, you will be overly reactive to the result.

  - If the previous day's section went well, this can make you lazy.

  - If it went poorly, this can make you discouraged.

  - It's not worth taking the risk of either of these reactions affecting your performance on the next section.
- The only point of Camo Review is to get a -0.

  - Do not phone in the Camo Review or rechoose the answer you originally chose out of loyalty because you aren't sure it's wrong.

  - If you got more than two questions back in the Camo Review, some of the questions are definitely wrong, so you must change at least some of them.
- Do not Camo a section immediately after completing it, unless you are directed explicitly to do so as part of another Workout or Routine.

  - If you Camo a section immediately after completing it, the Camo score will be worse and you will lose a valuable opportunity to improve.

### Learn From Your Mistakes

#### What problem are we solving?

Seeing the score is like popping a balloon.

You want to see the score so bad. Because you need to "see how you're doing," "understand where you are," "stay motivated," and "know what to work on."

So, of course, you need to check the score as soon as you're done with the section. But suddenly, as soon as you see that number.

There's nothing interesting left anymore.

Now you "know where you are," but the emotional reaction to where you are overwhelms any hope of strategic insights.

If you like the number, you'll walk away excited and think, "Okay, now I'm good."

And then the next section is likely to crash and burn from laziness and complacency. You don't need to work *that* hard when you're good, right?

If you don't like the number, tears follow.

Now there's no motivation to continue because nothing ever works. So what's the point? Why try? I should just give up and save the effort I would waste on this. I never get better.

When you do this, you actively decline to change the behaviors that caused your wrong answers. This causes you to not improve.

Failing to look inward to understand what you need to change will cause you to leave points on the table.

This Accuracy Fix stops that rollercoaster of pain and plateaus.

You can get better. Give yourself the chance to really understand what you're doing wrong.

When you finish an LR or RC section in LawHub, do not press Complete Section. Instead, write down your answers.

Use Self-Paced Mode on all LR and RC sections. Never use Exam Mode.

At least 24 hours later, enter your answers into Camo Review.

Do not check your answers against the answer key before entering your answers into Camo Review.

Camo Review will give you a list of questions to return to.

This list includes all of your wrong answers and an unknown number of decoy correct answers.

Reattempt all of the questions in your Camo Review from scratch. Attempt to do a better job on the question now than you did the first time.

Doing a better job means you should retranslate, requip, and re-CLIR the stimulus on LR or retranslate and requip the passage on RC. Retranslate every part of the question, consider every answer choice, and try to correct your process mistakes before choosing a new answer.

Ellen&#x27;s Advice
If You Want to Win

Never Camo before doing your sections for the day. Always Camo as the last LSAT thing you do for the day.

If you Camo immediately before completing another section, you will be overly reactive to the result.

If the previous day&#x27;s section went well, this can make you lazy.

If it went poorly, this can make you discouraged.

It&#x27;s not worth taking the risk of either of these reactions affecting your performance on the next section.

The only point of Camo Review is to get a -0.

Do not phone in the Camo Review or rechoose the answer you originally chose out of loyalty because you aren&#x27;t sure it&#x27;s wrong.

If you got more than two questions back in the Camo Review, some of the questions are definitely wrong, so you must change at least some of them.

Do not Camo a section immediately after completing it, unless you are directed explicitly to do so as part of another Workout or Routine.

If you Camo a section immediately after completing it, the Camo score will be worse and you will lose a valuable opportunity to improve.

## Don't Be Desperate in the Answers

### What problem are we solving?

Sometimes you just want to get the question right so badly that every answer choice looks like an escape hatch.

"Is it right? Is it right!??

If it's right, then I can move on.

Then I won't waste any more time.

Then the stimulus must have meant this.

Then this must have been what they were looking for."

Notice the desperation dripping off this monologue.

You're looking for an answer choice to rescue you, but actually the roles need to be reversed. You have to be the strong one.

Be for real. You are better than 80% wrong.

Answer choices are designed to exploit your desire for them to be right and to prey on moments of inattention.

But you can channel your desire to get the question right into building an invulnerable fortress of analysis throughout every stage of the question. This fortress (Translation, CLIR, Mission, Back Up Plan) will allow you to see exactly why these answers are wrong without. any. drama.

The answer choices only gain manipulative power over you when you look to them to save you. But you are not the freaking damsel that answer C is here to rescue. You're the hero.

You're here to judge answer C, and you're going to judge it according to your vetted, bulletproof standards.

The answers need to impress *you*, not the other way around.

This Accuracy Fix trains you to build the fortress.

Because you are smart enough. You have the tools, the time, and the resources to never need any answer choice to save you again.

Instructions

When you finish an LR or RC section in LawHub, do not press Complete Section. Instead, write down your answers.

Use Self-Paced Mode on all LR and RC sections. Never use Exam Mode.

At least 24 hours later, enter your answers into Camo Review.

Do not check your answers against the answer key before entering your answers into Camo Review.

Camo Review will give you a list of questions to return to.

This list includes all of your wrong answers and an unknown number of decoy correct answers.

Reattempt all of the questions in your Camo Review from scratch. Attempt to do a better job on the question now than you did the first time.

Doing a better job means you should retranslate, requip, and re-CLIR the stimulus on LR or retranslate and requip the passage on RC. Retranslate every part of the question, consider every answer choice, and try to correct your process mistakes before choosing a new answer.

Enter all your new answers into Camo Review.

Camo Review will identify all your Misreads, Conceptual Gaps, Self-Doubt, and Self-Confidence answers.

Your Misreads, Conceptual Gaps, and Self-Doubt answers have been automatically added to your Wrong Answer Journal. Go to your Wrong Answer Journal now to immediately begin the Wrong Answer Journal process.

Ellen&#x27;s Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

We go to Party Tricking Jail when you&#x27;ve become good the LSAT, but not quite great at the LSAT. 

I&#x27;ll often assign Party Tricking Jail when my students hit a plateau of around -4 on either LR or RC. These students have tried everything, worked hard, but they aren&#x27;t quite natural at the exam yet.

Often these students will be hardworking technicians who earnestly apply all the methodologies to the absolute best of their ability, but something just hasn&#x27;t clicked. Party Tricking Jail is what makes the LSAT click (when you give it a real chance).

Yes, it&#x27;s hard in the beginning. Yes, you have to show a little bit of resilience when you see low accuracy on the first few sections.

But on the other side, you&#x27;ll have a hard time believing you ever did the LSAT without being able to Party Trick at goal level.

How This Should Be Done

Feel free to customize the Party Tricking Jail goal accuracy to suit your needs.

In general, you should set the Party Tricking Jail goal accuracy to allow for 3-4 more questions wrong than your test-day goal accuracy. For instance, if your goal is -5 on LR/RC on test day, you could set your Party Tricking goal accuracy at -8 or -9. 

Take your Camo really seriously while you&#x27;re in Party Tricking Jail.

The real challenge of Party Tricking Jail comes in Camo. Do not just stick with your original answer because you don&#x27;t have any additional information. You do have additional information: You now know that the answer you chose is under suspicion because only a limited number of decoys will come up in Camo.

So if you have any doubt as to your answer, it&#x27;s time to re-evaluate the answer choices with the added knowledge that you should be suspicious of your original Party Trick answer. You can improve a lot from revisiting the list of questions you&#x27;ve already Party Tricked before you&#x27;ve had the stimulus spoiled.

The goal of the Camo pass in Party Tricking Jail is always to get a -0.

If you&#x27;re not changing your answers on enough questions to get a -0, you are not improving as much as you can from Party Tricking Jail.

SHOW LESS
How to Move On

Move on from Party Tricking Jail when you hit your accuracy goals.

If you do a section or two and have worse than 50% accuracy, that is not a reason to give up and move on from Party Tricking Jail.

It&#x27;s completely expected that you will not have amazing accuracy the first time you try a full section of Party Tricking Jail. That&#x27;s where the Workout starts. 

Your RC Party Tricking accuracy will be better than your LR Party Tricking accuracy because RC is inherently easier to Party Trick (you gain clues from the other questions). This is not a sign that there is anything wrong with you or that you need to stop doing the Workout.
