Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

This Routine purposefully messes with the answer choices to get you to wake up and stop auto-piloting your way through them.

We have some Answer Choice Translation in the warmup and some Party Tricking in the Mini-Cycle. But since the E Blackout removes one of the answers, it forces you to individually evaluate each answer choice's unique relation to the stimulus rather than acting comparatively and choosing the "best" answer. 

There's always just one right answer, and its correctness is determined by its individual relationship to the stimulus, not its relationship to any other answer choice.

This Routine is built to break down these bad answer choice habits.

How This Should Be Done

Students are often desperate to break a plateau, but they aren't desperate to change their habits.

Take the answer choice disruption in this Routine as an opportunity to get you out of any funks you've been in.

This Routine is designed to make you re-examine the answer choices you've taken for granted.

Don't be put off by the uncertainty inherent in the E Blackout or in Party Tricking in the Mini-Cycle. This level of uncertainty is a gift to help you build your intuition.

How to Move On

This is a good Routine to return to intermittently when your answer choices start to get sloppy.

There's no need to move on from it permanently since it can be useful even in the week of your test to offer an answer choice tune-up.
