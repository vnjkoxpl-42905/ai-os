Instructions

Look back over your Wrong Answer Journal.

Based on your past performance, write the top five things you want to do and remember in your LSAT practice moving forward.

Your top five things could include productive habits like always making a Mission on every question. They could also include ideas you want to remember like the three valid Some/Most Inferences.

Your Highlight Reel is your moment for introspection every week to identify the top places you see yourself stumbling in your prep and write how you want to address those places.

You can have a separate Highlight Reel for LR, RC, and for performance mindset, if you want. Otherwise, you can just have one Highlight Reel.

Ellen's Advice
Why Are We Doing This?

We do this Routine to improve your PT or section performance rather than just repeating your PT or section performance. By identifying the actions you took that caused you to miss questions, you enable yourself to change those actions (and raise the score). 

Despite this Routine's name, feel free to do it even when you're still taking stopwatch-timed or test-day timed sections. This Routine can be really useful even when you have not yet taken PTs.

How This Should Be Done

This Routine should be done at least one day after you've completed a PT. Take your time and focus on your review.

Feel free to add extra Targeted Accuracy Fixes to this Routine. You can add on any Workout that pushes you to change any aspect of your process that was problematic during your PT. 

Put your Highlight Reel on the wall of your study room. Review your Highlight Reel every day before starting your practice and on the morning of test day. 

If you are doing this Routine more than once per week, update your Highlight Reel each time rather than making an entirely new Highlight Reel every time you review a PT.

The scores you get on an experimental section in LawHub matter just as much as scores on regular sections in LawHub. LSAC re-ordered and recombined every PT's sections when they got rid of Logic Games, so the experimental sections you see on LawHub are just scrambled real sections. 

The experimental sections on each PT need review, just like the scored sections. The experimental sections on LawHub are real LSAT sections from real exams given on test day.

How to Move On

We never move on from reviewing our PTs. This is how we get ourselves ready for test day all the way up until the day before.
