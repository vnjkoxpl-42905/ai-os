## Method, Flaw, Argument Part and Weaken

[Method Question Strategy](../question-strategies.generated.md#method-question-strategy) asks what the author does across the reasoning rather than whether it is persuasive. The [three-column spreadsheet](../method-library.md#turn-opaque-descriptions-into-concrete-reasoning) preserves the whole answer text, an own-words translation and an invented fitting stimulus. It belongs in review after the question, not as a midsection interruption.

[Weaken Question Strategy](../question-strategies.generated.md#weaken-question-strategy) should be read with the [existing precision check](../method-library.md#keep-assumption-and-weakening-tasks-precise). Connect the whole choice to the actual conclusion's support. The source's claim that only one answer weakens at all is not adopted as a universal theorem.

The newer [Answer Choice Translation Primer](<../../../Loophole Lecture/Day Three/How and Why to Do the Answer Choice Translation Primer/README.md>) is related but different: three written sections, then three aloud RTQ sections, without reading stems/stimuli or choosing answers. Its two-column written stage does not add the invented-stimulus column from the Method/Flaw exercise. [Day Three's comparison](../../../Loophole%20Lecture/Day%20Three/comparison.md) explains that distinction.
