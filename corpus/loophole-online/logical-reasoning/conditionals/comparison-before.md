## Supporting assumptions and inferences have narrower relevance

The Sufficient Assumption and Necessary Assumption definitions/strategies, designing inferences, Principle Conform, Parallel Reasoning and Parallel Flaw support applying conditionals. Their general relevance alone does not make every such LR video conditional content. Prioritize explicit conditional titles, directly linked conditional training blocks, and substantive conditional segments found in their sources. Do not equate ordinary sufficient/necessary assumption terminology with a missing conditional lesson.

Native captions and machine ASR remain unreviewed text; historical adaptations remain distinct from original speech. No LSAT U curriculum was used to fill these gaps.
