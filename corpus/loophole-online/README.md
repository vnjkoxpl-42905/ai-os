## Keep originals, explanations and decisions distinguishable

- **Original evidence:** [recovered archive](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/README.md>) and new byte-verified evidence copies linked from the catalog. Recordings remain in their existing capture folders, with hashes.
- **Compiled teaching reference:** RC/LR/shared routes, [method library](method-library.md), and the four annotated collections below. These explain and connect source claims.
- **Comparisons:** each captured day has a comparison page recording meaningful additions, source differences and suspected caption errors.
- **Approved curriculum:** remains in its existing topic/project homes. Neither the library nor an archived instructor prescription approves adoption, publishing or student assignment.

Automatic captions are fully extracted but **not checked word by word against audio**. Exact written-note text and HTML preserve what the page displayed, including source typos. Written notes can differ from speech or from a separate workout page; keep each version visible.

**Day One gap:** only its Decaf written notes were later recovered. The other ten Day One pages were not checked for notes; the catalog labels them **not checked**, not **absent**. The later batches have explicit captured/not-rendered notes status.
