## Read, translate and quip

[The RTQ method](../method-library.md#read-translate-react-then-check-the-reasoning) links the reading lectures: take a complete conceptual chunk, restate it in ordinary language without losing scope, then react to that content. For LR, produce the appropriate CLIR after processing the stimulus. A reaction can be exploratory; an inference or selected answer still needs logical support.

## Choose review by the observed problem

| Observed problem or purpose | Start here | What the method checks |
|---|---|---|
| Stimulus forgotten after analysis | [Proto-Stimulus Rebound](../review-workouts.generated.md#proto-stimulus-rebound) and [synthesis](../method-library.md#check-whether-the-stimulus-survives-analysis-in-memory) | Retell after CLIR, compare missing/added/changed concepts. RC's related cumulative drill is [Decaf](../reading-comprehension/README.md). |
| Reattempt before knowing the key | [Camo](../method-library.md#review-without-knowing-which-questions-were-wrong) and [source workout](../review-workouts.generated.md#camo-review) | Mix missed questions with correct decoys, attempt fresh, compare outcomes. Labels suggest investigation, not a proven cause. |
| Convert a miss into a future action | [Wrong Answer Journal](../review-workouts.generated.md#wrong-answer-journal) | Record what the learner did and a directly responsive action, not another long answer explanation. |
| Question remains unresolved after review | [Island](../review-workouts.generated.md#how-and-why-to-use-the-island) | Keep an unresolved queue and retry. Familiarity with the answer letter is not explanation mastery. |
| Tighten an accuracy goal | [Accuracy Failsafe](../method-library.md#use-a-two-success-accuracy-progression) and [source](../review-workouts.generated.md#accuracy-failsafe) | Two consecutive qualifying sections tighten the source's next target; failures change the next practice action. |
| Longer reading practice or timing work | [Foundation routine](../review-workouts.generated.md#translation-foundation-routine), [Marathon](../review-workouts.generated.md#translation-marathon), [target caveats](../method-library.md#preserve-the-sources-routine-targets-without-making-them-requirements) | Historical workload and timing prescriptions, not blanket student requirements or proof that fatigue should be ignored. |

Camo normally describes waiting at least a day; Accuracy Failsafe explicitly specifies immediate Camo after a five-minute break. Preserve this stated exception. Keep initial answers and revealed results separate when blind review is the purpose.
