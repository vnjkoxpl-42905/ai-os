## Question strategies

The [Day Two RC Process and Missions](../../../Loophole%20Lecture/Day%20Two/README.md) establish the common procedure: translate the exact stem, form a text-grounded answer, then evaluate each whole choice against the task. A Mission is not a prediction to match literally.
