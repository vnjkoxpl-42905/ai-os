[RC home](README.md) · [New native source packages](<../../../Loophole Lecture/RC Activity Demos 2026-10-18/README.md>) · [Full workout instructions](../workouts/README.md)

These are Ellen Cassidy's demonstrations and activity instructions. This route collects the named exercises, rather than every RC theory or question-strategy lecture. Original automatic captions, written instructions and demonstrated process remain separate. Captions are not a word-by-word listening-verified edition.
