Instructions

  1. Make a spreadsheet with three columns, or use this template:

    1. Column 1: Vexing Answer Choice

    2. Column 2: Answer Choice Translation

    3. Column 3: Corresponding Stimulus

  2. Collect all the difficult answer choices to translate, focusing especially on Method/Flaw/Argument Part answer choices.

    1. This could include harvesting answer choices from your sections immediately after you're done or going back over old sections to harvest answer choices.

  3. Write the difficult answers in the Vexing Answer Choice column of the spreadsheet, one answer choice per row.

  4. Translate the answers in the Answer Choice Translation column of the spreadsheet.

  5. In the Corresponding Stimulus column of the spreadsheet, write an example stimulus which would make the answer choice in that row correct.
