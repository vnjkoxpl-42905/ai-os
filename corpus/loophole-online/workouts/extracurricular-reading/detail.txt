Instructions

Every day, translate elevated reading material from outside the LSAT, using the following sources:

ScienceDaily.com

Ideal daily reading for those who feel alienated by scientific language or fear science passages in RC

Be sure to choose the articles that challenge you the most, not articles that look familiar or interesting

Aeon.co

The most similar articles to LSAT RC on the internet

Reading an Aeon.co article every day is a great habit if you have no specific topic-based weaknesses, but would like to make RC-style reading a habit.

ALDaily.com

Ideal daily reading for those who feel alienated by abstract language, art, humanities, and pretension.

Be sure to choose the articles that look the most unfamiliar, not articles that speak to your pre-existing interests.

NYT Guide to Essential Knowledge

The single best way to fix gaps in common knowledge

Here is a list of sections in the Guide that are relevant to LSAT. If any of these topics would trouble you as an RC passage, read over that section of the guide.

LSAT Relevant Guide Sections: Architecture, Zoology, Botany, History, Art, Photography, Literature, Music (the serious parts about jazz and blues, especially), Film, Dance, Drama, Climate & Weather and Glossary of Weather Words (In Geography), Government, Law, Philosophy, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Astronomy, Geology

Crash Course

Not a written source, but Crash Course is too useful for solving common knowledge gaps to not include

Watch Crash Course while you're in line, waiting for someone, on hold, brushing your teeth, etc. Any time you could be scrolling on your phone, watch Crash Course instead.

Top Five LSAT Relevant Crash Course Sections: Intellectual Property (Ellen's favorite for LSAT students), Economics, US Government & Politics, Art History, and Philosophy

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

Our goal is threefold:

Expand your vocabulary

Harvest challenging words from your extracurricular reading and add it to them to your Vocab Tracker.

Practice these new words daily with or without AI.

Familiarize you with new academic topics

Many student have trouble locking in on topics that they find boring or unfamiliar. Expand your common knowledge so there are fewer topics that shake you.

Always choose topics that you are least familiar with and least interested in.  

Improve your Translation skills

Your Translation skill set is one of the primary determinants of your test-day performance, but real LSAT material is limited.

By translating outside sources, you get the chance to improve your Translation skill without only relying on non-renewable LSAT materials.
