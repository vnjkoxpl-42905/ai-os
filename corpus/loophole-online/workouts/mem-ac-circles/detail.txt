Instructions

NOTE: MemAC Circles are an advanced workout. Do not attempt MemAC Circles until you have beaten MemAC Level 5.

MemAC the answer choices at either Level 3 or Level 5, depending on your preference*.

Translate, quip, and CLIR the stimulus.

Read the question stem, identify the question type and whether the question is Powerful-Provable.

On each individual answer choice:

Read, translate, and quip the answer choice.

Investigate whether this answer choice meets the question type's Back Up Plan.

If you think this answer meets the Back-Up Plan question, highlight the first word of the answer.

If you are not sure whether this answer meets the Back-Up Plan question, leave it open.

If you know this answer doesn't meet the Back-Up Plan question, cross it out.

Choose amongst the open and highlighted answer choices.

Hyperskip, if needed.

Aim to hyperskip 5-7 questions per section.

Reattempt your hyperskips according to the LR Full Question Process, not the MemAC Circle Process.

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

MemAC Circles help keep your MemAC skills sharp while allowing you to make the stimulus less intimidating.

Once you can MemAC the answer choices, reading the stimulus is much more accessible, almost like you've been given a sneak preview.

MemAC Circles slow down the LR process to enhance answer choice understanding and make you less impulsive in your answer choice selection.

It can be difficult to ensure that you properly CLIR and Mission on MemAC Circles because the subconscious recognition of the correct answer can be involuntary, even while you're still in the middle of the stimulus.

Stay strong in your process. You don't want to get too hasty and miss questions because you didn't give every answer a proper post-stimulus chance.
