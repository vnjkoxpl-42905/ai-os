Instructions

Complete a section* normally, according to whatever workout you're using at the time, but with one adjustment:

If you're going to choose an answer, you have to choose it right after you first read it.

You can continue reading the answers to see if you would like to change your answer after choosing, but you cannot do so.

There is no hyperskip pass on DAREs because you cannot change your answer.

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

DARE Sections are a drastic measure to force you to feel the pain of Early Answer Lock In.

Don't do DARE Sections often.

Maybe do them once or twice total, unless you have a severe, recurrent Early Answer Lock In problem.

DARE Sections are meant to be a touch-the-stove-to-learn-it's-hot kind of Workout.
