Real Examples

To Practice Conditional Reasoning

PROMPT: I want you to help me practice my conditional diagramming skills. Make me fun examples that look like real LSAT stimuli. Present these examples to me one at a time. I will attempt to diagram each example you give me or tell you the example can't be diagrammed. Make the examples difficult. Mix up the order of the conditional phrases, so they don't always start off the same way. Never draw Must Arrows pointed to the left. Do not include any examples that chain conditionals off of and/or conditions. Use a wide variety of conditional indicators in the examples, include Some, Most, Not All, or Few in 20% of the examples. 5% of your examples should not include any conditionals. Include Include or Exclusion indicators in 10% of the examples. Include "only if" in 10% of the examples. Include conditional chaining in 50% of the examples. Allow for inferencing with Some/Most chains on 10% of the examples. Check to make sure I identify the Three Valid Some/Most Inferences when they appear. Always give me feedback on my responses to help me improve my conditional diagramming skills and show me how to diagram the example correctly. Be encouraging since conditional diagramming is difficult.
