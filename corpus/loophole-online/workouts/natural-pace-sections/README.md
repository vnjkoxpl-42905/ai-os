### Instructions

1. Set the LawHub to 400 minutes in Self-Paced Mode* .

   1. This is to make sure the LawHub timer doesn't interfere with your natural pace.
2. Start a stopwatch.

3. Complete your first pass through the section.

4. Press Lap on the stopwatch.

   1. On iPhone, the Lap button is labeled "Lap."

   2. On Android, the Lap button has a stopwatch symbol and is to the right of the Pause button.
5. Complete your hyperskip pass.

6. Stop the stopwatch.

7. Record the lap times and total time.

   1. The first lap time is your first-pass time. This tells us how long it took you to answer the questions.

   2. The second lap time is your hyperskip time. This tells us how long your hyperskip pass took.

   3. Your total time is how long you took on the section in total. This is your natural pace.

### Ellen's Advice

#### If You Want to Win

- Natural Pace sections are adaptable to any Workout. You can think of them like a modification on how any Workout is timed.

  - For instance, you can do a Natural Pace section on a Mini-Cycle, Inside Out, or Acronym.

  - Since Natural Pace sections only specify how the Workout is timed, they overlay on any other Workout.
- Natural Pace sections are great for fixing accuracy issues without the pressure of time.

- If you struggle with anxiety, you'll want to primarily focus on Natural Pace sections in the beginning of your prep until you start to feel comfortable with the exam.

  - You can work to bring your time down gradually through Marathons, Failsafes, and Pacing Practice later.
- If you have a stop/start breaks accommodation, you can take breaks during section by pressing Pause on the stopwatch.

  - Put a Post-It over the time display on the stopwatch so you can't see the amount of time that's passed when you press Pause.

## Intuition, Not Impulse

### What problem are we solving?

Impulse is great until you have no idea why you chose a wrong answer.

Acting on impulse is like the evil twin of acting on intuition. It happens when you try to be fast before you've ingrained the process at a subconscious level.

Intuition takes over after you've ingrained the process at a subconscious level. Intuition can be reliable and purposeful.

Impulse can't.

I know you think I don't understand: "Like come on, Ellen. Don't you know I have to do this in 35 minutes?" I get you.

This advice is coming from someone who could -0 Logical Reasoning sections in 27 minutes one month after meeting the LSAT for the first time.

It's not that I love to be slow or want you to be slow; I actually love speed. My Spotify history of Eminem's Rap God has the receipts (yes that song is so problematic it's also so fast and it just soothes my brain so much).

But I want you to be right more than I want you to be fast. If you're right, you can train to be fast. If you're fast and wrong, you'll have to slow down to train to be right.

No matter how quickly or intuitively I ever choose an answer, if you were to freeze time and say, "Hey Ellen, why'd you do that?" I'd be able to tell you.

Even if I'm not consciously thinking out all the words behind my decision, I *could* tell you why I'm doing it. If you can't say why you're doing something, it creates score variation, the hallmark of acting on impulse rather than purpose.

There will be a time when you'll approach the LSAT intuitively, but that intuition will be rooted in strategy. This Accuracy Fix is meant to ingrain that reliable, process-driven strategy that will mature into intuition.

You take care of the process, and the process will take care of the test.

Instructions

Set the LawHub to 400 minutes in Self-Paced Mode*.

This is to make sure the LawHub timer doesn&#x27;t interfere with your natural pace.

Start a stopwatch.

Complete your first pass through the section.

Press Lap on the stopwatch.

On iPhone, the Lap button is labeled &quot;Lap.&quot;

On Android, the Lap button has a stopwatch symbol and is to the right of the Pause button.

Complete your hyperskip pass.

Stop the stopwatch.

Record the lap times and total time.

The first lap time is your first-pass time. This tells us how long it took you to answer the questions.

The second lap time is your hyperskip time. This tells us how long your hyperskip pass took.

Your total time is how long you took on the section in total. This is your natural pace.

New Sections Only

Count-Up Stopwatch

Ellen&#x27;s Advice
If You Want to Win

Natural Pace sections are adaptable to any Workout. You can think of them like a modification on how any Workout is timed.

For instance, you can do a Natural Pace section on a Mini-Cycle, Inside Out, or Acronym.

Since Natural Pace sections only specify how the Workout is timed, they overlay on any other Workout.

Natural Pace sections are great for fixing accuracy issues without the pressure of time.

If you struggle with anxiety, you&#x27;ll want to primarily focus on Natural Pace sections in the beginning of your prep until you start to feel comfortable with the exam.

You can work to bring your time down gradually through Marathons, Failsafes, and Pacing Practice later.

If you have a stop/start breaks accommodation, you can take breaks during section by pressing Pause on the stopwatch.

Put a Post-It over the time display on the stopwatch so you can&#x27;t see the amount of time that&#x27;s passed when you press Pause.
