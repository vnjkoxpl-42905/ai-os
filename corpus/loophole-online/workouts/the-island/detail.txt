NOTE: The Island is a spreadsheet you make yourself with one column called Island and another column called Canoed Away.

If a question is still mystifying after Camo, just add the citation of the question to the Island.

This means at least one of these three things is true:

I don't understand why the right answer is right.

I don't understand why the wrong answer is wrong.

I have no idea how I could get this right in the future.

At the start of prep each day, spend 5 minutes on the Island retrying a question or two. 

If the question clicks, move it to Canoed Away. Otherwise, keep it on the Island. 

Fill in your Wrong Answer Journal with the new information you've learned now that the question has canoed away.
