Instructions

Translate the passage*.

Proceed to the first question and translate the question stem.

Make a Mission.

Making a Mission means answering the question yourself.

Go back to the passage to enable yourself to answer the question, if needed. Only look back at the passage in the Mission stage of the question.

Don't try to match your Mission in the answer choices.

On every individual answer choice:

Read, translate and quip the answer choice.

Investigate whether this answer choice meets the standard of what the question stem is asking.

If you think this answer meets the standard of the question stem, highlight the first word of the answer.

If you are not sure whether this answer meets the standard of the question stem, leave it open.

If you know this answer does not meets the standard of the question stem, cross it out.

Choose amongst the open and highlighted answer choices.

Hyperskip, if needed.

Aim to hyperskip a total of 5-7 questions per section.

Only go back to your hyperskips at the end of the entire section, not at the end of the passage they're associated with.

You can go back to the passage during the hyperskip.

Use any remaining time to return to your hyperskips.
