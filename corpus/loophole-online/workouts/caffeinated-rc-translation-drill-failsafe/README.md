#### Will This Help Me?

This will help you if

Skip for now if

You cannot reliably understand and retain the passage during Caffeinated RC Translation Drills.

timing compatible with 35m, 53m, 70m, Natural Pace, Marathon, Stopwatch

### Instructions

1. **Timing Rules**

   1. **First Day**:

      1. Do a Caffeinated Translation Drill on a passage with a stopwatch to establish your baseline natural pace* .
   2. **All Other Days**:

      1. Adopt the timing pattern that you earned the previous study day.

      2. Set a countdown timer for your baseline natural pace plus or minus the 20-second adjustment you have earned on your previous Failsafe.
2. Read, translate, and quip the first paragraph silently.

3. At the end of the paragraph, take a few seconds to relook at the paragraph and confirm that your Translation is right. Make sure you have everything and nothing is left out.

4. Repeat the process on the subsequent paragraphs.

5. At the end of the passage, take ~30 seconds or less to review the entire passage.

6. Stop the time and write the time down.

7. **Off time**, recap the Translation of the entire passage out loud. Check the text to see if there are any major gaps in your Translation.

   1. If there are more than two gaps, you have not beaten the Failsafe. Increase the time by 20 seconds on your next Failsafe.

   2. If there are less than two gaps, you have beaten the Failsafe. Decrease the time by 20 seconds on your next Failsafe.
8. You graduate from the Caffeinated RC Translation Drill Failsafes when you are able to beat the Failsafe consistently at an average of four minutes per passage while meeting theCaffeinated Translation Standard.

### Ellen's Advice

#### If You Want to Win

**The Caffeinated Translation Standard**: Even if you couldn't look back at the passage, you would still feel comfortable that you have a shot at perfect accuracy in the questions.

**Test-Day Caffeinated Translation Time Goal**: 3:30 to 4:30 per passage, but with an average per-passage Translation time of four minutes.
