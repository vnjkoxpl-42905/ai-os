Strengthen Your Working Memory

This will help you if:

You forget a significant amount of the stimulus by the time you finish its questions. You find yourself wanting to look back at the stimulus in the answer choices not because of generalized self-doubt but because you actually do not remember what happened.

Skip this for now if:

You retain the information in the stimulus evenly throughout the question and do not notice a drop off in your memory.
