Quick Facts
Will This Help Me?

THIS WILL HELP YOU IF

You forget a significant amount of the stimulus by the time you finish its questions. You find yourself wanting to look back at the stimulus in the answer choices not because of generalized self-doubt but because you actually do not remember what happened.

SKIP FOR NOW IF

You retain the information in the stimulus evenly throughout the question and do not notice a drop off in your memory.

Instructions

Translate, quip, and CLIR the stimulus*.

Cover the stimulus and say the full Translation from memory.

Try to reorder the argument parts in your retelling of the Translation.

Complete the rest of the question. Choose an answer.

Say the full Translation of the stimulus from memory again.

Relook at the stimulus and see if anything is missing, added, or changed.

Take a note of what was missing, added, or changed.
