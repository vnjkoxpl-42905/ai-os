Instructions

Read, translate, and quip every sentence*. Max every two sentences if they are really simple. Exactly how you would translate in LR. 

At the end of each paragraph, pause to review the Translation while looking at the paragraph and confirm with yourself that you really know it. 

In this step, you are not rereading from scratch. 

You're confirming the Translation you already have is right. 

This style of confirming your Translation with the text applies to every confirmation you ever do on RC.

After each paragraph, relook at all the paragraphs that you've read so far to confirm they are placed in your memory. 

Continue on until the end of the passage with the same process.

At the end of the passage, do a final review by relooking at the entire passage and reconfirming your Translation. This should take about 30 seconds.

Complete this same process on all remaining passages in the section.
