Instructions

NOTE: Cappuccino is similar to Decaf, except instead of recapping all of your Translations after finishing with each paragraph, you’ll be looking back at the text to confirm your Translations.

Read, translate, and quip paragraph 1*. Do it just like LR.

Look away from paragraph 1 and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of paragraph 1 silently.

Take a few seconds to confirm your Translation with the paragraph you just read.

In this step, you are not rereading from scratch. 

You're confirming the Translation you already have is right. 

This style of confirming your Translation with the text applies to every confirmation you ever do on RC.

Read, translate, and quip paragraph 2. Do it just like LR.

Look away from paragraph 2 and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of paragraph 2 silently.

Take a few seconds to confirm your Translation with all the text you've read of the passage so far.

This confirmation step will include all the paragraphs you've read so far.

Repeat this pattern for all remaining paragraphs.

OPTIONAL: Look away and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of the entire passage silently.

This step is optional, but you should do it when you first begin Cappuccino RC Translation Drills and periodically afterward, just to confirm that you can.

Complete this same process on all remaining passages in the section.
