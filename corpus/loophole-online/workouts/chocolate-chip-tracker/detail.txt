Instructions

NOTE: This workout is designed to reward you for completing objectives.

Choose one or more objectives to train yourself on during this workout. Look below for some suggested objectives.

Place a bowl of chocolate chips (or grapes, peanuts, M&Ms, or another small snack) to the side when you’re studying.

Eat one chocolate chip upon completion of every question when you fulfill your objectives.

Throw one chocolate chip into the trash immediately when you do not fulfill your objectives.

Favorite Chocolate Chips for this Workout: Guittard Milk Chocolate
