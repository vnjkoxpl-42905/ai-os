Instructions

Before starting your section*, draw the following two-column chart on your scratch paper, or use this template:

Column 1 Header: Original answer is WRONG

Column 2 Header: New answer is CORRECT

Draw horizontal lines below the header to represent rows in the chart.

As you complete the section, write the number of each hyperskipped question on the left side of each row.

When you return to each hyperskipped question on the hyperskip pass, investigate whether you meet:

The "Original answer is WRONG" criterion

The "New answer is CORRECT" criterion

If you meet each criterion, check the appropriate column in the row for that question.

If you check both columns on a question, you can change your answer.

This means you met both criteria for that question.

If you do not have two checkmarks on a question within 90 seconds of returning to the question, you cannot change your answer.
