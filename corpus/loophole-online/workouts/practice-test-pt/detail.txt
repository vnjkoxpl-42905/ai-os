Instructions

In Self-Paced Mode, open each section of a full PT* in separate tabs in LawHub before you start.

We are opening up separate tabs so we do not have to press Complete Section to go to the next section.

We care about not pressing Complete Section because we want to Camo the PT, rather than having all the correct answers revealed to us at the end of the PT.
