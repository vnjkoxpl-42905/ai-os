Instructions

NOTE: Party Trick Circles are an advanced workout. Do not attempt Party Trick Circles until you have completed a few sections of Party Tricking Jail.

Party Trick the answer choices*.

Translate, quip, and CLIR the stimulus.

Read the question stem, identify the question type and whether the question is Powerful-Provable.

On each individual answer choice:

Read, translate, and quip the answer choice.

Investigate whether this answer choice meets the question type's Back Up Plan.

If you think this answer meets the Back-Up Plan question, highlight the first word of the answer.

If you are not sure whether this answer meets the Back-Up Plan question, leave it open.

If you know this answer doesn't meet the Back-Up Plan question, cross it out.

Choose amongst the open and highlighted answer choices.

Hyperskip, if needed.

Aim to hyperskip 5-7 questions per section.

Reattempt your hyperskips according to the LR Full Question Process, not the Party Trick Circle Process.

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

Party Trick Circles fundamentally alter your perception of difficulty in Logical Reasoning.

The questions feel so different (and often so much easier) when you Party Trick them first.

Party Trick Circles can be a great way to break a plateau on LR.

It can be difficult to ensure that you properly CLIR and Mission on Party Trick Circles because the subconscious recognition of the correct answer can be involuntary, even while you're still in the middle of the stimulus.

Stay strong in your process.

Don't get too hasty and miss questions because you didn't give every answer a proper post-stimulus chance.
