Quick Facts
Will This Help Me?

THIS WILL HELP YOU IF

You forget a significant amount of the passage by the time you finish its questions. You find yourself wanting to look back at the passage in the answer choices not because of generalized self-doubt but because you actually do not remember what happened.

SKIP FOR NOW IF

You retain the information in the passage evenly throughout the questions and do not notice a drop off in your memory.

Instructions

Translate the passage*.

Complete all the questions associated with the passage.

Recap the full Translation of the passage out loud without looking at the passage.

This recap should be of the same quality as a full passage recap at the end of a Decaf RC Translation Drill.

Look at the passage and see if anything is missing, added, or changed.

If something is missing, note what was missing, added, or changed.
