Instructions

Translate and quip the passage or stimulus*.

If you encounter a word you are unsure of, highlight it.

Finish the passage or stimulus.

Be sure to highlight ALL the words you're unsure of.

If you wouldn't bet money on the definition of the word, that means you are unsure of the definition of that word.

Add the highlighted words to your Vocab Tracker and write in the definitions according to the instructions outlined in the How to Learn Vocab Without AI video.

Restart the Vocabulary Translation Drill on the same stimulus or passage now that you know what the words mean.

Continue this pattern for the entire section.

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

Vocabulary Translation Drills are designed to fill in gaps in vocabulary that impede understanding the stimulus.

It feels significantly different to engage with a stimulus when you know what all the words in the stimulus mean. Vocabulary Translation Drills allow students to experience that reality.

It is worth building your vocabulary skills. You can learn enough words to improve your score.

Yes, you don't know that the words you learn will be on your exam.

No one ever has any guarantee of what words will be on their exam, and it's on us to improve ourselves despite this fact.

Vocabulary building is cumulative and you will increase the chance that you know all the words that appear on your exam by gradually enhancing your vocabulary, no matter where you are starting out.

Vocabulary Translation Drill are a great idea for non-native English speakers, especially students who do not live in an English-speaking environment.
