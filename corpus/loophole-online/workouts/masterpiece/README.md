### Instructions

**NOTE**: Masterpieces are multi-day workouts focused on a single section* . You can do a three-day Masterpiece or a four-day Masterpiece to start. Don't worry, you do less and less work as the Masterpiece progresses each day.

1. Complete the section in any workout style.

   1. Mark questions for hyperskip, but don't perform your hyperskip pass yet.

   2. When you're done with the section, put it aside without grading it.
2. The next day, redo each question in the section fully.

   1. When you return to the section, keep your answer selected at first, but uncross off all other answer choices.

   2. Do not just look at your previous answer without actually redoing the full question. Your objective is to decide whether you want to stick to your previous answer or choose a new answer.

   3. On each question you have three options:

      1. Lock in your previous answer.

         1. Once an answer is locked, you will not look at this question again until Camo Day. Locking is a way to signal to yourself that you know this answer is correct and do not need to look at it again.

         2. **Do not lock in more than 50% of your remaining answers on any lock-in pass** until you get to the last day of your Masterpiece.
      2. Keep your answer the same, but leave the question unlocked.

         1. Leave a question unlocked if you are not sure about whether the answer you've selected is correct.
      3. Change your answer.

         1. If you change your answer, you may not lock the question on this pass.
3. The next day, return only to your unlocked questions and redo them. Decide to lock in or change your answers, following the same procedure you did on day 2.

   1. This process repeats for however many days your Masterpiece will last.

   2. **Make sure all questions are locked in by the last day.**
4. **Optional**: On the last day, print out a fresh copy of the section and redo it straight-through.

   1. Compare the fresh section to your locked-in answers and see if there are any differences. If so, reconcile the two sections. Decide on your final answer before putting it into Camo.
5. After your last pass on the section, with all answers locked in, proceed with Camo and Wrong Answer Journaling.
