Keep a list of the 15 hardest questions you've ever completed in your prep.

Each day, study two questions from the Hit List.

Dissect exactly how each question is working.

How does the question both camouflage and justify the correct answer?

What makes this question hard?

Figure out what you can take from this question.

And what can you work on in your process to improve your performance on questions that are difficult for the same reason going forward?
