Instructions

NOTE: Mini-Cycles involve working through full LR sections in batches of 9 questions at a time, without breaks between batches. Do not do less than a full section*.

For questions #1-3, translate and CLIR the stimulus only.

Do not look at the question stem or answer choices yet.

For questions #4-6, Party Trick the answer choices only.

Do not read the stimulus yet.

For questions #7-9, complete the full question according to the straight-through LR Full Question Process.

Return to questions #1-3 and Party Trick them.

After Party Tricking #1-3, relook at the stimulus and change your answer if needed.

Return to questions #4-6 and translate and CLIR.

Choose an answer based on your new understanding of the stimulus.

Note if the answer choice differs from your original Party-Tricked choice.

Repeat this process with the next 9 questions, and then again with the remaining questions in the section. It’s expected that the last batch of questions is less than 9, so you won’t do as many full questions in that batch.

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

Mini-Cycles are great workouts for students with substantial previous prep experience.

They will force you to ditch toxic habits because they are so intentionally disruptive.

By slowing you down with radically different processes, Mini-Cycles force you to act with intention, rather than replaying old impulses just to go fast.

This often results in a significant accuracy gain for previously plateaued test takers.

Mini-Cycles are not meant to be done in 35 minutes, so do not rush this Workout to accommodate test-day timing. 50 minutes is considered a fast Mini-Cycle.

Don't let the fact that you are not confident in your ability to Party Trick ruin your Mini-Cycles.

No one is confident in their ability to Party Trick – it's not a Workout where you'll initially be sure of yourself.

The whole point of Party Tricking is that it makes you hone your intuition, which has discomfort attached to it in the beginning.

Choose an answer to the best of your Party-Tricking ability.
