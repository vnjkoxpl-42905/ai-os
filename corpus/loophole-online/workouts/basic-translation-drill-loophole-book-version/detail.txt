Instructions

Grab a clean LR section* and start at question #1.

Read the stimulus well once. You can go back and re-read sentences as you go, as much as you want. Once you hit the last period at the end of the stimulus, you’re done.

Cover up the stimulus with your hand.

In your head, say, “OK, so…” and tell yourself what happened in the stimulus. Do it casually, like you’re explaining a story to a friend.

This explanation is your Translation of the stimulus. Write your Translation as a bulleted list in your notebook. Your goal is to reduce the stimulus to things that actually matter. By transforming the stimulus into your own words, you forget about the distracting jargon and focus more clearly.

Read the stimulus again to see how close you were. Record where you were off base and why.

Proceed to the next question and repeat these steps until you reach the end of the section. Do not read the question stem or answer choices on any of your Basic Translation Drills.
