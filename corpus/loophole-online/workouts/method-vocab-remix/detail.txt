Instructions

Write 10 Method/Flaw/Argument Part answer choices of your own design using two random words from the Uncut Method Vocab List.

Each of your answer choices should use different Uncut Method Vocab List words. No repeats.

Prioritize words you know the least well. 

Use the hardest words.

You are allowed to change the parts of speech of words on the Uncut Method Vocab List.

Write seven Method/Flaw/Argument Part answer choices of your own design using three random words from the Uncut Method Vocab List.

Write five Method/Flaw/Argument Part answer choices of your own design using four random words from the Uncut Method Vocab List.

Write two Method/Flaw/Argument Part answer choices of your own design using five random words from the Uncut Method Vocab List.

Write one Method/Flaw/Argument Part answer choices of your own design using six random words from the Uncut Method Vocab List.
