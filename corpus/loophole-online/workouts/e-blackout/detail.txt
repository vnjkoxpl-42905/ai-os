Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

E Blackouts are the single best answer choice analysis training workout around.

E Blackouts one of the only effective ways I've found to stop Early Answer Lock In and E Bias. No other workout even comes close.

E Blackouts force you to stop choosing answer comparatively.

On an E Blackout, you can't scan every answer and select the "best answer" by process of elimination.

You are forced to gauge each answer's individual relationship to the stimulus using the Back Up Plan, which results in much higher accuracy.

E Blackouts look a lot scarier than they actually are. Give them a chance, and you'll see that they make you a better test taker.

Weird but a lot of students improve their score when they E Blackout.

I know you might not believe me, but a tutoring student did E Blackout their real LSAT and got a 180. I have the receipts.
