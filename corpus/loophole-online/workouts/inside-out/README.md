### Instructions

1. **OPTIONAL**: Read the first and last sentence of the passage.

   1. Translate and quip these sentences. Add the sentences up to get an idea of what the passage is likely about.

   2. These sentences should allow you to think inferentially, not just limit your Party Tricking to what was literally mentioned in the first and last sentence.
2. Party Trick the passage's questions.

   1. Write down your answers so you know your Party Trick accuracy later, even if you change your answer after reading the passage.
3. RC Translation Drill the passage.

   1. Whether you use Decaf, Cappuccino, or Caffeinated depends on where you are in your prep, so use whichever one you're currently on.
4. Re-attempt the questions.

5. Repeat this process on all the remaining passages in the section* .

## Stop Mental and Physical Self-Sabotage

### What problem are we solving?

Have you ever done a section when:

- you're falling asleep in the middle?

- someone else is taking a call a little too close to you?

- you're looking at the clock over and over again and saying to yourself "I have to speed up"?

- you're saying to yourself, "I can't get this wrong. Oh my god, I don't understand. I have to get it. I'll never get into Berkeley. My dad is right about me."?

- you just saw a previous score and you're going to cry?

- you have a stye on your left eye and also maybe COVID?

Doing the LSAT like this is mental and physical self-sabotage.

You are willfully engaging in behaviors that decrease your score.

I care about you and I care about your score, so I'm going to give you some real talk here.

Telling yourself you can't do something drastically increases the chances you won't be able to do that thing.

So stop it.

Doing LSAT in an environment or situation that pulls on your attention sets you up to fail.

So stop it.

Thinking about the past, the future, and what other people think while you're doing the LSAT ensures that you do not do your best.

So stop it.

This is your Olympics.

We need you in peak mental and physical condition while you're training to ensure that you're in peak condition on test day. This means that you have to advocate for yourself.

Create space in your life for LSAT is to be the #1 priority, not something you do after every high priority is done.

This Accuracy Fix teaches you how to be that your advocate.

## Disrupt Your Toxic Habits

### What problem are we solving?

It's so easy to attribute a score you don't like to anything but your toxic habits. To say, "This one was just hard" or "I always have trouble with that question type." But none of those factors chose the wrong answer. You chose the wrong answer. And if you want your score to change, you have to change the toxic habits that made you choose that answer.

Toxic habits are like lifting weights with bad form – they feel like they're making lifting easier. But It's only a matter of time until you get injured. If you have bad form, you can't up your weight safely. Instead, you have to lower the weight and fix your form. (I would know... I had to have surgery to fix two hernias from bad weight-lighting form.)

The sad thing is that many toxic habits are recommended to well-meaning students who simply don't know any better. Just like the trainer who told me it was totally fine to up my weight way too fast.

If an "expert" tells you that you that only have to read the first word of most RC answers, who are you to know that's toxic?

You just started LSAT prep. You don't know any better. And you accidentally adopt a toxic habit that lowers your score.

That is the information asymmetry that keeps me grinding beyond the point of rationality. I hate that information asymmetry because it lets people take advantage of you.

Advice like that decreases your score and you have to feel the pain of score variation to figure out that it was toxic in the first place.

That's why I've created and tested a non-toxic, proven LSAT process for the past 30,000+ hours. The processes in Loophole Online are built to replace toxic habits from the ground up.

Implementing non-toxic habits will get you the answer 100 out of 100 times when you implement it as instructed. That's what this Accuracy Fix trains you to do.

## Don't Be Desperate in the Answers

### What problem are we solving?

Sometimes you just want to get the question right so badly that every answer choice looks like an escape hatch.

"Is it right? Is it right!??

If it's right, then I can move on.

Then I won't waste any more time.

Then the stimulus must have meant this.

Then this must have been what they were looking for."

Notice the desperation dripping off this monologue.

You're looking for an answer choice to rescue you, but actually the roles need to be reversed. You have to be the strong one.

Be for real. You are better than 80% wrong.

Answer choices are designed to exploit your desire for them to be right and to prey on moments of inattention.

But you can channel your desire to get the question right into building an invulnerable fortress of analysis throughout every stage of the question. This fortress (Translation, CLIR, Mission, Back Up Plan) will allow you to see exactly why these answers are wrong without. any. drama.

The answer choices only gain manipulative power over you when you look to them to save you. But you are not the freaking damsel that answer C is here to rescue. You're the hero.

You're here to judge answer C, and you're going to judge it according to your vetted, bulletproof standards.

The answers need to impress *you*, not the other way around.

This Accuracy Fix trains you to build the fortress.

Because you are smart enough. You have the tools, the time, and the resources to never need any answer choice to save you again.

What Problem Are We Solving?

Sometimes you just want to get the question right so badly that every answer choice looks like an escape hatch.

&quot;Is it right? Is it right!??

If it&#x27;s right, then I can move on.

Then I won&#x27;t waste any more time.

Then the stimulus must have meant this.

Then this must have been what they were looking for.&quot;

Notice the desperation dripping off this monologue. 

You&#x27;re looking for an answer choice to rescue you, but actually the roles need to be reversed. You have to be the strong one.

Be for real. You are better than 80% wrong. 

Answer choices are designed to exploit your desire for them to be right and to prey on moments of inattention.

But you can channel your desire to get the question right into building an invulnerable fortress of analysis throughout every stage of the question. This fortress (Translation, CLIR, Mission, Back Up Plan) will allow you to see exactly why these answers are wrong without. any. drama.

The answer choices only gain manipulative power over you when you look to them to save you. But you are not the freaking damsel that answer C is here to rescue. You&#x27;re the hero.

You&#x27;re here to judge answer C, and you&#x27;re going to judge it according to your vetted, bulletproof standards.

The answers need to impress you, not the other way around.

This Accuracy Fix trains you to build the fortress.

Because you are smart enough. You have the tools, the time, and the resources to never need any answer choice to save you again.
