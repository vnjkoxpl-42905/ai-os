Instructions

OPTIONAL: Read the first and last sentence of the passage.

Translate and quip these sentences. Add the sentences up to get an idea of what the passage is likely about.

These sentences should allow you to think inferentially, not just limit your Party Tricking to what was literally mentioned in the first and last sentence.

Party Trick the passage's questions.

Write down your answers so you know your Party Trick accuracy later, even if you change your answer after reading the passage.

RC Translation Drill the passage.

Whether you use Decaf, Cappuccino, or Caffeinated depends on where you are in your prep, so use whichever one you're currently on.

Re-attempt the questions.

Repeat this process on all the remaining passages in the section*.
