Instructions

At the end of the week, have a friend grade all your sections.

Ask your friend to only tell you how many you got wrong in each section.

This means your friend will only tell you that a given section was a -5. They will not tell you anything else about the section.

Go back to each section to find every wrong answer.

The goal is to correct every section to a -0 without using the answer key. 

Send your new answers to your friend to see how much you've improved.

Optional: Repeat this cycle until you get to -0 on each section.
