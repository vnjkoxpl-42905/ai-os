Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

Doing an Anti-PT will allow you to build control over your ability to apply the process when you intend to do so.

We can gain control over ourselves by seeing what happens when we purposefully turn control off.

Essentially, if you can purposefully turn all the rules off, you will start to see them more as a level-up and less of an obligation.

Anti-PTs also have the opportunity to show you how good you really have become.

Plenty of advanced students are surprised by how well they do on an Anti-PT. Their process has become more subconscious than they realize.
