What Problem Are We Solving?

The single biggest problem in LSAT prep is unrealistic planning and timelines. You absolutely can train yourself to massively improve your logic and Translation skills. But ya'll randomly pick test dates that make it impossible to train your skills in the amount of time you have before the test. This leads to panic, LSAT scores that shock and demotivate you, and an altogether toxic relationship with the test.
