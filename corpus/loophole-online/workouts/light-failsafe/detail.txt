Instructions

NOTE: You can do a Light Failsafe with any workout. Failsafes are time-training protocols that you can apply to any workout.

Timing Rules

First Day: Do a stopwatch-timed section* to establish your baseline natural pace.

All Other Days: Adopt the timing pattern that you earned previously. Use a countdown timer for section timing once you have begun to lower your time goal.

Take just enough time to complete the section to the best of your ability.

The pace should feel like an achievable challenge.

This is your chance to push your pacing while still maintaining your accuracy, so it’s time to lock in.

When you hit goal accuracy on two sections in a row, use a countdown timer to reduce your section time by 1-3 minutes on the next section until you hit your time goal.

If you finish before your countdown timer expires, go ahead and end the section. Record the time you took and use that as a new timing baseline to reduce from on the next section.
