Instructions

NOTE: An Analysis Skim is an addition to the RTQ stimulus process. You can seamlessly integrate it into any workout that involves an RTQ.

Read, translate, and quip the stimulus well*.

After you've quipped the last sentence of the stimulus, look at the whole stimulus again to take it apart and see what's really wrong.

This is your Analysis Skim. This is not a moment to reread the stimulus from scratch and fix problems with your Translation.

You must already have a solid memory of your Translation to perform an Analysis Skim.

Ask yourself, "What's wrong with this?" while looking over the whole stimulus at once. This will make your Loophole textually based and provide an opportunity to mark the conclusion.

Making your Loophole textual will enable you to critique the answers using mostly information from the stimulus instead of just information from the answers.
