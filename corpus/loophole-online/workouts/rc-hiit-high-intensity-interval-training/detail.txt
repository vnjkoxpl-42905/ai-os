Instructions

Translate the passage*

RC HIIT Phase 1: Complete the following RC Thunderdome outputs in under 30 seconds each before beginning the questions. These outputs may require looking at the passage.

Outline the structure of the passage overall.

You can use paragraphs as an organizing structure, but you can also delineate on a more fine-grained level, if desired.

Parallel one piece of the passage.

Pick an example, one line of reasoning, or a given person's point of view.

Sub out one word in the passage and replace it with a new word/phrase that means the same thing.

Pick one sentence out of the passage and identify its function.

What is the Main Point?

RC HIIT Phase 2: Complete the following RC Thunderdome outputs in under 30 seconds each. You have two options in how you complete the Phrase 2 outputs: You may complete all the additional RC Thunderdome outputs before beginning the questions or complete two to three additional RC Thunderdome outputs in between each of the questions. These outputs do not require you to look at the passage.

What is the tone of the passage?

Who is the author?

Who is the audience?

Who are the players in the passage and what do they believe?

Strengthen the author's opinion

If the passage feels neutral, write a confirming piece of evidence for the information the author is presenting.

Weaken the author's opinion

If the passage feels neutral, write a disconfirming piece of evidence for the information the author is presenting.

One Inference from the passage

One thing the author is likely to believe

Identify a principle underlying the passage as a whole or an argument within the passage

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

It is not important to look at the questions and answer choices during an RC HIIT.

The point of this workout is not to answer questions, but if you want to combine it with answer-choosing, I'm not fully against it.

Don't attempt to HIIT as a beginner.

If you haven't met the Translation time goal or don't have significant experience with full LR sections, wait to HIIT until you need it.

For the right kind of plateaued, advanced test taker, HIIT offer a singular opportunity for a perspective shift.

HIITs show you where you're weak.

If you find that you always struggle with one of the RC HIIT outputs, review the videos and written material on that topic.

HIITs are great for when you start to run out of unseen PTs near the end of your prep.

HIITs can still be done on previously seen material.

If you are an advanced student PTing at 172+, HIITing can really take your skills to the next level.

Like Translation + CLIR Drills, HIITs are a great spot to use AI voice dictation to speed up the workout. My favorite dictation software is Flow; it's free up to 2000 words per week.
