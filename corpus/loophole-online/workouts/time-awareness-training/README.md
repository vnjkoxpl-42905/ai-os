### Instructions

**NOTE**: Checking the time excessively during the exam is a major cause of accuracy issues. This Workout is designed to help you overcome the need to check the time by enhancing your intuitive understanding of how much time has passed.

1. Look at the clock and note the exact time with seconds.

2. Aim to look at the clock again exactly when your Time Goal has elapsed.

   1. Five minutes is the Level 1 Time Goal, so start here on your first Time Awareness Training.

   2. If you've done Time Awareness Training before, start at the last Time Goal you've earned.
3. When you think your Time Goal has elapsed, look at the clock.

   1. You may only look at the clock once per Time Awareness Training.
4. If you look at the clock within one minute of your Time Goal, progress your Time Goal to the next Level tomorrow.

   1. To progress to the next level of Time Awareness Training, you must check the clock within only one minute of your Time Goal.

   2. If you've checked the time within one minute of the Level 5 Time Goal, congratulations! You've graduated from Time Awareness Training.
5. If you look at the clock farther than one minute from your Time Goal, this round of Time Awareness Training is now over.

   1. Please restart Time Awareness Training with the same Time Goal tomorrow.
