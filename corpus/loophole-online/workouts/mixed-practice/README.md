### Ellen's Advice

#### If You Want to Win

- The point of Mixed Practice is to complete the actions typically associated with a PT without all of the baggage typically associated with a PT.

- Approach Mixed Practice as a real test-day conditions dry run.

- Results of Mixed Practice are just as indicative of test-day performance as a PT.

- Focus on the raw score rather than the scaled score, which should be easy since there is no scaled score.

- For test takers with anxiety, Mixed Practice is a perfect substitute for PTs.

## Get Goal Accuracy under Timed Conditions More Often than Not

### What problem are we solving?

This is probably the most important goal in all of Loophole Online, right? Every Workout, Routine, Strategy Session, and Objective in this whole platform is really just geared to get you here.

If you're getting goal accuracy under timed conditions, nothing else matters. If you're not getting goal accuracy under timed conditions, nothing else matters either, unless it's helping you get to goal time and accuracy.

If you're not getting goal accuracy under timed conditions, you should not sit for an official LSAT administration, unless you are comfortable changing your goal.
