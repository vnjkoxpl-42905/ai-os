Quick Facts
Will This Help Me?

THIS WILL HELP YOU IF

You need to warm up for your exam without spending too much time beforehand and you typically score higher on the first sections you complete in your homework. The Light Warmup can be a good warmup to do in the car right before walking into the test center since it is so short.

SKIP FOR NOW IF

Light Warmups are harmless.

Instructions

Open up one previously-seen LR section and one previously-seen RC section*.

On the first four questions of the LR section, translate, quip, and CLIR the stimuli.

On the first four questions of the RC section, translate and quip the answer choices.

On the next four of the LR section, translate, quip, and CLIR the stimuli.

Start your PT or section immediately.

SECTIONS TO USE

Ideal for Previously Seen Sections

HOW TO TIME

Count-Up Stopwatch

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

Do not check your CLIRs right after the warmup. Checking will distract you from getting into a flow state.
