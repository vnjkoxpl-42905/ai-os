### Quick Facts

#### Will This Help Me?

This will help you if

You need to warm up for your exam without spending too much time beforehand and you typically score higher on the first sections you complete in your homework. The Light Warmup can be a good warmup to do in the car right before walking into the test center since it is so short.

Skip for now if

Light Warmups are harmless.

challenge index Pretty Chill

level Intermediate

minimum time 15+ minutes

timing compatible with Stopwatch

### Instructions

1. Open up one previously-seen LR section and one previously-seen RC section* .

2. On the first four questions of the LR section, translate, quip, and CLIR the stimuli.

3. On the first four questions of the RC section, translate and quip the answer choices.

4. On the next four of the LR section, translate, quip, and CLIR the stimuli.

5. Start your PT or section immediately.

### Ellen's Advice

#### If You Want to Win

Do not check your CLIRs right after the warmup. Checking will distract you from getting into a flow state.

## Practice Occurs Under Full Test Conditions

### What problem are we solving?

You should not take an official LSAT until you have practiced regularly in test conditions. This means you're practicing in a quiet environment, taking exactly the allocated time you'll have on test day (no more no less), and you are not taking breaks to go on your phone or talk to people. Practicing in chaotic, unplanned conditions causes more score chaos than you'd think.
