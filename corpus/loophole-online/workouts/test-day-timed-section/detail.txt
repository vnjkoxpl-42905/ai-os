Will This Help Me?

THIS WILL HELP YOU IF

You have met your time and accuracy goals, and you need to practice for how you'll take the LSAT on test day.

SKIP FOR NOW IF

You have not come close to your time and accuracy goals. The section will be messy if you haven't come close to your time and accuracy goals, and we don't want to waste material.

Instructions

Open an LR or RC section* in Self-Paced Mode in LawHub and set the LawHub timer for the amount of time you will have on test day.

Perform the LR or RC full process silently.

Hyperskip within the time limit.

When time expires, note your answers so you can enter them into Camo.

At least 24 hours later, Camo and Wrong Answer Journal the section.

Ellen's Advice
If You Want to Win

If you have accommodations, your Test-Day Timed Sections may have different protocols than those listed above. Use your accommodations as needed for your own testing experience.

Always use Self-Paced Mode in LawHub.

Do not use Exam Mode, even when you're taking test-conditions sections.

We do not use Exam Mode because it will immediately reveal all the correct answers and score at the end of the section, making it impossible to Camo the section.
