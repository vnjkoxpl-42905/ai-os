# Loophole Online: teach retention, then diagnose the student's actions

Private teacher research from Ellen Cassidy's Loophole Online clips captured 2026-08-24 through 2026-08-30. Publication dates are mostly absent. These are attributed source methods, not a newly approved LSAT U curriculum. The [source summary](../../wiki/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07.md) owns archive provenance and hashes. The [complete per-file review](../../research/obsidian-vault-comparison/2026-10-07/teaching-review.md) links every recovered teaching file, including duplicate and defective captures.

## Make the student's understanding observable

Cassidy's core unit is **read, translate, quip (RTQ)** on a manageable chunk of text. Translation retells all conceptual information in the student's own words while looking away. A quip is a content-specific reaction, example, connection or question followed by an answer. It exposes whether the paraphrase represents understanding or merely recycled words. Preserve qualifiers, dates, lists and scope. The final analytical output is CLIR: controversy, loophole, inference or resolution according to stimulus structure. A final quip can already serve that role. [RTQ source](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/training-camp/translation/Whats Rtq • LO.md>), [translation source](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/training-camp/translation/Translate Prove You Were Listening • LO.md>)

Teacher use: ask the student to retell a chunk and react to its exact content before accepting “I understood it.” Distinguish lost text from a bad inference. An educated vocabulary guess is provisional; ordinary context can help comprehension, but unsupported external facts do not become evidence for an answer.

Cassidy trains this process aloud with continuous recordings before silent use. Her foundation routine adds consecutive sections, vocabulary/common-knowledge reading and weekly marathons; its workload and timing goals are prescriptions from this source, not independently tested promises. [Foundation routine](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/routines/Translation Foundation Routine • LO.md>)

## Choose a drill for the observed failure

| Observed failure | Recovered source method |
|---|---|
| Student forgets details after analyzing the stimulus | Proto-Stimulus Rebound: retell from memory, check missing/added/changed concepts. |
| RC paragraphs disappear as reading continues | Decaf RC: full paragraph translations, then cumulative recaps of all preceding paragraphs. |
| Abstract answer choices remain opaque | Three-column spreadsheet: exact choice, own-words translation, invented stimulus that would make it correct. |
| Student mechanically diagrams before understanding | Personalized if-then paraphrase before diagramming; match both text and diagram to the paraphrase. |
| Unless indicators remain slow or error-prone | Rotate three of four unless statements and diagram fresh, rather than memorizing diagrams. |
| Student judges answers by familiarity or a single keyword | Read and translate the whole choice, then evaluate it against the actual stimulus and question task. |

These methods add concrete practice protocols to the existing [curriculum index](../index.md). Existing [conditional reference](<../Curriculum/Conditional Logic/Notes/_megadoc_reference_conditionals.md>) and [necessary-assumption notes](<../Curriculum/Necessary Assumption/Notes/_megadoc_notes_necessary_assumption.md>) remain their current authorities; no existing lessons were overwritten.

## Review the process without revealing the key first

Camo mixes missed questions with correct decoys and requires a fresh attempt before checking. Its four outcomes distinguish twice-wrong conceptual gaps, wrong-to-right misreads, right-to-wrong self-doubt and twice-right self-confidence. Those labels are diagnostic starting points; they do not establish the exact cause alone. Cassidy generally prescribes at least 24 hours before Camo and doing it after the day's new practice. **Accuracy Failsafe explicitly calls for immediate Camo after a five-minute break**, an exception the Camo workout allows. [Camo workout](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/workouts/Camo Review • LO.md>), [Failsafe workout](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/workouts/Accuracy Failsafe • LO.md>)

The Wrong Answer Journal asks what the student did and what they will change, with brief transferable actions rather than long answer explanations. The Island holds questions still unclear after review for five-minute daily retries. [Journal](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/workouts/Wrong Answer Journal • LO.md>), [Island](<../../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/loophole-online/training-camp/concept-review/How and Why to Use the Island • LO.md>)

## Keep teaching caveats visible

- Some necessary-assumption notes say that if the conclusion is true, the assumption must be true. That conflates a conclusion's truth with the support supplied by this argument. Use the existing argument-survival/negation treatment; retain these source formulations as unresolved, not adopted doctrine.
- The necessary-condition trick generalizes a conditional principle-matching shortcut. Its unconditional rejection language needs adjudication before reuse, including equivalent contraposed formulations.
- The Weaken clip asserts that exactly one answer can weaken at all. Preserve this as Cassidy's claim; do not turn it into a universal selection rule.
- Cognitive mechanisms, speed stories, workload efficacy and historical tool/privacy claims are source assertions, not verified experiments or current product facts.
- The two objection quizzes largely overlap; the chapter transcription has missing questions 1, 2 and 6. It complements the existing [Assumption versus Objection quiz](<../Curriculum/Flaw and Famous Flaws/Homework/Pop Quiz - Assumption vs Objection.md>), rather than replacing it.
