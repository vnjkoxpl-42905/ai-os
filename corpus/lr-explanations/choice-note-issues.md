# Choice Note issues

A running list of problems in existing Choice Notes (the note under each answer choice), found while reading the corpus. Source is the database. Each line: question, choice, the problem. When a whole explanation is bad, it goes on [[revamp-list]] instead; this page is for problems in individual notes. Strike through with a date when fixed.

## Patterns seen across the corpus

- **Verdict-only notes.** Loose explanations average about one note per question that says only "This is irrelevant" or "Out of scope" with no reason. A student learns nothing from it about why.
- **The popular trap gets a thin note.** In loose explanations the choice most students picked is often the one with the shortest note, which is backwards.
- **PT157 and PT158 explicit-Standard notes.** Em-dashes at about ten times the corpus rate, formulaic phrasing, and factual slips. The whole block is on [[revamp-list]].
