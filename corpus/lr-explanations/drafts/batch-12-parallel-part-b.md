## PT102-S4-Q20 | Parallel | C

### Argument Breakdown

**Conclusion:** The claim that apes have language capacity but never use it is ill-considered.

**Evidence:** Language gives an enormous advantage. Having the capacity and never using it would be like having wings fit for flight and never flying.

**Evaluate:** The argument ridicules a claim that a creature has a useful built-in ability that it has never once used. It makes the point by comparing it to an obvious physical ability left unused.

**Goal:** Claim that an ability exists but has never been used is compared to a body part never used for its obvious job.

### Choice Notes

**(A) Bad Premise Match.** Never sleeping and a meat-free lion aren't about an unused ability.

**(B) Bad Premise Match.** Visiting without founding cities isn't an unused capacity.

**(C) Correct.** Telekinetic powers that no human has ever used are compared to an insect with legs that never walks. Same move.

**(D) Bad Premise Match.** Growing tobacco without smoking it is a choice people could easily make, often to sell the crop. It isn't an absurdly unused built-in ability.

**(E) Bad Premise Match.** Not everyone with a car drives it. That is ordinary, not absurd.
