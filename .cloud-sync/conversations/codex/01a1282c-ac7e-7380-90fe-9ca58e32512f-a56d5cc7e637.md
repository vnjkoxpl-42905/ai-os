|---|---|

| **2. Sufficient condition** | Keep. The alternatives distinguish a guarantee from a possibility or the only possible route. |

| **8. Fragile parcel: left side** | Let students place the complete condition on the left. Keep the indicator word separate. |

| **10. Reordered “if” sentence** | Strong question. Make students repair a reversed diagram to show that sentence position does not determine arrow position. |

**The most important addition is Q23’s replacement:**

> Every delivery marked collected has a pickup receipt.  
> This delivery has a pickup receipt.  
> **Must it have been marked collected?**

The answer is **we cannot determine that from this rule**. Students need to demonstrate that having the necessary condition does not establish the sufficient condition.

I would also improve retries. Currently, students see the correct answer and retry the same question. That supports review, but a **fresh parallel example** would better show whether they learned the distinction.
