## assistant

The goal should be: **Use a conditional rule to decide what must be true and what you cannot conclude.**

After learning sufficient conditions, necessary conditions, and the arrow, students should practice:

- **Left side is true:** follow the arrow. The right side must be true.
- **Right side is true:** that alone does not establish the left side.
- **Left side is false:** that alone does not establish whether the right side is true.
- **Only a rule is given:** don’t assume either condition has actually occurred.

The current drill tests these skills through the raffle, market, permission-slip, and café examples.

But there’s a sequencing issue: **it goes beyond identifying the two sides and reading the arrow.** It also tests invalid reversal and invalid negation. We should make sure those ideas have been taught before asking students to apply them.

My recommendation: make this first drill about **identifying the sufficient condition, identifying the necessary condition, and following the arrow when the sufficient condition is true.** Save the other cases for a later drill after teaching them explicitly.
