I recommend this order:

2. **Apply a rule to a fact.** Show the rule, the matching fact, and the conclusion separately.
3. **Link two rules.** Show how the first rule’s result meets the second rule’s condition. Explain that this produces a new rule, without proving that anyone meets its condition.
