  const flag = ACCESS_FLAG[kind];

    .from("profiles")
    .select(flag)

    .single();

    .from("profiles")

    return;

    case 'smartboard_recap':
    case 'announcement':
    case 'note':
      return `/classroom/item/${encodeURIComponent(item.id)}`;
    default:
      return `/classroom/item/${encodeURIComponent(item.id)}`;

what does it mean local storage and compared to?

## assistant

Two places her progress can live:

**Local storage** is a small data store inside her browser, on her device. It's fast and works offline, but it belongs to that one browser on that one machine. If she switches from her laptop to her phone, clears her browser data, or uses an incognito window, whatever was only in local storage is gone or invisible.

**Supabase (the cloud database)** is the app's server-side store, tied to her account rather than her device. Anything written there follows her wherever she logs in.

The Intro to LR bootcamp uses both, in this order:

1. Every action she takes is written to local storage instantly, so the page never waits on the network.
2. About a second later, the same data is pushed to the `bootcamp_kv` table in Supabase under her user id.
3. When she opens the bootcamp on any device, the app pulls her rows from Supabase and merges them into that browser's local copy, with the newest write winning.

So local storage is the cache; Supabase is the real record. The one exception is audio recordings (the "Tell it back" step, if she records), which stay on the device only and are not uploaded.

The part I verified: her account is signed in, so she gets the synced version, and 12 other students already have progress rows in `bootcamp_kv` from this same path. The part I did not verify: an actual click-through as her, since I have no login for her account.
