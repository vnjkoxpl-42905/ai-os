./Curriculum/Sufficient Assumption/Notes/_megadoc_notes_sufficient_assumption.md:63:Sufficient Assumption. QUESTION STEM KEYWORDS. EXAMPLES. Which of the following if assumed would allow the conclusion to follow logically? The conclusion above is properly drawn if which of the following is assumed? READING GOAL. Find the Conclusion. Find the Support. Ask yourself if theres a new term or idea in the conclusion. (If so that idea needs to be in the correct answer.) Ask yourself what the evidence told us about each half of the conclusion. If there is conditional logic mentally or physically diagram it. Link conditionals together where possible. If there isnt conditional logic try to think of the one way that the conclusion wouldnt be true. The correct answer would need to rule out that possibility. PREPHRASE GOAL. Solve for the correct answer ahead of time. Most of the time were pre-phrasing a conditional idea trying to get from this idea to that idea. The correct answer loves to put that connection in contrapositive form. So if were thinking I need to hear that golfing requires money we remind ourselves that it might sound like if you dont have money you cant golf. CORRECT ANSWER. Which answer when added to the evidence guarantees the truth of the conclusion? Correct answers will do one of these things: Provide the missing link that completes a chain of ideas. Provide the missing fact that triggers a conditional. Rule out the one possible way the conclusion could be wrong. Create a situation where whether X is true or X is false the conclusion is true. WRONG ANSWERS. Right Ideas Wrong Order Illegal negations or reversals of the link we were seeking. Lacks Crucial Term Most commonly this means the answer lacks the undefined New Idea in the conclusion so its powerless to prove that New Idea. Too Weak We have to fully prove the conclusion so some or most usually wont cut it. Stimulus Tendencies. Often shorter arguments with obvious conclusion or premise indicator words. Lots of conditional logic is involved in these arguments. Sometimes math-y stuff. COMMON FORMS. Form 1 A is B. B is C. Thus A is Z. Sufficient Assumption: C is Z. Form 2 A is B. B is C. Thus X is C. Sufficient Assumption: X is A. Form 3 A is B. Y is Z. Thus A is Z. Sufficient Assumption: B is Y. Older versions of Sufficient Assumption were pretty consistent in using verbatim words and phrases multiple times to clearly connect the dots. More modern examples might ask us to equate a word and its definition like seeing possessing more money and property as a match for wealthier or cautiously weighing the evidence before making a decision as a match for prudent. EXAMPLES. Bridge Idea If Premise then Conclusion. Humans are amazing. After all humans invented roller coasters. (SA: invented RCs then Amazing). It is well known that Teslas are the most fuel efficient sports car available. Thus the best sports car available is a Tesla. (SA: most fuel efficient then best). Missing Fact. Only people well-versed with Game of Thrones are invited to Tommys party on Sunday. Thus Shelly must not be invited. (SA: Shelly doesnt know GoT well). If it rains on Thursday then I wont be able to play basketball which was going to be my only exercise this week. Getting at least some exercise every week was my New Years Resolution. So evidently Im not going to be able to keep my New Years Resolution. (SA: its gonna rain on Thursday). Either Carrie or Brad must get coffee for any staff meeting. Since there is a staff meeting tomorrow morning Brad will have to get coffee tomorrow morning. (SA: Carrie cant get the coffee tomorrow). CONDITIONAL CHAINS. Clowns should be excommunicated for scary things are a nuisance and all clowns are scary. (SA: nuisance then should be excommunicated). Buying something impractical requires a firm commitment to having fun. Thus buying a hoverboard is evidence that one is a child at heart since people who are firmly committed to having fun are still children at heart. (SA: hoverboard then impractical). UNIVERSALIZE THE EVIDENCE. We shouldnt accuse Harry of being ungrateful since Thomas behaved in a similar fashion and we didnt accuse Thomas of being ungrateful. (SA: we should judge all cases of ungrateful the same way). Hakeem is a lawyer and he is very verbose. Hence it must be that all lawyers are verbose.
