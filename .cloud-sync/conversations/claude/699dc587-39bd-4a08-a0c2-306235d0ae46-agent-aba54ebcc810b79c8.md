122	- **Sufficient Assumption (193):** Conclusion > Evidence > Evaluate > Goal (53%); Evidence > Conclusion > Evaluate (21%). Goal usually diagrams the missing link as the logic path.

17	## What separates a top-tier explanation from a loose one
18	
19	Length does not. These do:
20	
21	1. **The Evaluate step argues with the argument** ("How could we argue the opposite?") instead of restating it.
22	2. **The Goal names the answer** it expects, not just the task.
23	3. **Every wrong answer gets a reason and usually a test.** Loose ones average one verdict-only note per question ("This is irrelevant").
24	4. **The most popular trap gets the most attention.**
25	5. **The correct-answer note teaches the move**, so a student can repeat it on the next question, not just confirm the key.
