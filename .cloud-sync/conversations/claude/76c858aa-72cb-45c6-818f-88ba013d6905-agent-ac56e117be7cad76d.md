1	# Booking — Context
2	
3	The language of paying for and scheduling tutoring time. Covers the student booking surface (`/schedule`, `/booking`), the public booking microsite (`/book/:slug`), and the Single Session page.
4	
5	## Terms
6	
7	### Single Session
8	One tutoring session paid for individually ($45 for the 70-minute session) rather than out of a Package, booked from a public shareable link by anyone — prospect or current student — without logging in. Needs no Booking Pass. Replaces the Cal.com "LSAT Session" (`session-link`) event, which stays live in parallel until Joshua retires it. _Avoid (per the root glossary): one-off, ad hoc session, hourly._
9	
10	### Package
11	Five or ten hours of tutoring bought upfront through Stripe, consumed by booking sessions out of a Booking Pass.
12	
13	### Booking Pass
14	The prepaid entitlement a student holds when sessions are **owed** to them, carrying the count owed and the count used, reachable at its own private high-entropy booking link. Granted by Joshua or by a matched package payment. A Single Session never creates or consumes one. _Avoid: booking link (that is the pass's URL, not the thing), wallet, credits-as-product._
15	
16	### Booker
17	The person on the public page picking a slot. Not necessarily an authenticated user or an existing student; identified by the name and email they enter.
18	
19	### Slot
20	A concrete start time offered on the calendar. For the Single Session, slots run at a 70-minute pitch from the start of each day's availability window, within the booking window, respecting minimum notice and calendar conflicts.
21	
22	### Hold
23	A short-lived claim on a Slot created when a Booker submits their details and is sent to payment. While live it blocks the Slot for everyone else; it converts to a booking when payment succeeds and evaporates if payment doesn't arrive in time.
24	
25	### Booking Window
26	How far into the future slots are offered. The Single Session mirrors Cal.com: 6 business days out, non-rolling.
27	
28	### Minimum Notice
29	How close to now a Slot may start. The Single Session requires 24 hours.
