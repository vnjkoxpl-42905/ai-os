## The arc every follow-up follows

1. **Greeting** — "Hi {{first_name}},"
2. **Warm recap** — it was great meeting you + affirm them ("I could tell right away you're ambitious and taking this seriously" OR "I hope the consultation was helpful and you took something useful away").
3. **Breathing-room line** — you gave them a couple days to think before following up.
4. **"Here are the next steps:"** — the booking/payment options.
5. **The form** — registration/application so you can tailor the plan.
6. **Reply-back close** — once they book and submit, reply and you'll send onboarding "so we can start strong" / "smooth from day one."
7. **Sign-off.**

## Rules for the drafter (human or automated)

- Pick the variant to match the person: A for most warm conversions, B when you want a lighter touch, C when cost/comparison is on their mind.
- Always keep the "gave you a little time to think" line. It's part of your voice and sets the tone.
- Mirror one real detail from the consultation if you have it (goal, timeline, score, what they struggle with).
- Reply inside the existing consultation thread; don't start a new one.
- Prose plus the short labeled option/step list only. No em dashes. Keep under ~150 words.
- Double-check the correct links are pasted (session vs consultation vs Stripe) before sending.
- If they've gone quiet, a gentle check-in works ("just checking in to say hello and let you know I'm still really looking forward to our sessions").
