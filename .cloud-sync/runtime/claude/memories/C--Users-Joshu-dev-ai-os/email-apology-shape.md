Approved and sent on 2026-09-11 (Sandra booking-error reply, three drafts). The sent text is quoted in the voice file as the reference. Shape: apology first ("I'm sorry about this. The error was on my end, not yours."), then their position (nothing used, nothing booked), one-sentence cause + "That is fixed now", the link, "If you run into any other issues, let me know," and close "Once again, my apologies. / Best, / Joshua".

**Why:** He edited my first draft line by line: the apology was buried at the end as "Sorry for the runaround," I promised "a calendar invite with the Google Meet link right after," I restated the pass numbers, and I left out the offer to add the time by hand. "To confirm:" framing for the answer to their question was his add.
**How to apply:** Read the "When something on his side broke" section of `wiki/teaching-voice.md` before drafting any make-good email, and add each new correction there as it happens. See [[no-em-dashes-no-filler-in-joshuas-writing]] and [[booking-emails-omit-pass-settings]].
