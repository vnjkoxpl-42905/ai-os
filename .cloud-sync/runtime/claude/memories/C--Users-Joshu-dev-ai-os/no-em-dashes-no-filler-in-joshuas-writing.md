Two hard rules for anything written under Joshua's name, including short student emails:

1. **No em dashes.** Not "pile-ups" — none at all. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
2. **No motivational filler or cliche.** Lines like "Keep at them, that's the point" or
   "trust the process" read as AI-generated and get cut. Say the concrete thing or say
   nothing.

**Why:** He flagged both in a student email draft on 2026-09-08. The teaching brand
depends on sounding like a person who actually knows the material, and stock
encouragement signals the opposite.

**How to apply:** After drafting anything student-facing, scan for em dashes and for any
sentence that would survive unchanged in a different email about a different topic. Cut
both. See [[lsatu-copy-corrections-log]] and the global writing rules in CLAUDE.md.
