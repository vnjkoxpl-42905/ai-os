When writing or editing LSAT lesson copy, say what the thing is in plain words first, then show it. Cut sophistry: no "a rule makes a promise", no "the left side depends on the right", no "nothing to stand on", no vague "the other one". Keep it natural, intuitive, personable, in his teaching voice.

**Why:** On 2026-10-11 Joshua rejected the first contrapositive lesson as "terribly done", then rejected the rewrite for "a lot of sophistry". He wants the contrapositive taught as the logical equivalent: the same rule said a different way, interchangeable, equally valid. His own session transcripts say exactly that.

**How to apply:**
- Read `wiki/contrapositive-teaching.md` in the vault before any contrapositive work. It holds his own wording and the mistakes he warns about.
- Order for a concept lesson: what it is, see it work, how to do it, what it is not, the steps in short, why it matters.
- Every time he corrects wording, add it to `wiki/COPY-CORRECTIONS.md` the same turn, with the reason. Read that file before writing new notes. This is how the same mistake is not repeated.
- Title lessons by topic ("Contrapositive"). Card fronts ask a specific question and the back gives the answer first. "Correct", not "Right".
- Related: [[no-em-dashes-no-filler-in-joshuas-writing]], [[lsat-teaching-voice-standard]], [[no-internal-labels-in-student-lessons]].
- Never say the contrapositive is "the one other thing" a rule tells you (his 2026-10-11 correction). Say equivalent and interchangeable: know one, you know the other.
- Do not use the raffle/bicycle as the example in new contrapositive or arrow material. Use a fresh everyday example (current contrapositive note: elevator moving, doors closed).
- Contrapositive lesson: no thought experiment or guess card (it confused him). Follow the source HTML explanation directly in close language, definition first, equivalence stated, "left side / right side of the arrow". Never mention Logic Games. Never reuse the source coffee or breathing/air examples.
- Rules say "the power MUST be on". Avoid the word "foreign". Voice = his Intro to LR bootcamp: plain claim, short stacked landings, "Here's the thing", dry wit. A cheeky exclamation on the one key word is OK in the contrapositive lesson, with a one-time punch-in and glow; motion must carry the reasoning and respect reduced motion.
- NEVER show negation as a squiggle (~) beside an idea, anywhere, including answer choices and the reference practice. Use the word "not" or a diagonal strike from the idea's top-left to bottom-right corner (component Neg / Notation in ContrapositiveVisuals.tsx; the ~ is gone from the conditionals module as of 2026-10-12). Write the contrapositive as a plain sentence early.
- Anything that is wrong must LOOK wrong: red card, X badge, the word "Wrong" (reverse and inverse cards). Look-alike wrong answers and the right one never share a table. No repeated content; high-end, sleek visuals for anything interactive.
