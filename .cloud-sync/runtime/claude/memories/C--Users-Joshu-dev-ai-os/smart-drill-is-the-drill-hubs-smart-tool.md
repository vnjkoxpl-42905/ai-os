On the LSAT U drill hub, the smart/adaptive feature is named **Smart Drill**. Use that
exact visible label in both LR and RC. Do not hide it behind task-oriented euphemisms such
as "Practice your weakest types" or "Read one passage."

The "Welcome back, Joshua" hero is for choosing how to drill. Assigned work must not take
over that hero or demote Smart Drill to an easy-to-miss text link. Put assigned work in the
lower Today/assigned area, one of the two bays below the hero, where it remains prominent
without confusing the page's primary tool.

LR Smart Drill uses the adaptive question engine. RC Smart Drill uses adaptive passage
selection. They share the same product name while keeping their section-specific behavior.
