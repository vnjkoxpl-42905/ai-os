# Consultation Inquiry Reply Template

Use this when someone new emails inquiring about LSAT tutoring. Goal: reply warmly in Joshua's voice and get them to book the free consultation. Follow voice-principles.md. Keep it short, prose only, no bullets in the email itself.

**Links**
- Consultation (default): https://cal.com/joshua-jtx5ud/consultation
- Single session: https://cal.com/joshua-jtx5ud/session
- Rate: $40/hour

---

## Master structure (the arc every reply follows)

1. **Greeting** — "Hi {{first_name}},"
2. **Acknowledge + thank** — warm open that reacts to their message.
3. **Mirror their situation** — reflect back their specific detail (score, timeline, plateau, referral, struggle) so it reads personal, not canned.
4. **Fit framing** — one line offering to talk and see if you're a good fit. Add rate only if they asked.
5. **The ask (link)** — book the free consultation as the clear next step.
6. **What the call covers** — one sentence so the call feels low-pressure and useful.
7. **Fallback** — invite them to just reply if scheduling breaks.
8. **Sign-off** — "Best,\nJoshua"

## Slot options (pull from these, matched to voice)

**{{acknowledgment}}** — pick one:
- "Thanks for reaching out."
- "Thanks for reaching out, and it's great to hear from you."
- "It's a pleasure to meet you, and thanks for reaching out about LSAT tutoring."
- (Delayed reply) "Thanks for reaching out, and my apologies for the delay in getting back to you."

**{{situation_mirror}}** — react to their actual detail:
- Referral: "It's great that {{referrer}} pointed you my way."
- Timeline: "A {{month}} test date gives us a solid window to build a clear plan."
- Score/diagnostic: "Going from a {{start_score}} to where you're aiming is real progress, and the ability is clearly there."
- Plateau: "Hitting a plateau on {{platform}} is really common, and the good news is it's very fixable once you have a more structured approach."
- Specific struggle (timing, rereading, nerves): "What you're describing with {{struggle}} is super common, and it's very fixable with the right approach."
- Vague "more info": "I'd be glad to share more about how I work and learn a bit about what you're aiming for."

**{{rate_line}}** — only if they asked about cost:
- " My rate is $40 per hour."

## Rules for the drafter (human or automated)

- Always mirror at least one specific detail from their email. If none exists, use the "more info" line. Never send the bare skeleton.
- Only include the rate line if they asked about cost.
- Open with the apology line only if the reply is more than ~24 hours after their email.
- Consultation link is the default. Use the session link only for someone already past the consult stage.
- Keep it under ~120 words. Prose, no bullets, no em dashes.
- Match their formality: "Hi" default, "Hey" only if they were clearly casual.
- If a thread already exists with this person, reply in it rather than starting new.
