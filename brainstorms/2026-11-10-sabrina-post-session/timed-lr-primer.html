Before you start · Timed Logical Reasoning section

    Give Each Question a Job

    In a timed Logical Reasoning section, pick the best answer to each question. On your first pass, take the points you can get and flag questions you want to come back to. Use your remaining time for the flagged questions.

    Pick your target before the clock starts

    Choose when you want to finish question ten. Try 15 minutes as a starting point. Write it down. When you review, count how many answers were correct under time and how many changed to correct after time. Record how far you got before going back to any flags.

    Know what you're looking for

    Name what the stem wants before you read the choices. For a necessary assumption, look for something the argument needs. For weaken, look for a choice that makes the conclusion harder to believe given the evidence.

    When you miss, find the first point where you got stuck. If you were rereading two choices, explain what each choice did to the argument. If you used up the time you wanted for later questions, practise flagging sooner on the next section.
