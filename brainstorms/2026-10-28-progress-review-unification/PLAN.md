## Goal and decision

Make the cycle from seeing a pattern to reviewing the relevant work obvious, fast and useful, without mixing incompatible metrics or placing two full dashboards on one screen.

Recommended product structure: one sidebar destination named Review, with three route-backed views: To review, Progress, History. Put question-type exploration inside Progress through the existing all-type selector/master-detail pattern. Keep Wrong-answer journal and Flagged management reachable as contextual utility links and legacy subviews; they are capabilities, not merely aliases for queue filters. Preserve their edits and source-specific behavior. Keep existing teacher dossier routes and selected-student ownership separate from the signed-in person's hub.

Alternatives to challenge: (A) retain two sidebar destinations but add exact contextual handoffs; lowest migration risk, still forces a conceptual split. (B) one Review workspace with distinct views; recommended balance. (C) merge analytics, queue and every filter into one long page; rejected because it hides the primary task and conflates populations. Claude should challenge B if evidence favors A or a simpler variant.

## What must remain different

Progress reserves the original first graded answer per canonical question, with only released first outcomes entering performance statistics; repeats remain history. Review is an actionable collection across ten source families, including repeated sittings, flags on correct/unanswered questions, visited blanks, authored activities and teacher-selected exact submissions. Review rows may represent the same question in different sittings. Completing a supported task, clearing a flag and answering correctly are independent actions. Never call queue count an accuracy denominator or unique-question total. Never rewrite historical first-answer identity or outcome when review completes. Revealing a formerly withheld result may make that same original answer eligible for released-outcome statistics; it must never promote a later repeat to first or rewrite the saved answer. Never create universal Mark reviewed: current exact completion is bounded to supported native-lab/Hard Sentences saved submissions.

Permissions stay at child/source level: To review is signed-in; wrong-answer journal has_journal_access; flagged management has_flagged_access; Progress/History/type evidence has_analytics_access. The combined hub must not require analytics to access review work. Preserve unresolved/loading/denied states, canonical owner resolution, account switches, selected-student scope and all existing source/player guards. A link does not grant access.
