# Smart Drill Hub: Brainstorm / Discovery Notes
Date: 2026-09-13 · Goal: Define the LR and RC drill hub so Smart Drill is unmistakable and assigned work has a clear, separate home.

## Summary / key decisions

- Smart Drill must be present on the main drill hub and named exactly **Smart Drill**.
- The existing hero makes assigned work compete with and obscure the smart drilling feature.
- Audit recommendation awaiting confirmation: keep the hero focused on Smart Drill and manual drill building; move assigned work into the lower assigned-work area.

## Q&A log

### Q1: What permanently owns the hero?

- Decision: the hero always presents Smart Drill and Build a Drill.
- Assigned work moves entirely into the lower-right assigned-work area.
- Assigned work never replaces either drilling action.

### Q2: What should the hero headline say?

- Superseded decision: remove "Welcome back, Joshua" from the hero.
- The left side becomes a dedicated Smart Drill area with a button labeled exactly "Smart Drill."
- Approved starting copy: "Smart Drill chooses what you should practice next based on your work."
- Final approved copy: "Let Smart Drill choose what would be most useful to practice next."
- Use the same supporting sentence in LR and RC. The section toggle supplies the material context.

## Corrections from Joshua

- Reject the mechanical RC wording. Do not say "fresh passage near your level based on your work."
- The hero should sound natural and personable, not explain the adaptive selection algorithm.
- Prefer language about what would be useful to practice next.

### Q1: What permanently owns the hero?

**Decision:** The hero permanently presents the two drilling choices:

1. **Smart Drill**, where LSAT U chooses what the student should practice.
2. **Build a Drill**, where the student chooses the content.

Assigned work never replaces either action. It moves entirely into the lower-right
assigned-work area, with the next assignment first and the remaining assignments below.

Joshua confirmed the recommendation without changes on 2026-09-13.

### Q5: What belongs in the lower-right panel?

- Decision: rename the lower-right panel "Assigned work."
- It contains only actual assignments, with the next assignment first.
- Messages remain in Messages.
- Unfinished personal drills move to the lower-left practice area.
- General notifications remain notifications and do not appear as assigned work.
- Joshua confirmed this separation on 2026-09-13.

### Q6: What does Smart Drill launch?

- Decision: Smart Drill starts immediately without a setup screen.
- LR keeps the continuous adaptive run that serves one question at a time.
- RC keeps the adaptive one-passage launch: untimed, with answers after the passage.
- Manual configuration belongs exclusively to Build a Drill.
- Joshua confirmed the recommendation on 2026-09-13.

### Q7: How is the manual option named?

- Superseded in part: do not force one shared heading if it weakens the current section-specific builder language.
- Keep the builder's section-specific wording, including "Build the reading set you need" in RC.
- The builder remains the student-controlled alternative to Smart Drill.

### Q8: How much assigned work appears on the hub?

- Decision: show the next three assignments, ordered by priority and recency.
- When more exist, show "View all assigned work."
- Empty-state copy: "Nothing assigned right now."
- Do not backfill the empty state with recommendations or invented activity.
- Joshua confirmed the recommendation on 2026-09-13.

### Q9: Where does unfinished personal practice appear?

- Decision: show one "Resume your drill" row at the top of the lower-left practice area.
- Show it only when the unfinished drill matches the currently selected LR or RC mode.
- Keep the selected section's insights and history beneath it.
- Joshua confirmed the recommendation on 2026-09-13.

### Q10: Relative visual weight of Smart Drill and the builder

- Preserve the existing two-part hero division and its general balance.
- Replace the left welcome/assignment area with a dedicated Smart Drill area.
- Keep the right manual-builder concept and the existing RC language "Build the reading set you need."
- Smart Drill must be unmistakable, but it should not visually dominate the builder.
- Joshua corrected the earlier recommendation to make Smart Drill the visually dominant action.

### Q11: Right-side builder headings

- Decision: keep the existing section-specific headings.
- LR: "Build the drill you need."
- RC: "Build the reading set you need."
- The left side is "Smart Drill" in both modes, with roughly equal visual weight between the two halves.
- Joshua confirmed the recommendation on 2026-09-13.

### Q12: Right-side builder button labels

- Decision: keep "Set up an LR drill" and "Set up an RC drill."
- "Set up" signals that configuration follows; "Smart Drill" starts immediately.
- Joshua confirmed the recommendation on 2026-09-13.

### Q13: Mobile stacking order

- Decision: stack Smart Drill first, the section-specific manual builder second, the practice/resume area third, and Assigned work fourth.
- Keep the two drilling choices together so assigned work never separates them.
- Joshua confirmed the recommendation on 2026-09-13.
