# Negation Station

**30 sentences. Write the negation of each one. Do the whole set more than once.**

This comes first, before any necessary assumption questions. If you second-guess a negation in the middle of a timed section, you'll drop the technique and go back to guessing. So you practise it until it's automatic.

The rule from our session: **a negation is the weakest claim that contradicts the original.** It isn't the opposite. *All babies are cute* becomes *not all babies are cute*, not *no babies are cute.*

The trap this drill catches is **flipping instead of negating.** That's the contrapositive. Negation never flips anything. You put a "not" on the whole idea and stop there.

One habit: before you type, ask what's the smallest thing that would make the original sentence false. Write that.
