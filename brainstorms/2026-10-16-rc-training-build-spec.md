## Outcomes and invariant teaching procedure

One RC Training activity and one current workout. Read/translate a whole paragraph, hide all text and silently recall that newest paragraph, reveal and briefly confirm all text read so far, then continue. Preserve details, qualifications, relationships and brief reactions while reading. No sentence-by-sentence hidden recall, mandatory cumulative hidden retelling, grading or forced cutoff. Optional whole-passage recall at passage end can be silent or spoken; only spoken recap records audio. Comparative A/B confirmation choice remains supported and accurately labelled. Untimed with optional elapsed display; passage time resets, section time continues. Legacy attempts retain their actual procedure; do not relabel all historical records indiscriminately.
