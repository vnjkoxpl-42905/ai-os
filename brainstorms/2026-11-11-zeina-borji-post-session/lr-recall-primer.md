# Before Your LR Recall Practice

Read the stimulus while it is on screen. When the text disappears, put what you read into your own words. Do a couple of sections this way.

Start with two things you can check. Did you keep the author's claim? Did you keep the reason the author gave? When the stimulus is available again, check both against the wording.

## Keep the detail that changes the argument

If a rule says a grant goes only to shops that filed a report, keep the word **only** in your rephrase. “Shops file reports to get grants” loses the requirement. Try “A shop has to file its report to qualify.”

## After a miss, name the part you lost

Look for the claim, the reason, or a word that limits either one. Say the sentence again with that detail back in it. On the next stimulus, say the claim and the reason before you go to the choices.
