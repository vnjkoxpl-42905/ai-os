# Classroom LR rating placement
Date 2026-11-17 · Goal is to agree on the placement, pulse, and click behavior of the classroom LR rating.

## Summary / key decisions

- The LR rating is static display text centered above the orb, within the dark globe panel.
- The globe itself starts LR Smart Drill. A faint lightning bolt inside the globe becomes more visible on hover.
- The globe pulses gently at intervals while it is in view. The rating stays still.
- Preserve the black globe and silver rim.
