# Missing Links: First Taste

**Monday, 2026-10-26: six questions, available and due Monday. Do this drill before the full untimed PrepTest 107 on Tuesday, 2026-10-27.**

This set mixes Sufficient Assumption, Necessary Assumption and Principle Strengthen questions. Read the question stem before deciding what the missing link must do. Work for a clear explanation before speed.

## Pick the test first

- **Sufficient Assumption:** add the answer as a premise. Together with the evidence, it must prove the conclusion.
- **Necessary Assumption:** ask whether the reasoning requires the answer. It can be required without being enough to prove the conclusion.
- **Principle Strengthen:** apply the stated principle task and look for support that improves the argument. Support is not automatically a guarantee.

The routine below is for the sufficient-assumption items. Do not force it onto every question in the set.

## On a sufficient-assumption item

1. Find the exact conclusion. Write its naming part and telling part in plain words.
2. List what the evidence actually gives you.
3. Say what connection is missing. Are you looking for a rule connecting the evidence to the result, or a fact that meets a rule already given?
4. Add an answer you are considering as another premise. The original evidence plus the answer must be enough for the conclusion.

## The trap

A rule with the right words can point the wrong way. If your evidence says Mina completed training and your conclusion says Mina may enter the lab, you need **completed training → may enter the lab**. The reverse only tells you what entrants have done.

## One habit

After choosing, read the entire argument with your answer added. Explain why the conclusion has to follow. If you can still describe a case where all of it is true but the conclusion is false, keep looking.
