# Drill 1: question-by-question audit

Scope: read-only review requested by Joshua, 2026-10-11. Current24 questions and option feedback inspected; current browser UI observed. No drill changes made. Recommendations below are proposals, not approved design requirements.

## Main recommendation

Keep the foundational coverage and vary the work students perform. The current drill primarily tests recognition among prepared answers. Use four consistent interaction patterns: select a complete clause or indicator; place or repair an arrow; evaluate what a rule and fact establish; construct a possible case. Support each with discrete keyboard-operable controls rather than drag-only interaction. Preserve simple multiple choice where it serves the concept.

## Sequence and scoring

Use a progression from meaning to placement to indicators to application to challenge. Keep selected paired teaching examples, then space transfer checks apart. Do not mechanically remove helpful repetition. Maintain first-attempt practice score without equating it to mastery. Indicator coverage here can remain a foundation for the dedicated lessons that follow; adding every later expression is not required to make this drill intensive.
