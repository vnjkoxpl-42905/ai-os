## Corrections required before adapting observed teaching

The parent identified three accuracy hazards in subsequently inspected activities. These are original logical checks, not source quotations:

- Negating more than half means **at most half**, including exactly 50%; restricting the negation to 49% is incorrect.
- A normative conclusion about what should be done need not be the consequent in every conditional. Separate deontic content from arrow direction rather than impose a universal normative-right/empirical-left shortcut.
- Naming a complementary category is not a general negation method. The complement must cover every possibility excluded by the original claim; scope, compounds, and quantifiers still require their own treatment.

Preserve these issues as explicit adaptation checks. Familiar source authority does not make an oversimplified rule suitable for LSAT U.

## Original LSAT U practice proposals

Use one shared rule set to isolate different operations: every certified guide is insured; every insured guide has a permit.

- Translate each rule before combining them.
- Derive certified → permit without assuming any certified guide exists.
- Add the fact that Mira is certified; derive Mira's insurance and permit.
- Add instead that Mira lacks a permit; use contraposition to infer she is not certified.
- Ask whether two rules ending in insured can be connected; require explanation of why a shared destination supplies no bridge.

These are original example proposals. Beginner work can isolate one operation; Medium can require choosing the operation; Advanced can vary language and compound structure. Timing remains optional for Medium and Advanced. Quantifiers belong in the following phase under Joshua's requirement, with their own assignable skills.
