## What an existing archived lesson demonstrates

[Must Be True, video 33013](https://app.lsatlab.com/video-library/?videoid=33013), Patrick Tyrrell, 2026-08-27, 97 minutes. Evidence comes from the existing local archive and its coverage analysis, not fresh playback. Summary: the opening separates contraposition, connecting rules, and applying a fact. It contrasts valid bridges with rules that share only a result, uses contraposition to reveal hidden links, addresses illegal reversal and negation, and distinguishes guaranteed consequences from frequency claims. Five worked LSAT applications explore requirements, compounds, subject scope, passive wording, and answer strength. Diagramming is presented as useful when it resolves complexity. This establishes substantive conditional transfer within an inference lesson, not a complete standalone conditional curriculum.

## Teaching progression we can infer, with limits

The library places conditional reasoning among reusable argument patterns, then organizes applications by question family. The archived MBT class demonstrates movement from explicit operations to worked LSAT applications. A useful LSAT U progression is therefore meaning and translation → valid transformations → rule linking versus fact-triggering → application. This is an LSAT U design inference, not a verified timestamp-by-timestamp outline of LSAT Lab's Conditional Logic lesson.

Training evidence is strongest for instructor explanations, contrasting valid/invalid moves, repetition as a fluency goal, and worked LSAT questions. Number of repetitions, student answer interface, automated feedback, difficulty progression, mastery gates, and independent reassessment were not verified in this pass. A video library is not itself evidence of a complete independently guided pathway.

## Original LSAT U practice proposals

Use one shared rule set to isolate different operations: every certified guide is insured; every insured guide has a permit.

- Translate each rule before combining them.
- Derive certified → permit without assuming any certified guide exists.
- Add the fact that Mira is certified; derive Mira's insurance and permit.
- Add instead that Mira lacks a permit; use contraposition to infer she is not certified.
- Ask whether two rules ending in insured can be connected; require explanation of why a shared destination supplies no bridge.

These are original example proposals. Beginner work can isolate one operation; Medium can require choosing the operation; Advanced can vary language and compound structure. Timing remains optional for Medium and Advanced. Quantifiers belong in the following phase under Joshua's requirement, with their own assignable skills.
