## What these sets actually teach

Intro Part 1 asks students to highlight the indicator and underline both complete conditions. Part 2 asks them to diagram the same statements and write contrapositives. The key additionally provides group labels, English translations, symbolic translations, and contrapositives. The repeated scenarios support scaffolded practice but should not be counted as fresh transfer questions.

Translation asks for forward diagrams and contrapositives for 35 statements. It expands sentence order, negative conditions, quantifiers, context, compound predicates, and unusual subject matter. It is not a foundations-only quiz: contrapositive knowledge is required throughout. Some key explanations are wrong, others remove qualifications or modality.

## Integration recommendation

Use these as a provenance-linked exercise bank, not two more long lesson pages. The strongest contribution is practice applying one operation repeatedly to differently worded sentences.

1. **After sufficient/necessary lessons and must arrow:** a short selection from intro1,3,11,12,15 and translation1,12,13,15,18. Ask students to select the complete left/right condition and then place the arrow. Do not require a contrapositive before its lesson.
2. **Find the complete condition:** intro1/5/8/11/13 (corrected keys), translation9/24/30/33 (corrected). Have students select spans or repair a truncated diagram. These directly justify the page's distinct job; no need for another indicator list.
3. **Group1/Group2 flashcards:** reuse individual expressions with source-linked complete examples, not verbatim flawed keys. Translation10 versus19 is a useful the-only/only contrast after explanation is fixed;16 tests a non-conditional occurrence of where.
4. **Contrapositive drill later:** intro originals once cleaned, translation21/23/24 for negatives. Include explicit negation grouping before25/27/35.
5. **Advanced interpretation:** translation7/11/19 distinguish assuming a rule from agreeing with it;29/33 teach modality/timing;31 withheld pending clarification. Intro2/4/9 are better as edited content or deliberate critique exercises, not auto-graded clean rules.

For each derived app item record original file/hash/page/item, exact prompt, pedagogical stage, intended response, complete condition spans, forward relation, contraposition only if taught, corrected-key rationale, and status (approved/needs review). Separate source transcription from corrected derivative. Never silently overwrite the original key.
