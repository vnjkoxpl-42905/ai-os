## Original LSAT U examples, not provider material

- **Qualifying details:** A grant above $2,000 awarded to a new applicant requires committee review. Keep amount and applicant status together when translating. An experienced applicant's award cannot be classified from that rule alone.
- **Obligation versus action:** A damaged machine must be inspected. An undamaged machine might still be inspected. Being outside the trigger proves neither absence of inspection nor absence of another inspection requirement.
- **Universal requirement:** A certified fleet requires every vehicle to pass inspection. One failed vehicle proves the fleet is not certified. This combines quantifier negation with contraposition, so it belongs after Joshua's quantifier phase introduces those operations.
- **Compound principle:** A valid submission requires both a signature and a date. Missing either establishes invalidity. Having both does not itself establish validity.
- **Chain with uncertainty:** Enrollment guarantees account creation; account creation guarantees an identifier. Enrollment probably improves attendance. Enrollment therefore guarantees an identifier, while improved attendance remains uncertain.
