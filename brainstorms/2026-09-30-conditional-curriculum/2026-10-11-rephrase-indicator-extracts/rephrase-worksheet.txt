Quick Guide (G1/G2 Only)
G1 (sufficient): if, when, where, all, every, any, the only, anyone, whenever
G2 (necessary): only, only if, only when, only where, always, requires, must, only those
Task: translate to if/then, give the contrapositive, then write the indicator word and check Sufficient or Necessary.
