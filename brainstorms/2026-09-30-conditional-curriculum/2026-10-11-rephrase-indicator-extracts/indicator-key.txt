Part 3  Original · Contrapositive · If-Then
(arrow always left → right; slash shows negation)
1. All interns who complete the program are offered full-time positions.
Original: Complete the program → Be offered full-time positions.
Contrapositive: /Be offered full-time positions → /Complete the program
If-then: If interns complete the program, then they are offered full-time positions.
Contra: If interns are not offered full-time positions, then they did not complete the
program.
