## Preserve the useful distinctions and adjudicate source shortcuts

The inspected Other Conjunction Indicators page treats but, even if and even though as AND. Do not copy that as a universal semantic rewrite. Even if does not establish that its antecedent is true; even though usually presents that clause as a fact. Any LSAT U treatment should distinguish the context-specific logical relationship rather than make a blanket connective substitution.

The domain simplification also needs an explicit boundary: the rule is applicable only after its domain condition is established. Likewise, quantified all-to-most-to-some shorthand requires the relevant existence assumption, addressed in [the quantity analysis](research-7sage-quantifiers.md). These are original analytical cautions, not claims that a title alone proves the source wrong.

- **Skill:** the operation the student learns, based on inspected teaching rather than title alone.
- **Prerequisites:** explicitly stated prerequisites separately from inferred dependencies.
- **Instruction:** what reasoning is explained, the distinction it makes and any error it rules out.
- **Training mechanism:** demonstration, worksheet, classification, derivation, guided attempt or independent check.
- **Practice and feedback:** visible task count, what the answer reveal explains, and whether both diagram and contrapositive appear.
- **Transition:** how the resource prepares for the next resource; label inference when the platform does not state it.
- **Evidence:** text read, answer reveal inspected, video-only/unread audiovisual, or title/order metadata only.

## A subsection should be a complete learning unit

Proposed independently assignable subsection structure: a short explanation of one skill, one worked original example, a guided attempt, fresh independent practice, immediate move-specific feedback, and an exit check. The teacher should be able to assign the whole stage or only the failed skill. The student should see why that subsection is next and what would count as readiness to progress.

Existing whole-resource assignments, browser-local refresher progress, saved hub practice and General indicator ledgers are different mechanisms. A unified curriculum needs to connect them; it should not silently claim they already form an adaptive mastery pathway.
