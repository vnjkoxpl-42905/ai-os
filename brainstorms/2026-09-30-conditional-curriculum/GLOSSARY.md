# Conditional Curriculum

The learning pathway for conditional reasoning, from complete beginner through expert, supporting independent study and targeted teacher assignment.

## Language

**Curriculum**:
The complete learning pathway covering the skills needed to understand and apply conditional reasoning, from beginner through expert.
_Avoid_: Bootcamp as a synonym for the whole curriculum

**Subsection**:
An independently assignable part of the curriculum. A subsection may cover an area or a specific skill.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level as a synonym for a subsection

**Area**:
A broad part of conditional reasoning that contains several related skills, such as complex conditional linking.
_Avoid_: Level as a synonym for an area

**Specific skill**:
A narrowly defined ability that can be taught, practiced, and assigned on its own, such as translating Group 3 words.
_Avoid_: Whole Activity as a synonym for a specific skill

**Conditional phase**:
The first phase of the full pathway, covering conditional understanding, translation, inference, linking, complex rules, and LSAT applications.

**Learning sequence**:
The teaching and practice that take a student through a specific skill from the beginning. Every specific skill also has a separately assignable practice drill.
_Avoid_: Practice drill as a synonym for the complete learning sequence

**Practice drill**:
Work assigned directly to practice a skill, without requiring its full teaching sequence again. Specific-skill and general drills offer Beginner, Medium, and Advanced difficulty.
_Avoid_: Full curriculum as a synonym for a practice drill

**Quantifier phase**:
The subsequent phase covering some, most, and all reasoning. It follows the conditional phase in the full pathway and can also be assigned independently.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level as a synonym for a phase

**General practice**:
Practice across several conditional skills at a selected difficulty. It is distinct from taking the complete curriculum in learning order.
_Avoid_: Full curriculum as a synonym for general practice

**Difficulty level**:
The degree of challenge of assigned work: Beginner, Medium, or Advanced. Difficulty can be selected for general practice or for a specific area or skill; criteria remain to be defined.
_Avoid_: Scope, phase, or topic as synonyms for difficulty
