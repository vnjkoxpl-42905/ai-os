Lamplight: strongest immediate task focus and distinctive warm light/dark field contrast. Take lit task surface, restrained warm accent, deliberate one-primary-action hierarchy, window-week idea as an optional exploration. Reject literal hanging-wire height on phone and extensive lower-page spacing; hero consumes much phone view and calendar/practice move far down. Strongest art direction, not a finished visual system.

Marginalia: strongest typography and calm paper/ink restraint; desktop sidenotes are editorially sophisticated. Take contextual annotations, precise blue link treatment, fine rules and thoughtful type scale selectively. Reject making the entire task overview paragraphs or duplicating many actions between main text and notes. The compact original calendar reads more quickly. Better typographic reference than wholesale dashboard structure.

Carrel: most tactile depth and coherent object vocabulary. Take paper edge layering, clear due stamp, envelope as personal-message accent, useful session ticket, and restrained lift/drag feedback. Reject stacking all assignments into a file pile, decorative blank ruled areas, truncated tabs and giant physical props. On phone continue action falls below initial viewport. Many different objects compete. Stronger texture/material craft, weaker overall restraint than original.

Baseline: preserve calm light canvas, dark dominant task card, clear first action, stable navigation and compact seven-day strip. Main weakness is generic panel treatment and limited distinctive motion/material identity, not a need to abandon its structure. Do not blend all three metaphors into one screen. Choose one coherent language and borrow only earned details.
