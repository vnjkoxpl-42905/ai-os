## Why the grades moved

**Lamplight, mobile +1.**
- What I saw: the first 390×844 screen holds the greeting, the summary line, the lamp, the whole Negation Station card, the twelve-square progress bar and "Continue at prompt 6". All of it fits above the fold.
- My judgement: of the three directions, this phone first screen is the one most focused on tonight's task. The full-page render had hidden that.
- Still a problem: the nav still cuts off at "Lib".

**Marginalia, mobile +1.**
- What I saw: on the phone sheet, tapping a numbered row ("① Negation Station Open") opens a tinted panel. It holds the description, the tally marks and "Five of twelve prompts set", plus a "Close" link.
- My judgement: this answers my earlier criticism that mobile drops the sidenote content. The content is there, one tap away, and the blue CTA is above the fold.
- Still a problem: the first phone frame is blank apart from the nav and footer, which confirms the loading flash I saw on desktop.

**Carrel, mobile unchanged at 6.**
- What I saw: on the first phone screen, the empty ruled lines push the progress dots to the bottom edge and the "Continue" button below the fold. The tabs are cut to "Nega…", "Flaw…", "Note…", "Reca…".
- What the phone sheet adds: the swipe works well on phone. The card flings off at an angle, "Flaw questions, guided set 3" comes to the top, and the summary sentence updates to match. That's good interaction design, and it backs up Carrel's motion score of 8. It doesn't fix the screen layout.
