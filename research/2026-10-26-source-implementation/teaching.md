## Use previews to make teaching edits reviewable

This is a reusable local procedure, not a new activated skill or a completed media trial.

1. Write one teaching objective and the exact statements/qualifiers that must survive the edit. Use original or explicitly cleared material; exclude student recordings from a synthetic pilot.
2. Record source filenames, rights status, duration and requested output before editing. Keep source bytes unchanged.
3. Make the cut before decorative media. Preserve complete spoken qualifications; inspect the transcript at every cut rather than treating word alignment as proof of meaning.
4. Render a short low-resolution preview with existing local tooling after its required documentation and commands are checked. No paid pack, API transcription or upload is needed merely to adopt this review method.
5. Review timestamped intervals for reasoning, clipped words, captions hiding the source, legibility, audio level and pacing. Record each requested change with start/end time and expected behavior.
6. Revise the preview and check those intervals again. Record actual verification separately from expected behavior. A probe report cannot establish readable captions, natural motion or correct teaching.
7. Render the final output after the preview is accepted under the applicable workflow, and keep final review separate from any send/publication authorization.
8. Save only preferences proven useful across actual revisions as reusable instructions. The creator's reported fifteen revisions or speed claims are not a quality target.
