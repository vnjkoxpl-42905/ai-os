The story

The second half of the window was about twice as busy as the first. Calendar sessions went from 76 in the first thirteen weeks to 159 in the next thirteen, and Stripe charges went from $2,441 to $5,774. The busiest teaching week was the week of 2026-08-24 with 17 sessions; the biggest Stripe week was the week of 2026-08-24 at $1,200.

Consultations drove the growth. They climbed through July, peaking at 13 in the week of 2026-07-13, and sessions rose a few weeks behind them. That pattern matches the decision on 2026-07-20 to move consultation intake into LSAT U. Then consultations went quiet: there are 0 on the calendar from 2026-09-28 onward. Sessions have held at 12 to 17 a week since 2026-09-14, but with no new consultations the pipeline that fed August and September is empty.

Money does not track sessions week by week. Students prepay for passes, so a big charge lands once and the sessions spread out after it. The week of 2026-10-05 had 12 sessions and no Stripe charges at all. Stripe also does not see every payment, so treat these numbers as a floor, not total revenue.

The platform grew in the same months. LSAT U recorded 28 real signups (test accounts removed), most of them from July on, and the September redesign and the conditional bootcamp followed. 7 of those signups still show no access, so they joined and are waiting on a grant.

Before planning on these numbers: the session count is a count of calendar titles, not paid or attended sessions. Cal.com was not pulled for this page, and Stripe is not the only payment channel.
