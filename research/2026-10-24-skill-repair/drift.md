Retain catalog bodies where they repair unsafe upstream defaults. - Existing genuine compatibility dispatchers (`second-brain-query`, `second-brain-lint`, and assignment routing) are distinct from stale copied bodies. Preserve verified dispatch behavior; do not merely add a dispatcher phrase to conceal differing full implementations.
