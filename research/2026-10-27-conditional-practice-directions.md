## Direction1: Editor-style workspace (recommended)
- Inside the existing bootcamp shell, one integrated category/activity navigator. Descriptive group names: Translate sentences; Connect conditional rules; Review flashcards; Practise LSAT questions; Mixed conditional practice. Keep distinct named activities and permitted settings.
- Central selected-activity instructions. Contextual settings sit beside them on wide screens, below on narrower screens. One clear Start action launches the existing configured player; no examples or trial questions in setup.
- Rich neutral material: graphite structural chrome, warm light reading plane and one subdued selection accent. Distinction comes from joined surfaces, contact edges, inset selected state, stronger type hierarchy and precise grouping rather than a repeated card deck.
- Narrow layouts collapse navigation into a labeled selection control and show the selected activity/setup directly. Avoid stacking all five large category cards above every activity and setup on a phone.
- Optional search and keyboard shortcuts support finding, never replace the visible browse route. If motion is added, use a short panel transition that retains location and has a reduced-motion equivalent.
- Risk: too many columns inside the already-present bootcamp sidebar. Keep two internal regions at typical1307px width; use a third inspector only if available content width permits it. Never shrink description type to force panels to fit.

## Direction2: Focused launcher
- First surface shows explicit practice areas and named drills. Selecting a drill replaces the browsing surface with its instructions and settings.
- Compact visible context: Practice type, Activity, Change. One main setup surface with a strong Start action. Back restores the same category/activity/settings rather than resetting to the first group.
- Use a contained charcoal setup stage with a light readable instruction area, clear form labels and distinct controls. Large typography is reserved for the selected drill, not a slogan.
- This gives a more concentrated, cinematic transition and shorter phone setup. Trade-off: alternatives are less visible while configuring, so Change and preserved selection are essential.
