## What is actually new

Targeted search of current corpus/wiki/raw Markdown found an archived Loophole marketing homepage, not these lesson transcripts. The recovered clips are attributed to Ellen Cassidy/Elemental Prep through descriptions and lesson text, even where author frontmatter is empty. Original sources are preserved in each file's `source:` field under `https://new.theloophole.com/`. Captures range from 2026-08-24 through 2026-08-30; publication dates are generally unknown. No URLs were visited and no live platform claims were verified in this review.
