## The teaching contribution is diagnosis before prescription

The 7Sage webinar is a 2022-01-11 source with a complete transcript through 1:01:39. It supplies several practical coaching distinctions:

- **Four error types:** reading, logical fundamentals, question-type process, and test-taking strategy. A learner who cannot explain the logic may need fundamentals work; a learner who understands it but allocates time badly needs strategy practice. Labels are hypotheses to test with the learner, not facts inferred from a red answer alone.
- **Read the whole timing sequence.** A missed final passage may result from earlier questions consuming the time. Correct earlier answers can still reveal costly inefficiency. Conversely, avoid treating late misses as proof that the final passage was intrinsically hardest.
- **Second-pass review is distinct from blind review.** Revisiting a flagged item within the timed section should reconsider its conclusion, quantifiers and reasoning rather than automatically repeat the first interpretation. Their suggestion to rebuild every flagged question from scratch is a tutor's recommendation with a time cost, not a universal rule.
- **Confidence and opportunity cost matter together.** The source recommends flagging unresolved two-choice decisions and investigating both overconfidence and underconfidence. Time spent reconfirming correct answers may crowd out a tractable missed question. Its 90% confidence threshold and 60-to-120-second timing ranges are speaker-specific heuristics, not calibrated or adopted thresholds.
- **Identify shared processes across question types.** Strengthen, weaken, evaluate, resolve and sufficient-assumption tasks can introduce information. Rejecting an answer merely because it was not stated in the stimulus may confuse these tasks with inference tasks. Necessary assumption is not simply strengthening, and sufficient assumption requires establishing the conclusion rather than making it somewhat more likely.
- **Translate review into the next timed attempt.** The source asks learners to state the diagnosed issue before another section and ask how they could solve the question under timed conditions. This differs from achieving a correct answer only through unlimited rereading or dictionary use.

The RC discussion distinguishes explicit mention from implication and attributes a comparative-passage method and timing targets to the speaker. Preserve those as historical tutoring choices. Logic-games examples, historical score estimates, named difficulty ratings and old service links need contextual labels; none becomes current test or platform ground truth.

No exact webinar source ID or exact four-category diagnostic framework was found in the inspected current corpus. Related blind-review and diagnosis language exists in curriculum and explanation pages; this is a candidate teaching synthesis, not a claim that Joshua has never taught these ideas.

## The plugin reports cannot be merged into one factual verdict

Priority estimates roughly 6.8k catalog entries; Gemini estimates 2.2k. Priority explicitly admits it did not retrieve the complete catalog and grades 87 named plugins. Gemini describes broad coverage and several precise performance numbers without usable underlying citations. Both were created on 2026-08-23 and tailored to a 15,000-note, 8 GB Mac. Those assumptions do not establish this Windows laptop's present hardware, vault workload, or phone performance.

Concrete conflicts to retain:

- Priority recommends Tasks, cautious Tag Wrangler use, optional Excalidraw and an Omnisearch benchmark. Gemini rejects or strongly discourages them based on broad performance assertions.
- Gemini asserts Native Bases uses SQLite, Omnisearch's index consumes hundreds of MB, Canvas Image LOD reduces 1.2 GB to 50 MB, and a final stack rests at 150-to-300 MB. These are unverified source claims, not established architecture or benchmarks.
- Gemini's claim that local API token authentication ensures absolute data sovereignty does not establish how an external AI provider handles retrieved contents. Priority appropriately separates a local integration from provider-dependent transmission.
- Both propose Local REST API with native MCP support. Its present API, release, endpoint, permission model and actual need must be verified before configuration. Filesystem access already available to local agents may cover much of the use case.
- The overview explicitly retains Priority grades despite Gemini concerns and marks Canvas Image LOD's claimed performance numbers unverified. That synthesis is a research judgment, not Joshua's approval to install plugins.

The useful missing contribution is a **repeatable evaluation method**: baseline the actual device; add one capability at a time; compare startup, idle resource use, navigation and typing; keep a plugin only if a repeated real workflow improves. Prioritize native capabilities and avoid stacking duplicate indexes, sync layers or agent interfaces. A narrow read-only or new-test-note trial should precede bulk mutation. These are test-design ideas, not an instruction to change the present vault.

1. Add the four context failure modes as a compact diagnostic aid: a wrong fact, too much irrelevant material, missing/misrouted context, or contradictory source generations. The source's categories overlap; use them to find evidence, not as a scoring system.
2. When retrieval fails, record the requested fact, actual route taken, correct source, and narrow routing fix. Existing broad instructions to look first are not a replacement for tracing a specific failure.
3. Preserve model-independent prompting lessons: outcome, purpose, acceptance criteria, real constraints, evidence-backed reporting and independent verification. Evaluate effort on representative tasks instead of copying benchmark or billing claims from video transcripts.
4. Preserve the session-audit and skill-review process as possible improvements to existing operator workflows. Current `references/automation-backlog.md` already cites `brainstorms/2026-07-19-session-mining-reflection.md`; do not assume that process is missing solely because this video is absent.
5. Keep Scrollcraft's visitor-belief/ordered-journey questions available for a future real website task. Existing design sources overlap; avoid inventing a new frontend doctrine or enabling paid asset calls from a transcript.
