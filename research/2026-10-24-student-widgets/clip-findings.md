## The three useful additions

1. **Always available Find a question.** Put a quiet search trigger in the top rail and/or a compact utility row, accessible by keyboard. Open a focused picker with LR/RC toggle and PrepTest / Section / Question or Passage fields. Accept a pasted reference, show a readable result, then let the student open it or save it. Include recent searches or saved picks so the component reduces repeat navigation. Keep its visual prominence below the main resume card. No countdown, animated texture or large promotional copy needed.
2. **Recent activity with resumable rows.** Three to five compact rows: exact activity name, LR/RC or lesson context, time last opened, and an explicit status such as In progress or Reviewed. Use Continue, View result or Open as appropriate. A quiet All activity destination can follow. The visual should answer “Where was I?” without replicating every assigned-work row or adding invented score charts. This responds to the user's new request; no equivalent section is in the inspected current 04 HTML.
3. **A small saved-question tray.** The picker can save a question into a compact list, with an optional note or Bring to session action. Reuse the existing session-preparation panel and its saved question rather than create another parallel notes system. This connects independent practice, question retrieval and tutoring preparation. If the first two additions already make the home dense, keep this inside the picker/session panel rather than another dashboard card.

These features are additive UI exploration. Sample data and simulated actions must remain labelled as such; no backend integration or production release was requested.
