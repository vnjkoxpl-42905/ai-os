GLYPH - ambient information display
Details and extras
Narrative

Glyph is a customizable e-ink desktop display designed for monitoring information, productivity workflows, and connected services through a dedicated physical interface.

The project explores a system-oriented approach to ambient computing, combining persistent information surfaces, modular widgets, and configurable data sources into a single desktop device.

The project included the development of the hardware identity, industrial design language, companion mobile application, interface system, interaction patterns, motion behavior, and product visualizations.

Project Information

Glyph is a customizable e-ink desktop display designed to surface essential information in a calm and persistent way.

The device acts as a dedicated dashboard for market data, productivity tools, notifications, environmental metrics, and custom integrations, reducing the need to constantly check phones, browsers, and secondary applications.

The project explored the intersection of hardware, interface design, and ambient computing. The system was designed around glanceable information, low-distraction interactions, modular widgets, and a monochromatic visual language optimized for e-ink displays.

Users can configure layouts, data sources, and widgets through a companion mobile application, creating a personalized information hub tailored to individual workflows.
