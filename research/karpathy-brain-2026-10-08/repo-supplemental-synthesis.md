## Findings with source evidence

- Historical captioning sources connect preprocessing, caption batching, shared recurrent state, masked loss, optimizer snapshots and prediction/evaluation visualization. The [LanguageModel source](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--neuraltalk2--misc--LanguageModel-lua.md) also admits incomplete beam-search certainty: “improve performance, so I am declaring this correct.” This counterexample limits any universal claim that every historical implementation demanded rigorous verification.
- Paper notes challenge baselines, parameter/FLOP comparisons, objectives, metrics, ablations and missing experimental details. VIN asks: “Why do the authors only evaluate success rate when the training criterion is expected reward?” [VIN](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--paper-notes--vin-md.md). Matching Networks requests an Exemplar SVM comparison. These are dated critiques, not independently replicated paper results.
- Researchpooler separates gathering, enrichment, analysis and rendering while keeping prototype records flexible: “However, I am a believer of avoiding premature encapsulation.” [README](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--researchpooler--README.md). This is a scoped prototype tradeoff with acknowledged limitations.
- Lecture notebooks progressively expose count models, likelihood, embeddings, activation initialization, batch normalization, gradients and hierarchical context. The second micrograd notebook explicitly fixes an accumulation bug: `# NOTE: in the video I incorrectly used = instead of +=. Fixed here.` [Notebook source](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--nn-zero-to-hero--lectures--micrograd--micrograd_lecture_second_half_roughly-ipynb.md). Saved losses are preserved output evidence, not new runs; unfinished sampling exercises and stale output/configuration mismatches remain explicit in their pages.
- RustBPE uses weighted unique chunks, local count deltas, priority selection, streaming refill and parallel Rust work. Its Python suite asserts `assert tiktoken_ids == rustbpe_ids, "Tiktoken should match RustBPE"`. [Tests](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--rustbpe--tests--python--test_tokenizer-py.md). Round-trip and batch equivalence are checked separately from timing; tie ambiguity is warned. Test definitions are not proof of passing runs. The encoder logs and skips regex failures. [Implementation](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--rustbpe--src--lib-rs.md).
- Browser RL examples derive Bellman/TD updates, then expose rewards, exploration, traces, planning and policy changes. Smooth policy updates are “non-standard and something I added for nicer visualizations”. [Gridworld TD](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--reinforcejs--gridworld_td-html.md). Policy-gradient classes are explicitly “buggy implementation, doesnt work...” and not exported. [RL library](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--reinforcejs--lib--rl-js.md). PuckWorld's removal of a target network is scoped to simple toy experiments and brevity, not a universal DQN recommendation.
- The character demo teaches learned embeddings, recurrent state, next-character cross-entropy, training perplexity, temperature and sample inspection. Its stated input is “The demo is pre-filled with sentences from” Paul Graham essays. [Demo](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--recurrentjs--character_demo-html.md). The embedded essay dataset was read but remains Paul Graham material, not Karpathy persona doctrine. Its gradient-check function exists with its UI hook disabled.
- Mixture-density exploration makes uncertainty distributional: “This means that the network knows what it doesn't know:”. [Notebook](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/supplemental/repos--karpathy--randomfun--MixtureDensityNets-ipynb.md). Saved gradient diagnostics and loss traces are historical notebook output, not current correctness guarantees.
- MADE/flow sources expose masks, inversion and log-Jacobian bookkeeping; semantic review retains third-party adaptation and saved-output limits. Researchlei and ScholarOctopus explore coauthorship, citation links, TF-IDF/t-SNE maps and proximity/search interfaces; map proximity is a heuristic, not validated semantic relevance.
- Reader3 separates physical EPUB files, logical reading order and TOC entries and makes text available for LLM context. Rendergit distinguishes human HTML from model text exports with explicit file-size/binary skips. These are source tools, not proof that every repository byte is included.
- ulogme shows heuristic capture and aggregation through dated files, day partitions and category rules. Static source limitations include first-entry accounting and unknown-duration assumptions. No monitoring was started, no personal logs were read and no health/productivity inference was adopted.
