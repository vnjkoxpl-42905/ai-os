## Supported patterns and qualifications

1. **Measure against a real baseline with controlled comparison.** [Autoresearch instructions](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/autoresearch-program.md) require the first run to establish a baseline and protect evaluation. [Reported agent experiment failures](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/experiment-design-failures.md) identify absent baselines, ablations and runtime/FLOP controls. Independent project instructions and a separate experiential post support this pattern.
2. **Prefer simple inspectable mechanisms, then add complexity.** [Minbpe](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/minbpe-readme.md) emphasizes readable hackable code, [its exercise](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/minbpe-exercise.md) builds capabilities stepwise, and [agent coding notes](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/claude-coding-notes.md) recommend a likely-correct naive algorithm before optimization. The exercise and README from one project alone would not establish two independent examples.
3. **Do not buy small metric gains with uncontrolled cost or complexity.** [Autoresearch](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/repos/autoresearch-program.md) names the complexity criterion; [objective and simplicity](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/objective-and-simplicity.md) and [two-metric acceptance](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/two-metric-acceptance.md) explain runtime/memory/quality tradeoffs. Fixed-time and loss-plus-time policies are different valid experiment designs; do not collapse them into one universal formula.
4. **Expose assumptions and give observable success criteria.** [Claude coding notes](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/claude-coding-notes.md) criticize silent assumptions and recommend tests-first and success criteria. [Explicit delegation](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/explicit-delegation.md) says to understand the agent's tools and actions. These are two different dated posts, not independent controlled studies.
5. **Keep memory inspectable, local and portable.** [Knowledge bases](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/llm-knowledge-bases.md) describes raw-to-wiki, indexed retrieval, filed answers and lint. [Explicit local memory](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/explicit-local-memory.md) explains user ownership and interchangeable AI. The related [idea-file post](../../wiki/sources/karpathy/x/idea-files.md) describes the gist and is not an independent instance of deployment success.

These patterns are inputs to the seven-rule derivation, not seven completed rules. The parent task must corroborate teaching claims in distinct lecture/blog sources. Do not fabricate independent support for predictions, deliberately broken examples, or platform verification merely to match a video's labels.
