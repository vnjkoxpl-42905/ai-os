## Processing is not the same as personal evidence

Machine payload processes include government occupation HTML, generated dependency locks, datasets, collector metadata and generated SVG geometry. Full byte consumption and inspectable parser output do not mean a model semantically understood every value, table sentence or library algorithm. Mixed reviews distinguish authored notebook cells/text from images, weights and generated outputs. Some plots were inspected; others remain unreviewed and their source pages say so.

Foreign authors, third-party libraries, shared course authorship, fictional/model-generated prose, generated estimates and collector scripts are not promoted as Karpathy's beliefs. Preserved source bugs and conflicting statements remain explicit, including evaluation, distributed-reduction and historical beam-search limitations. The seven rules are a sourced operational synthesis, not an assertion of universal historical behavior.

## What this completion does not claim

- Companion blog HTML represents the same articles; markup, embedded media and linked pages are not separate reviewed articles.
- Caption fidelity to every spoken word, missing captionless uploads, video diagrams/audio and mirror editing remain unverified.
- Truncated quoted-post previews, linked media/cards/documents and exhaustive X history are not certified recovered. Previously collected long-form repairs remain distinct source revisions.
- Repository code was inspected statically; its tests, training, hardware requirements and production acceptance were not executed by compilation.
- The collector's hardcoded completeness fields are not acquisition proof. See [handoff verification](HANDOFF-VERIFICATION.md) and [X rerun result](X-RERUN-RESULT.md).
