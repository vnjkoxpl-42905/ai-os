# Near-white and charcoal refinement

Owner direction: restore near-white/charcoal; avoid beige/sage. Keep UI composition, top rails and GSAP. Not a platform-wide deployment.

Palette: canvas #f8f9fb; raised surface #ffffff; low surface #f0f2f5; charcoal #17191d; lifted charcoal #282b31; primary text #202329; secondary text #626974; line #dce0e6. Tiny slate-steel progress/focus accents give neutral contrast without a new branded hue. Soft contact shadows, ambient elevation and inset edge highlights provide depth. No glows.

Inspected21st Vercel https://21st.dev/@serafimcloud/themes/vercel and ModernMinimal https://21st.dev/@serafimcloud/themes/modern-minimal. Adapted neutral role hierarchy, not a wholesale copied theme. ModernMinimal blueprimary rejected. Softened Vercel pureblack to charcoal for this product.
