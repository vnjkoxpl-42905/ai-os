## Design takeaways root can integrate

- Storyboards reduce expensive animation-regeneration loops.
- Motion needs multiple natural-time frame checks; a final static screenshot does not prove the transition worked.
- Layering, spacing, type, colour and reference composition matter beyond a text fade-in.
- Use custom references to choose a visual family, while keeping actual product content distinct.
- Save accepted workflow preferences after iterative review, rather than adopting each influencer tool wholesale.
- Scroll-driven cinematic marketing effects are possibilities, not automatic requirements for a repeat-use study dashboard.
