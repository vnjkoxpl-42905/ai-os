Logic Training
Category 1 sufficient condition: If,When,All,The Only, Every, Any.
Category 2:Necessary condition:Only if, Only when,Only where,Only,Always,Must,
Requires
follow steps Conditional Statements
1. Identify the logical operator. category 1 vs category 2
2. Identify the two main concepts (groups, categories, events, ideas). 3. Assign
symbols to the two main concepts.
3. Apply the translation rule.
4. Find the contrapositive.
5. Translate back to English.
Part 1: Identifying Logical Indicators and Underlying
Concepts
Instructions: For each statement below, identify and highlight the logical indicator,
then underline the two main concepts or groups.
1. A warranty is valid if the product is registered within 30 days.
2. Whenever you see lightning, expect thunder to follow.
3. The gate opens only if the correct code is entered.
4. All managers must undergo annual training.
5. The scholarship applies to any student who maintains a 4.0 GPA.
6. You can access the VIP area only when your ID is verified.
7. Only after the agreement is signed will the project commence.
8. Members who pay their dues can attend the meeting.
9. If the alarm sounds, the building should be evacuated immediately.
10. Passengers must present a ticket to board the train.
11. A refund is issued only if the item is returned unopened.
12. The team goes to the finals if they win the semifinals.
13. Every applicant who passes the test will receive an interview call.
14. You must be over 18 to vote in the election.
15. If you plant trees, you will support the environment.
