## How the patterns transfer

- Tracker: one evidence cell per answer, each revealing its saved record. Keep native buttons, visible labels and touch targets. Its original div trigger should not replace the prototype's keyboard semantics.
- BarList: aligned labels/counts and whole-row interaction. Preserve correct/denominator geometry for accuracy; its maximum-normalized lengths and minimum nonzero widths are unsuitable unchanged.
- Check: a short stroke reveal can acknowledge deliberate selection. Saved historical results should remain immediately legible, with no repeated entrance on every filter update. Recreate with existing SVG/Motion facilities when that is lighter than a Lottie dependency.
- X: preserve its crossed meaning. The source's optional plus morph would confuse a missed-answer status.
- Retain reduced-motion behavior and warm paper/cobalt/amber materials. Public references inform the interaction; they do not replace the existing product brief.

## Working rule

Scout the catalog, inspect the live demo, find independently published author/source material, then adapt reusable code or recreate the observed behavior. Record confirmed source details separately from visual inference. A marketplace code quota limits that retrieval route; it does not stop visual research or original implementation.

No component was installed or copied into LSAT U during this follow-up. The existing prototype and its earlier verification status are unchanged.
