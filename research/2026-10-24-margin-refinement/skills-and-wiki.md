## Which can honestly grade out of 100?

Impeccable critique is the closest installed structured assessment. Its `reference/critique.md` combines two independent assessments, Nielsen's 10 heuristics scored 0-4 each (total /40), cognitive-load checks, and a deterministic pattern detector. `reference/heuristics-scoring.md` defines those anchors. It does not supply a universal objective aesthetic /100 score. A normalized usability score can be `total/40*100`, but that must remain labelled usability rather than visual excellence.

For this student UI brief use a declared visual rubric instead: hierarchy 25, typography 20, composition/spacing/depth 20, colour coherence 15, purposeful motion 10, responsive presentation 10. Visually score before and after against the same captures, with concrete evidence per deduction. Label it design judgment. Detector cleanliness or a skill's preset must not be passed off as objective taste.

## Useful saved principles, not new prescriptions

The index route succeeds through `wiki/index.md` -> `references/website-design-planning.md`, and AI workspace hub -> `wiki/sources/top-7-claude-design-skills-chase-ai.md`. One-hop backlinks were checked via `rg`; related source summaries and action queue use the same owning reference.

- Start with the student's immediate decision. An authenticated dashboard does not need a landing page's persuasion hierarchy.
- Give each section a useful job before selecting widgets. One prominent resumed-task action should win attention.
- Vary visual intensity: a decisive focal card and quiet surrounding areas can coexist.
- Add depth through deliberate layers and small differences in elevation, not a collection of unrelated metaphors or heavy 3D.
- Choose references for specific observable qualities, not broad adjectives such as premium.
- Signature interaction should help understand information or feedback. Repetition without new help is a stronger warning than a particular font or gradient.
- Inspect rendered positions and mobile sizes, and sample scroll/motion frames. Component source, test assertions and metadata do not establish visual quality.
- Taste, font, gradients and serif typography alone do not prove generic AI output. Judge the composition and student purpose.
