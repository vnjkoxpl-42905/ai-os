# RC practice needs one coherent journey from selection to improvement

Audit started 2026-10-16; section follow-up, live Agent Browser visual verification, and Gemini Argon independent review completed 2026-10-17. Scope confirmed by Joshua: **RC Practice & Drilling first** (`/practice/drills?section=rc`, `/rc/search`, Global Question Finder, `/practice/exams` RC sections, RC drill/section players, and RC results/review); shared improvements cover LR. **Strictly excludes RC Bootcamp.** This is an inspection and execution brief, not an implementation or release claim.

**Independent Gemini Argon review is COMPLETE.** See [review-disposition.md](review-disposition.md) for the full verification of Codex's `RC-01` through `RC-12` findings and the **9 new material findings (`A1` through `A9`)** discovered during Argon's hands-on Agent Browser UI/UX walkthrough and deep source pass.

## The strongest direction

Preserve LSAT U's passage-first builder, explicit feedback choices, readable topic/framework filters, structured passage summaries (`Topic`, `Framework`, `Main Point`, `P1-P4` map), and existing reader tools (including LSAT U's existing `Paragraph focus` and 6 accessibility color schemes currently hidden inside the `Aa` button). Fix the broken history/adaptive signals (`A1`-`A3`), the fragmented search and broken Global Question Finder dropdown (`RC-01`, `RC-06`, `A5`), and the standalone RC section runner (`RC-08`, `RC-09`, `A4`) before adding more controls. Add reusable RC sets, rich history cards with passage titles/scores and a `Discard` action (`A6`), a spoiler-safe review journey (`RC-04`, `A9`), and transparent adaptation (`RC-02`).

7Sage's clearest advantages are reusable templates, deeper history-aware selection, richer timing choices, optional training modes, configurable blind-review recommendations, and explanations connected to question analytics. LSAT U can improve on the inspected competitor with clearer terminology, tolerant source-address search, honest exposure tracking, goal-based setup, a comparative passage `[Both | Passage A | Passage B]` switcher (`A9`), side-by-side structured passage maps in review (`A9`), and evidence-linked review. These are proposed advantages, not measured superiority today.

## Evidence and limits

The [observation ledger](observations.md) records E01-E30 from Codex's signed-in browser interaction on both products, supplemented by Gemini Argon's hands-on visual UI/UX walkthrough in the dedicated Agent Browser (Port 9223) recorded in [review-disposition.md](review-disposition.md). The same real passage, PT101 S4 P1 about P.D. James, was selected and loaded in both ordinary drill players, and Comparative Reading (`PT123 S4 P2`, *"The Capacity for Music"*) was inspected live in LSAT U's player. The same RC section, PT102 S1, was opened in both actual section players and exited without answers or submission. Existing completed and in-progress LSAT U attempts were inspected without changing their answers. The [source inventory](source-inventory.md) maps implementation and historical repair evidence across both the Codex and Argon source passes.

This was a broad functional and visual audit, not exhaustive certification. Not every timing/feedback combination was completed; neither audit section was scored. Physical mobile, actual screen-reader speech, 200% zoom, offline/concurrent devices, complete explanation coverage and score validity were not tested. Source inspection does not prove deployed revision. Missing evidence is marked as unverified rather than a defect.

### How to read priority

- P1: materially changes what practice means, loses learner intent, or undermines trustworthy learning decisions.
- P2: recurring friction, missing capability or confusing state that should be fixed in the first coherent release.
- P3: later enhancement or content investment after core acceptance passes.

## What should exceed the inspected 7Sage experience

1. **Choose by intent.** "One fresh passage," "Work on inference," "Revisit flagged questions," and "Simulate a section" produce a readable plan before launch. Advanced filters (including Question Family, Question Type, and Comparative Features) remain one click away inside the builder.
2. **One tolerant finder that never launches a ticking drill by accident.** Fix the `TEST` / `SECTION` / `Question` selector bar in Global Finder (`Ctrl+K`), accept `PT101 S4 Q1`, `101.4.1`, canonical full passage addresses and punctuation variations, and always land on a safe, spoiler-protected preview before starting a clock.
3. **First-class Comparative Reading ergonomics.** Add a sticky `[Both | Passage A | Passage B]` view bar at the top of Comparative passages and display target-passage badges (`Passage A`, `Passage B`, `Both`) so students don't scroll endlessly through a stacked wall of text.
4. **Side-by-side Passage Map in Review.** Keep LSAT U's structured `Passage summary` (`Topic`, `Framework`, `Main Point`, `P1-P4` breakdown) anchored in a tab on the left passage column during Review so the student can compare the paragraph map against the question and choices on the right without pushing the question off-screen.
5. **Surface LSAT U's existing Reader Focus & Display themes.** Promote `Paragraph focus` and the 6 high-contrast color schemes (`Yellow on navy`, `Purple on light green`, `White on black`, etc.) out of the hidden `Aa` tooltip into a clear `Display` toolbar control.
6. **Identifiable History & Results.** Every `In progress`, `Completed`, and `Results` card shows the exact official source (`PT 139 · S3 · P1 · Improving Farm Economics`), Topic, Format, and Score, with a clean `Discard` option for unfinished sittings.
7. **Progress that distinguishes accuracy from familiarity and skips.** Excluded sessions are truly excluded (`A1`), skipped questions never inflate adaptive accuracy or mark a passage completed (`A2`), history never truncates at 1,000 rows (`A3`), and fresh first attempts stay distinct from repeat mastery (`RC-02`).

## Parity disposition prevents feature copying without purpose

Must match the observed useful capabilities: reusable RC recipes/sets; scalable manual selection; history facets; timing/accommodation controls; optional anticipation and segmented reading; coherent reader tools across both drills and standalone timed sections; review recommendations; notes/bookmarks; explanations connected to source identity and attempt history.

Evaluate after core reliability: target-time estimates, answer popularity/cohort statistics, alternate instructor videos, print, test-day disruption, comments, AI/tutor entry points and score prediction. Each gets a deliberate disposition in the plan. Do not fabricate population statistics or add score prediction from insufficient samples. A feature can be deferred with a reason; it cannot silently disappear from the parity ledger.

Do not copy: punctuation-sensitive search, unexplained tag abbreviations, configuration overload, or a prediction step that collects no useful reasoning.
