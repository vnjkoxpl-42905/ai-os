## Product outcome and measurable standard

A learner can find the intended material, understand the exact practice conditions, complete or resume it safely, review without accidental spoilers, and choose a justified next step. Ordinary drills and protected sections use a consistent reader without weakening timing, assignment identity or reveal boundaries.

Before claiming superiority, run equivalent tasks in both products with the same viewport, account assumptions and passage. Proposed release thresholds, to be baselined rather than presented as current measurements:

| Task / quality | Acceptance target |
|---|---|
| Start a fresh one-passage drill | At least 90% of representative first-time learners complete unaided in 60 seconds; median no slower than competitor baseline. |
| Find an exact source question | At least 90% complete within 30 seconds with common address formats or dropdown selectors; zero wrong-source or accidental live-drill launches in test corpus. |
| Explain configuration before start | At least 90% correctly identify passage count, question scope, timer behavior and answer-reveal timing. |
| Build and reuse a constrained set | At least 90% complete unaided; no silent filter relaxation, duplicate selections or reserved-material exposure. |
| Resume or discard interrupted work | Acknowledged answers/marks persist, history cards display `PT · S · P · Title`, in-progress drills can be cleanly discarded, timer follows policy, no duplicate attempt on retries. |
| Blind review | No score, key, answer styling or correctness-derived label leaks before explicit reveal (including on `RcResults` before `Review all`). |
| Accessibility | All core journeys keyboard-operable, visible focus, no traps; actual screen-reader and 200% zoom checks; contrast and reduced-motion checks. |
| Responsive & Comparative use | Actual narrow phone, tablet and laptop trials; controls remain reachable; comparative passage `[Both | Passage A | Passage B]` context preserved; `Passage summary` in review does not push questions off-screen. |
| Perceived performance | Establish baseline; target search feedback within 300ms after local debounce and p95 server result within 1s on agreed test network; manual passage list paginated/virtualized (not a 248-passage / 4,051-node DOM dump). |
| Learning effectiveness | Pilot fresh versus repeated-material results and delayed retention separately. Excluded sessions (`excluded_from_analytics`) and skipped questions (`is_correct: null`) never corrupt adaptive accuracy or passage completion status. |

Recruit a small formative group first (5-8 representative learners), revise failure points, then a larger acceptance group sufficient to report counts and uncertainty. Report sample size, tasks and failures; percentages from a tiny group are descriptive, not population proof.

| Attempt | An answer event/version within a sitting; first answer, skipped/unanswered (`selected: null, is_correct: null`), blind review answer and later practice remain distinguishable. |

| Fresh | A disclosed rule based on exposure/attempt policy across non-excluded sessions. Avoid silently redefining historical fresh counts. |

| Feedback policy | After question, after passage, at end, or sealed simulation. Controls and persistence follow the actual policy. |
| Training overlay | Optional prediction, reading segmentation or recall assistance. It cannot leak into protected simulation or alter its score semantics. |
| Review episode | Durable reattempt/reasoning record linked to immutable original performance. Revisit behavior is explicit. |

### Invariants

**History & analytics integrity policy (`A1`, `A2`, `A3`):**
- `getQuestionHistory()` must exclude any attempt belonging to an `rc_sessions` row where `excluded_from_analytics = true` (`A1`).
- Unanswered/skipped questions (`selected: null, is_correct: null`) must never be counted as correct (`!everIncorrect`) in `rollingAccuracy()` or mark an unanswered passage as `"Completed"` in `passageStatus()` (`A2`).
- `getQuestionHistory()` must paginate past Supabase's default 1,000-row PostgREST cap so learners with >1,000 attempt rows never lose recent history (`A3`).

**Exposure policy:** verified no-exposure/no-attempt material may qualify as Fresh. Historical or imported exposure that cannot be established remains Unknown and is excluded from strict Fresh by default; learners can explicitly include Unknown with counts disclosed. Preserve legacy classifications for historical reporting rather than retroactively changing scores. Deduplicate exposure events across preview, search, reader and explanation surfaces using actor, stable source, exposure kind and event identity; do not equate repeated rendering with a new attempt.

**Confidence policy:** optional per-question low/medium/high self-report in flexible practice, or after finishing and before reveal; never inferred as self-report from response speed. Missing confidence means Unknown and is not automatically low. Protected timed simulation has no required confidence interruption. Review suggestions can use flags/timing with their own labels when confidence is absent.

**Old-link policy:** preserve existing Results, Review, assignment and protected-test deep links. Historical Results links first explain that proceeding reveals score/answers when not already revealed, without creating a new attempt. Review links open the correct source/session. The new gate must not alter completed assignment state, sealed scope or protected source records. Persist reveal status separately from performance and test direct/back/refresh navigation.

1. A selected passage launches exactly its disclosed questions. Targeted-question practice is a separate explicit choice, not an accidental result of a filter. Clicking a passage or question in `/rc/search` or `QuestionFinder` (`Ctrl+K`) opens a read-only preview (`/rc/preview`), never an immediate ticking drill (`RC-01`, `A5`).
2. No empty passage, orphan question, wrong source, duplicate item or missing comparative half can enter a sitting. Fail visibly before starting.
3. Preview or configuration changes cannot silently change a previously acknowledged selection. Revalidation reports removed/unavailable items and asks for a new start decision.
4. Starting is idempotent (`RcHubSurface` and `RcResults.drillMisses`). An uncertain server outcome or double-click does not become a second sitting on retry (`RC-07`, `A9`).
5. Original scored answers, flags and clock records of protected work remain immutable in review. Learning records are separate.
6. UI cannot claim saved before acknowledgement. Failed review saves remain visibly unsaved and retryable without losing the learner's answer.
7. Timing begins at the documented acknowledged boundary. Pause, backgrounding, reconnect, accommodations and expiry behavior are policy-defined and tested.
8. Historical repair receipts remain regression requirements. Do not reopen or remove safeguards because a new component is simpler.
