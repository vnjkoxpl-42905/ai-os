## LR and shared architecture implications

[`DrillMode`](file:///C:/Users/Joshu/dev/ai-os-projects/lsatu-rc-create/src/types/drill.ts#L3) includes `adaptive`, `full-section`, `type-drill`, `natural-drill`, and `practice-set`. [`Typed launch contracts`](file:///C:/Users/Joshu/dev/ai-os-projects/lsatu-rc-create/src/lib/drillLaunch.ts#L90) distinguish Smart Drill, official section, saved set, assignment, guided drill, bootcamp practice set, bootcamp day, and explicit question lists.

Consequences for implementation:
- Share terminology, search grammar, filter chips, source-address display, save-status patterns, and accessibility primitives across RC/LR.
- Preserve RC passage membership and comparative A/B structure. Do not adapt LR question-ID launch by silently dropping passage context.
- Model reusable templates separately from sittings and attempts; launching a template creates a new sitting without changing historical records.
- Make section simulation, flexible practice, targeted questions and pedagogical activities distinct contracts with clear timing/reveal rules.
- Preserve sealed assignments and protected review when improving ordinary drill controls.
- Keep Smart Drill's current question identity separate from queued index identity when adapting shared controls.
