- `propositional`: atoms have stable ID plus explicit subject/domain/restriction metadata. Conditions are recursive Atom/Not/And/Or trees. For concrete finite-group tasks, the stated universe/population size is part of the authored problem and exhaustive model enumeration checks the actual answer/distractors.
