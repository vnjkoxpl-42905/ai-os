# Plan the visitor's decisions before the visual treatment

For the 25 Claude Design Tricks resource/setup review and current homepage tool status, see [dashboard design readiness](../research/2026-10-23-dashboard-design-readiness.md). The shared `design-resource-library` skill maps all25 tips without choosing an artistic direction.

Recovered method, adapted from the historical clip [I Built The Ultimate Claude Website Design Skill](<../raw/sources/old-obsidian-vault-2026-10-07/Clippings/I Built The Ultimate Claude Website Design Skill (steal this).md>). This is a planning reference, not a verified description of current software or an approved LSAT U redesign.

1. State who arrives, what they currently believe, and what evidence would let them take the next step. A section needs a job in that sequence.
2. Outline the scroll journey before selecting components. Put explanation and proof where the visitor needs them, rather than filling a standard template.
3. Inventory available assets. Use real product screenshots and real evidence for factual claims. Treat generated imagery as illustration, with its purpose specified.
4. Vary visual intensity to support attention. Quiet explanation and a deliberate focal point can coexist; animation needs a reason tied to the visitor's task.
5. Choose any signature interaction around useful information or feedback. It should help the visitor understand or decide.
6. Check screenshots at meaningful positions and viewport sizes. Compare the rendered result with the intended sequence, readability, evidence, and next action.

## What two later videos add (ingested 2026-10-12)

Nate Herk's [Fable 5.1](../wiki/sources/fable-5-1-kills-ai-website-slop.md) and [GPT-6 Astra](../wiki/sources/gpt-6-astra-kills-ai-website-slop.md) videos reach the same method from the build side. These are source claims, not tested here.

- **Fit the visitor before choosing effects.** His "three P's" (the pain, the person, the promise) come first. A site is slop when it feels made for someone else, however polished it looks. This is step 1 above, stated as a failure test.
- **Give the model references, not adjectives.** "Make it premium" fails. Name a reference site and say what you like about it, or borrow specific components. His sources: 21st.dev (components with prompts), godly.website (sites and sections), Awwwards (by industry), Motion Sites (backgrounds).
- **Depth through layering.** Separate planes that move at different rates on scroll add depth without a busy page. Subtle scroll feedback, such as text revealing as it enters, often does more than heavy 3D.
- **The verification loop extends step 6.** Check mobile, and have the agent screenshot frame by frame while scrolling, then fix what looks wrong. His one-shot sites still had visible flaws, so a first pass is a draft.
- **Method outweighs model.** With the same prompts, assets and skill, different models produced similar sites in his tests.

The `scroll-craft` skill he promotes is installed in this vault (2026-10-12) but has not been evaluated here.

## Five review checks from a later video (ingested 2026-11-20)

Nate Herk's [five patterns video](../wiki/sources/10000-ai-websites-nate-herk.md) turns this method into checks you can run on a finished page. Source claims; his conversion figures were not verified.

- **First screen answers three questions:** who is this for, what do I get (an outcome, not a feature), and what do I do next (one action). Write the answers before asking for design. This sharpens step 1.
- **One accent, only on the action.** Check proportions at roughly 60% background, 30% structure, 10% accent. If the accent also sits on headings, icons and borders, the button stops standing out.
- **Three layers:** something real behind, content in the middle, the one action in front. Let one element break a section edge; lift only the main action with a soft shadow.
- **Motion with one job, one mover per screen.** First-screen words appear immediately, never animated in. Respect reduced motion. This narrows his earlier parallax advice above.
- **A free sample before the ask:** a short quiz, estimator or tool that answers the question every visitor has before buying. For lsatprep.study see [LQ-007](../wiki/lsatu-idea-queue.md).

## Applying this to an activity entry (2026-10-16)

Local synthesis from Joshua's LR Recall annotations, the [teaching voice](../wiki/teaching-voice.md), [copy corrections](../wiki/COPY-CORRECTIONS.md) and the sources above. This is an application of the method, not a new claim attributed to the videos.

- Start from the student's immediate decision: choose work, learn the method or inspect a result. An authenticated activity entry does not need the persuasive hierarchy of an acquisition page.
- One prominent task action is enough. Its label must match its effect. A section chooser, a new attempt and a resumed attempt are different operations.
- Repetition without new help is a useful slop warning: a slogan repeating the title, a second demonstration teaching the same thing, or another comparison block after the method and examples. Remove the redundancy rather than only rewriting its wording.
- Place a method's steps beside its actual examples. Explicit labels such as Video examples and Example 1 are easier to identify than an atmospheric heading or a technical citation used as the main label.
- Preserve useful reasoning and complete requirements while shortening navigation copy. Brevity must not change silent reading into speaking, remove looking away or discard qualifications in recall.
- Evaluate what the interface helps the person do. Gradients, serif type, illustration or a three-part phrase alone cannot establish that content is AI-written or unsuitable. An unsupported claim about understanding or a promised improvement schedule is a separate accuracy problem.

## Turn a reference into a bounded review (2026-10-26)

Local working recipe adapted from the [reference-led hacks](../wiki/sources/claude-design-fifteen-hacks-jack.md), [Design Loop account](../wiki/sources/astra-website-design-loop-jack.md), and [REA investigation](../wiki/sources/rea-feature-investigation-chase-ai.md). This is our recipe; the promoted original prompt packs were not recovered. Use it within an authorized design task after the existing UI plan approval.

1. Name one immediate decision and the exact surface being reviewed. Record the baseline artifact and a screenshot actually inspected. For a public acquisition page, identify the evidence needed before a consultation; for a signed-in student, identify the next work action. Do not import acquisition-page persuasion into the student dashboard.
2. Choose at most three references for that decision. Record exact URL or saved path, viewport, region, capture date and the useful behavior or visual principle. A screenshot supports appearance; inspect interaction separately before claiming how it works.
3. Keep three evidence columns: **seen**, **inferred**, **unknown**. Add a specific finding only when the baseline and reference support it: location, observable problem, effect on the decision, proposed change and check. Do not manufacture consensus or numerical certainty from repeated agent opinions.
4. Make one focused iteration and one verification pass. Allow one repair pass for a failed check. If still unresolved, retain the baseline and record the failure rather than continue an unbounded polish loop.
5. Check readable desktop and phone renders, keyboard access, empty/error/loading states and reduced motion where relevant. Record whether evidence was a still image, actual natural playback or simulated interaction. Exported CSS/control values must survive a reload before a live-tweak prototype is considered reproducible.

Reusable review record:

```text
Decision and surface:
Approved scope and baseline path:
Reference URL/path, captured date, viewport and exact region:
Seen:
Inferred:
Unknown:
Finding: location / observable problem / user effect
Proposed change and smallest implementation:
Check and evidence path:
Result: passed / failed / not exercised
Remaining uncertainty and next owner:
```

For reusable assets, append source, licence or permission evidence, original file identity, intended use and any transformation. Keep generated illustrations separate from factual product evidence. Preserve selected brand tokens in the owning app/design reference only after Joshua selects the direction; an exploratory prototype does not settle the brand. Existing archive and library routes are sufficient until a recorded retrieval miss establishes a need for another capture/indexing tool.
