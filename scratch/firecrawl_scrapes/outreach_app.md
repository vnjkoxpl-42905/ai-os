# Outreach and OpenAI Launch Native ChatGPT App

Published: June 3, 2026

Last modified: July 24, 2026

![Outreach and OpenAI Launch Native ChatGPT App](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/696ea7514e736c595e9a23bf/6a206933453adb9d0f21ad13_Outreach%20%2B%20OpenAI%20Announcement_blog.jpeg)

**TL;DR:** Outreach has launched the first revenue orchestration platform app available natively in OpenAI's flagship products [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) and [Codex](https://chatgpt.com/codex/). By leveraging the Model Context Protocol ( [MCP](https://www.outreach.ai/resources/blog/what-is-model-context-protocol-mcp)) Server, sales teams can access [real pipeline data](https://www.outreach.ai/resources/blog/how-to-meet-your-sales-quota), generate collateral, and update deals directly within ChatGPT and Codex, resulting in a shorter path from questions to actions.

Managing different platforms creates overhead that could be spent on strategic work. Sellers are switching between tools to pull pipeline data, draft communications, and keep CRM records current. Every context switch costs time and execution quality. To help revenue teams consolidate those workflows, Outreach has announced a landmark partnership with OpenAI.

This week, [Outreach launched the Outreach app in ChatGPT and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server for Codex](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260603702778/en/Outreach-Launches-First-of-its-Kind-Revenue-Orchestration-Platform-App-in-ChatGPT-and-MCP-Server-for-Codex), making Outreach the first and only [revenue orchestration platform](https://www.outreach.ai/platform) available natively in OpenAI's flagship products.

Revenue teams can now access trusted business context inside the [AI tools](https://www.outreach.ai/resources/blog/best-ai-tools-automate-sales-follow-ups) they already use. The result is a direct path to drafting collateral, updating deals, and moving pipeline forward—without leaving the tools your teams rely on every day. In this blog post, we'll go over how this integration can impact revenue for go-to-market teams.

## How Does the Outreach App for ChatGPT Help Sales Teams?

[Sales](https://www.outreach.ai/persona/sales-leadership), [revenue operations](https://www.outreach.ai/persona/revenue-operations), and [customer success teams](https://www.outreach.ai/persona/customer-success) often struggle with disconnected data. The [Outreach app](https://support.outreach.io/support/solutions/articles/159000425603-outreach-mobile-app-overview) brings [Outreach agents](https://www.outreach.ai/ai-agents) and insights directly into ChatGPT as a native application.

Sellers can act on real pipeline data, generate collateral, and complete advanced sales tasks without switching between platforms. [Abhijit Mitra](https://linkedin.com/in/mitrasaab), CEO at Outreach, explains what this means for revenue teams:

“

This is the first time revenue teams can pull trusted Outreach context
directly into ChatGPT and Codex. We are giving sellers and leaders a
direct path to take action on trusted business context. MCP is what
makes this work across the AI ecosystem, and OpenAI is a critical part
of that future.


— Abhijit Mitra, CEO, Outreach

For CROs, the benefit is straightforward: your teams get a single place to work on pipeline and deals, grounded in the data your forecast already depends on.

## What Are the Benefits of the Outreach MCP Server for Codex?

For organizations building custom [AI workflows](https://www.outreach.ai/resources/blog/ai-agent-data-sources), the Outreach MCP Server extends Outreach's capabilities directly into Codex. By connecting Outreach context with advanced AI models through an open standard, customers gain the flexibility to create tailored, actionable workflows that scale with their needs.

The MCP Server enables revenue teams to access trusted insights, automate complex tasks, and execute with consistency at scale—all while maintaining the security and compliance necessary for enterprise-grade solutions.

## Unlock AI-Powered Revenue Workflows

The integration of Outreach with OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex marks a major step forward for revenue teams looking to streamline their workflows. By bringing real pipeline data and actionable insights into the tools teams already use, Outreach eliminates unnecessary complexity and empowers teams to focus on what matters most: closing deals and driving growth.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Outreach app for ChatGPT?

The Outreach app for ChatGPT is a native integration that brings live pipeline data, deal insights, and revenue workflows directly into ChatGPT, so sales teams can take action without switching platforms.

### What is an MCP Server and how does Outreach use it?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI models connect to external data sources. Outreach's MCP Server for Codex allows developers to build custom agentic workflows powered by real Outreach pipeline context.

### Is Outreach the first revenue orchestration platform in ChatGPT?

Yes, Outreach is the first and only revenue orchestration platform available natively in OpenAI's ChatGPT and Codex.

### What can sales teams do with Outreach inside ChatGPT?

Teams can query live pipeline data, generate sales collateral, update deals, and complete advanced sales tasks, all without leaving ChatGPT.

### Does the integration work for RevOps and CS teams too?

Yes. The integration is designed for sales, revenue operations, and customer success teams that need a unified workspace grounded in forecast data.
