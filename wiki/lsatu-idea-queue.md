# LSAT U idea queue: what your LSAT sources suggest building or trying

When an ingested LSAT source suggests something to act on, it lands here as one item with clear steps and a link to its source summary: a platform feature, a drill format, a teaching method to adopt, or an outside tool or platform to try. The teaching knowledge itself still compiles into the [corpus](../corpus/index.md); this page only holds what someone has to build or do. `second-brain-ingest` adds items; `second-brain-task` shows them, takes Joshua's approval, builds them and clears them. A finished or declined item is removed from this page and recorded on its source summary under `## Queue record`. Agreed product work still belongs in [the task ledger](tasks.md). AI workspace ideas have their own [AI action queue](../references/ai-workspace/action-queue.md).

- Steps:

  4. Check: Joshua has a one-page proposal naming the question it answers, the draft copy and how completion and bookings would be measured.
- Effort: medium · Status: new (2026-11-20)
