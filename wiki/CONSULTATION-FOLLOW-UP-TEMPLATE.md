five identical sends in Gmail between 2026-08-08 and 2026-09-08 (Reet, Fernande,
Avneet, Whitney, Cameron). This is the source of truth; the February 2026 PDF in
`raw/sources/personal/email-templates/` (two numbered steps + Google Form) is retired.

## Rules

- The opener is fixed. Do not add a personalized recap sentence; none of the five sends had one.
- The only variable lines: first name, the 7Sage line (in or out), and an optional
  "Sorry for the delay in getting these over to you." after the opener if the send is
  more than a day late (Avneet 09-04).
- If the student already emailed first, reply in their thread with the same body.
- If a specific weekly slot was agreed on the call, one plain sentence confirming it
  can go before the close ("Sunday works for me."), nothing more.
- Subject for a new thread: `Next steps + tutoring options` (Cameron 09-08).
- Draft only. Joshua sends.
