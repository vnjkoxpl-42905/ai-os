## Transcription quality, generally

Gemini ASR corrupts proper nouns heavily and inconsistently: 7Sage appears in 9 spellings,
LSAT as Elsa/ELSAT/LSI/Alsad/LEAP, contrapositive as "contraositive"/"quantropositive",
ad hominem as "ad homonym". Speaker labels are corrupted too — Joshua's turns are attributed
to **"Joshua Fisseha's Presentation"** (a screen-share artifact) in several files, so any
pipeline matching on speaker name will fail to link those sessions. Some files contain tokens
neither speaker uttered.

**The RC framework was the worst case**: rendered SEAR / SER / CER / Sierra / steer / serial,
and the letters match no expansion of the four moves. Joshua ruled **CEER** canonical on
2026-07-20 (Comparing, Explaining, Evaluating, Recommending); the distillates were normalized
to it, with the lines documenting the variants deliberately preserved.
