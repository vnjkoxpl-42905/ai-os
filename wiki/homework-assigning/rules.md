# Rules: what to assign after a session

Hand-written. Not regenerated. `playbook.md` is the generated, inferred view; this file is what Joshua has actually said.

## The goal

After Joshua runs the post-session handoff and homework step, the skill must already know exactly what to assign. It does not stop to ask him routine questions. It looks up the tags in [by-topic.md](by-topic.md), checks what the student holds, and writes the proposal for Joshua to approve.

## Confirmed by Joshua (2026-11-14)

1. **Reuse the teaching, rewrite the recap.** When an earlier note already covers the topic, reuse its teaching part and write a fresh recap section for this session.
2. **Activities follow the topic.** Negation Station after necessary-assumption work, Conditional Rephrase and the conditional bootcamp when conditionals are weak, Causation Station for causal topics.
3. **These are examples, not a fixed menu.** The right variation depends on what fits the session. Judgment per session beats a lookup.
4. **Timing is not a question.** The skill decides and proposes; it does not debate timing with him.

- **Naming:** a plain phrase for what the student learns ("Finding the Conclusion by Asking Why"). Kicker: "Session recap · <Month D>".
- **Shell:** warm paper ground, Fraunces headings, Outfit body, brass accent, a hero with three floating cards, numbered sections, tap-to-check sorting, a "What to do next" close. It is one self-contained page.
