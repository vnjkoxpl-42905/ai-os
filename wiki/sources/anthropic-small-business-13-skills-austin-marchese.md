# Anthropic small business skill architecture

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CNsP0mi1DLw. Archived 2026-10-26; publication and capture date unknown.

The supplied Austin Marchese transcript (0:00 through 15:53) was read in full. The speaker reports a small-business plugin growing from 31 to 44 skills across six categories. These counts, installation steps, connector coverage and provider capability claims are dated source claims, not verified current Anthropic state. The sponsor segment promotes Clay; no account, pricing, provider count or integration was tested.

The reusable architectural distinction is between a utility that finishes one concrete job and an orchestrator that chooses utilities, orders them and combines outputs. The speaker identifies overlapping trigger phrases between content strategy/social content engine and growth pulse/marketing Monday. His proposed remedy is a skill-routing audit with explicit boundaries. Consolidation is useful when several briefings compete for attention: retain a report someone acts on, rather than adding notification volume.

The transcript names build connector, build agent (described as skill creation), brand style, grow pipeline, speed to lead, social content engine, SEO/AI visibility, marketing Monday, proposal builder, contract review, Monday brief and inbox manager. It does not audibly enumerate every new skill or reproduce the on-screen comparison tables and prompts. Those visuals were not inspected. Claims that specific skills were removed or reorganized are attributed to the speaker.

Automate preparation, then put consequential judgment with the owner. Sales research, ranking, outreach drafts, finance preparation and briefing synthesis are examples. This source does not authorize sending outreach, installing plugins, or changing Joshua's approval rules.
