## What the source says

### Systems and iteration

The creator recommends extracting a reusable design system from an existing website and reusing it across technical and nontechnical outputs (0:00 to 1:22). Inline comments and drawings make feedback precise (9:10). He proposes divergent page or component variations followed by convergence on the preferred concept (10:19 to 11:15).

### Seventeen uses

The examples cover systems, animation, 3D assets with usage mockups, old-document rebranding, event flyers, colour/type comparisons, HTML email templates, inline feedback, funnel mapping, multiple variants, clickable wireframes, cross-team comments, templates, client-branded proposals, lead magnets, resumes and final copy cleanup. The email example adapts variables to the authentication backend (8:21 to 8:57).

### Limits and incentives

He distinguishes Design from general-purpose Code/Co-work/Chat, describes it as layout rather than native image generation (4:37), and recommends low-risk internal animations rather than an exact product launch video (1:32). This is an Anthropic-sponsored video with BuildPartner marketing. Productivity and tool-capability statements are the creator's claims, not independently tested facts.

## Application and limits

For the student dashboard, use concrete component references and a visible target, keep the approved charcoal/near-white system coherent, compare meaningful alternatives and inspect actual rendered motion. A clip does not justify adding every effect or tool. Joshua's existing voice and product decisions take priority over generic marketing prescriptions. These are application judgments, distinct from source claims.
