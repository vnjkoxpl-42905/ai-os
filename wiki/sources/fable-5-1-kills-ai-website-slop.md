# Premium AI websites come from references, layering and screenshot checks, not the model

Nate Herk's "Fable 5.1 FINALLY Kills AI Website Slop" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FFWtxjvW2ts), published 2026-09-01, clipped 2026-09-08). Full clip and transcript (about 16:12) read 2026-10-12. Evidence: [archived clip, provenance and hashes](../../raw/sources/clips/fable-5-1-kills-ai-website-slop/PROVENANCE.md), SHA-256 `8712c4afafb5dbfd3d4aa9a42c4dc9f606aaab4ad0d720d2b6a2a02b8c364070`. Follow-up video: [GPT-6 Astra](gpt-6-astra-kills-ai-website-slop.md).

The narrator sells a community and built the `scroll-craft` skill he promotes; read the claims with that in mind.

## What the video claims

- **"Make it premium" does not work (3:53).** The model needs something concrete: a reference site, named components, a clear effect.
- **Layering gives depth (4:15).** His favorite site splits the hero into separate planes (back mountain, middle mountain, laptop, front trees) that move at different rates on scroll.
- **Where he finds references (5:14, 7:25):** 21st.dev (components with copyable prompts), godly.website (whole sites and sections), Awwwards (award sites by industry), Motion Sites (animated backgrounds). For one product he had the model study a competitor's page and rebuild its feel with his brand.
- **His seven principles (10:41):** (1) brand guidelines; (2) the "three P's": the pain, the person, the promise; (3) scroll responsiveness, so the visitor feels feedback as they scroll, even subtle text reveals; (4) inspiration; (5) existing components to borrow; (6) verified mobile layout; (7) a verification loop where the agent screenshots the page frame by frame while scrolling and fixes what looks wrong.
- **Model matters less than the method (13:48).** With the same prompts, assets and skill, Fable 5 and 5.1 produced similar sites. He reports 5.1 used fewer tokens in each of his paired runs (for example 1,118 vs 1,737) and less of his weekly limit. These are his unaudited numbers.
- His own one-shot results still had flaws he pointed out (a hard cut at 10:04), so the examples are starting points, not finished sites.
