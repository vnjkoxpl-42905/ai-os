# Routines retain their ordered prescriptions and complete workout instructions

Ellen Cassidy / Loophole Online. Publication dates unknown. Captured from Joshua's authenticated browser on 2026-10-15 as private teacher research. Source filing does not adopt teaching claims, assign student work or publish vendor material.

The [routine library](../../corpus/loophole-online/routines/README.md) and [self-contained searchable catalog](../../corpus/loophole-online/routines/catalog.html) contain all 58 observed routines from three inventory pages, with 228 ordered schedule steps. Repeated steps and routine-specific timing, volume and modifications remain in their original order. Every primary README contains the full routine, all its steps, and complete canonical workout instructions, advice, examples, footnotes, related prep content and readable templates. Generic workout bodies are deduplicated within a routine, yielding 222 embedded bodies across the library. The [workout library](../../corpus/loophole-online/workouts/README.md) remains their canonical source home.

Seven supplemental expanded training-block captures and five newly referenced prep-map pages are included. The Uncut Method Vocab List PDF and its full extracted text are preserved; its readable text is inline in Abstract Answer Training Regimen. Canonical workout footnotes are explicitly attributed as reused source evidence for 152 associated schedule steps, rather than claimed as fresh routine-popup captures.

## Five source controls remain discoverable

Section Impact, Prep Stage, Challenge Index, Routine Progression and Useful Filters are mapped. The routine-specific Useful Filters are Level, Study Block Length and Workout Included. All observed choices remain available, including empty choices. Study Block Length uses actual live memberships (6, 37, 52, 58 and 0 for one through five-plus hours), not inferred duration ranges. All 89 captured membership sets match the generated mappings: 70 Workout Included, 11 progressions, five study blocks and three challenge buckets. All 58 routines have classifications; unknown mappings are empty.

## Source defects remain visible

Three schedule rows link to a source-generated undefined workout URL. Two are legitimate authored tasks in Abstract Answer Training Regimen whose instructions are preserved. Beginner Accuracy Routine (Day 1), step 3 supplies no authored instructions at all; the local document explicitly marks that blank and invents nothing. Therefore the complete-within-inventory flag is false even though every observed page and available schedule instruction is captured.

Seventeen routines have differences between the site's Workout Included filter and their actual ordered schedule. Both are preserved separately: the filter reproduces observed membership, while the schedule retains actual steps. These differences, the blank task, link defects and coverage are listed in the [verification report](../../corpus/loophole-online/routines/verification.generated.json).
