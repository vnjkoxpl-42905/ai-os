## What this batch contributes

This April-June2023 batch distinguishes costly pretraining from cheap PEFT/LoRA customization, but qualifies finetuning as a complex undertaking and advises not abandoning prompting too soon. He notes inference cost changes what optimal training means. Teaching commentary praises Socratic tutors that avoid giving answers, while emphasizing iteration/data/finetuning rather than an out-of-box capability. The tokenization post identifies trailing-space distribution shifts, spelling failures and tokenizer/model frequency mismatch as practical edge cases. Other posts advocate low-friction voice/I/O, rapid interactive prototypes and data quality/diversity over raw quantity. Personal leisure and local recommendations are dated observations, not rules for Joshua.
