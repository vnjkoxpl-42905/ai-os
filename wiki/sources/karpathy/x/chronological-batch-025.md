## What this batch contributes

nanochat unifies tokenizer, pretraining, midtraining, SFT, optional RL, inference and report card into a minimal baseline, with architectural choices initially based on single runs rather than exhaustive tuning. Tiny models cannot reliably reproduce a personal brain; RAG over writing is the accessible alternative to research-heavy finetuning. Dwarkesh follow-up explicitly asks small understandable changes, API-doc proof, fewer assumptions and learning instead of 1000-line autonomous returns. RL remains a layer, not something he wants removed; additional review-learning layers complement it. Synthetic identity data requires diversity and explicit capability facts. Recovery learning should not reward manufacturing errors; tokenizer regex replacement needs expert review and large tests.
