## What Karpathy argues

In the passage following “As for my take,” he frames pretraining as a practical cold-start response:

> TLDR: Pretraining is our crappy evolution.

He explicitly leaves room for disagreement:

> And I say both of these with double digit percent uncertainty and cheer the work of those who disagree, especially those a lot more ambitious bitter lesson wise.

The closing practical paragraph suggests exploring beyond prevailing optimization work:

> Probably we are still not sufficiently bitter lesson pilled and there is a very good chance of more powerful ideas and paradigms, other than exhaustive benchbuilding and benchmaxxing.

## Interpretation and limits

This supports retaining uncertainty when comparing AI paradigms, rather than turning one analogy into an established explanation. The opening exposition reports Sutton's viewpoint; it must not be attributed as Karpathy's own endorsed account without qualification. Biological comparisons and future directions are author arguments, not facts independently established here. The appendix preserves disagreements and uncertainty; linked conversations, podcast, prior writing and references remain separate unreviewed sources. The syndicated X version is not a second independent source supporting a rule.
