## What this source says

Karpathy labels two million selfies with an approximate engagement proxy controlling follower counts and tags, fine-tunes a VGG model and reports 60 percent validation accuracy. He inspects held-out rankings, representation clusters and crop search, exposing demographic/style correlations and crops that remove the person entirely. He reports unsuccessful spatial-transformer optimization and an impractical mobile port, then recommends learning through projects and writing about them.

## Interpretation and limits

The proxy is noisy popularity, not objective quality, attractiveness or fair personal assessment. Rankings reflect the sampled social context and cannot become normative demographic rules. The failed crop illustrates objective mismatch. Product, framework and learning-resource recommendations are historical. Photos, animations and live bot were not inspected; no model experiment was rerun. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article. Statements are attributed to the captured source; no changing external capability was verified live in this compilation.
