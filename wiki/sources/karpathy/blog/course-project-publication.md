## What this source says

This guest tutorial recounts the 2020 Fusical project and recommends choosing teammates and mentors, feasible measurable questions, existing data and baselines, regular collaboration, complete experiment records, top-to-bottom notebooks, clean public repositories and outsider review. It distinguishes a learning report with failed experiments from a publication argument supported by novelty, ablations and validation. The team reports approximately 64 percent test accuracy and publication despite not ranking in the top three.
