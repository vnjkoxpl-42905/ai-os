## What this source says

Karpathy demonstrates customizing a 2013 Twitter DOM with a Chrome extension: removing text, loading new tweets, charging unique tweet IDs to an author/retweeter, persisting a dictionary in extension storage and highlighting rare accounts or favorites. He explains the inspect-element-to-script feedback loop and local persistence across tab closure.
