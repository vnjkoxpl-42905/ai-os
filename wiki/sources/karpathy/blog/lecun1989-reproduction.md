## What this source says

Karpathy reproduces a 1989 handwritten-digit network in PyTorch, explicitly using resized MNIST substitutes and guessed details because the original data/specification were incomplete. He then changes loss, optimizer, augmentation and dropout while checking train/test behavior and isolated alternatives. More data also improves results. He distinguishes hardware speed, algorithmic changes and scaling, then speculates about future foundation-model use rather than training every target model from scratch.
