## What this source says

Karpathy recounts research rotations through unsupervised visual features, 3D object discovery and supervised video classification. AlexNet transfer changed his expectations and encouraged large supervised multi-task representations. He notices that sports classification could succeed from static appearance, defeating the intended need for rich motion features. He warns that human and computer data access differ, making human-learning analogies unreliable.

Short exact evidence from the [archived text](../../../../raw/sources/karpathy-brain/raw/blog/md/github-blog/karpathy.github.io_2014_07_03_feature-learning-escapades.md):

## Interpretation and limits

Check that task success requires the mechanism one intends to learn, and revise a research hypothesis when empirical alternatives work. These are 2014 views on visual learning, not a timeless ban on unsupervised or self-supervised learning. Later course self-supervision content qualifies that historical skepticism.
