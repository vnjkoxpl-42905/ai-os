## What this source says

Karpathy uses a year of personal weight-loss experiments to motivate self-study of metabolism, energy storage, ATP and mass accounting. He records intake, watch-estimated expenditure, scale weight and body-composition estimates, finds observed loss slower than a simple model predicts, and considers intake/expenditure error, conversion approximations, hydration and muscle change. Conflicting BIA/DEXA estimates leave the explanation unresolved. His reported biomarkers improved without a large subjective improvement.

## Interpretation and limits

The reusable epistemic pattern is explicit predictions, measured disagreement, multiple candidate explanations and unresolved uncertainty. The nutrition, fasting, radiation, device and physiology assertions are captured personal claims, not validated health advice. Do not apply his diet or self-experiments to Joshua or promote the strong BIA conclusion to universal fact. Figures, clinical literature and original measurements were not separately inspected; no medical recommendation is made. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article. Statements are attributed to the captured source; no changing external capability was verified live in this compilation.
