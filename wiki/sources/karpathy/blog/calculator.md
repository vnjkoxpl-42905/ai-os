## What this source says

Karpathy praises the calculator as a durable, self-contained arithmetic aid with minimal dependencies and no ongoing account, subscription or connectivity requirement. He contrasts this with products that demand setup, data access and recurring service relations. He presents the calculator as an ideal developers can approach along several dimensions, while acknowledging that it need not be practical for every product.

## Interpretation and limits

Prefer the smallest dependency footprint that still meets the job. This supports assessing ongoing operating burden, not banning all necessary online services or changing current subscriptions.
