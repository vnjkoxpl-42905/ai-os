## What this source says

Karpathy manually classified 400 CIFAR-10 images and reported about 94 percent accuracy. He describes ambiguity, varying viewpoints, local identifying parts and broader context, using the experience to understand the task and training-data coverage. An explicit 2015 update admits that his original projected performance ceiling was surpassed.

## Interpretation and limits

Inspect examples yourself and compare a measured human baseline with model behavior; retain corrections to forecasts. The sample is one person and 400 images, not a universal human benchmark. The 2011 and 2015 performance figures are historical. Images were not inspected. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article. Statements are attributed to the captured source; no changing external capability was verified live in this compilation.
