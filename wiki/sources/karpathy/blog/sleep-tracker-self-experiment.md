## What this source says

Karpathy records roughly two months of his own readings from Oura, Whoop, Eight Sleep and Apple Watch with AutoSleep, comparing distributions, pairwise correlations, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and perceived morning quality. He prefers Oura/Whoop in that experience, notes score saturation, unexplained measurement offsets and confounded lifestyle events, and distinguishes form-factor/app usability from scores.

## Interpretation and limits

The useful operating pattern is recording raw repeated measurements before optimizing and checking metric dynamic range. This is an uncontrolled single-person comparison. Agreement between trackers is not validation against sleep ground truth, and perceived productivity is not a causal clinical result. Do not promote device recommendations or sleep/fitness prescriptions into Joshua's canonical guidance. Charts and linked spreadsheet were not separately reviewed. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article. Statements are attributed to the captured source; no changing external capability was verified live in this compilation.
