## What this source says

The course tutorial walks through a historical EC2 GPU image with Caffe, Torch7, Theano, Keras, Lasagne, CUDA and cuDNN preinstalled. It covers account verification, instance selection, key-pair permissions, SSH, a Caffe timing command and storage. It warns that temporary storage does not persist and that GPU and disk charges continue unless resources are managed.
