## What this source says

Karpathy contrasts hand-written programs that automate specifiable transformations with learned programs optimized against an objective. He argues that verifiability predicts which tasks improve fastest under AI: an environment should permit repeated resettable, efficient attempts with an automated reward. He uses this to explain a jagged frontier between measurable tasks and harder-to-score creative, strategic or contextual tasks.

Short exact evidence from the [archived text](../../../../raw/sources/karpathy-brain/raw/blog/md/bearblog/karpathy.bearblog.dev_verifiability.md):

> Software 2.0 easily automates what you can verify.

## Interpretation and limits

For automation, define a repeatable attempt and a checkable outcome. This is an argument about optimization opportunity, not proof that any rubric is correct or that all human judgment should be automated.
