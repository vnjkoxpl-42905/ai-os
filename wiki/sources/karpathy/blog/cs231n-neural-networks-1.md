## What this source says

The course introduces neural networks as matrix transformations interleaved with nonlinearities, then qualifies the biological neuron analogy as very coarse. It compares saturation, centering, dying ReLUs, leaky variants and Maxout, explains layer conventions and parameter counts, and distinguishes universal approximation from learnable useful structure. Capacity and regularization are presented separately from architecture size.
