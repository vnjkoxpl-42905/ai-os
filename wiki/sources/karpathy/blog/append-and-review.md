## What this source says

Karpathy keeps one Apple Notes text note. New thoughts and tasks are appended at the top with little metadata, except occasional watch/listen/read markers. Periodic review rescues relevant entries toward the top, merges related thoughts and leaves lower-priority ideas available farther down. The stated benefit is low capture friction and confidence that an idea can be revisited without occupying working memory.

Transfer low-friction capture and deliberate review to the existing inbox/hot-cache workflow.
