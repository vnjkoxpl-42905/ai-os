## What this source says

Karpathy argues that animal and LLM intelligence arise under different objectives. He contrasts survival, embodiment and social selection with text imitation, reinforcement-learning task rewards and commercial user feedback. He links this difference to jagged capability and warns against reasoning about language models solely as animals.

Short exact evidence from the [archived text](../../../../raw/sources/karpathy-brain/raw/blog/md/bearblog/karpathy.bearblog.dev_the-space-of-minds.md):
