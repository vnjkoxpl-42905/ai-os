## What this source says

Karpathy highlights verifiable-reward reinforcement learning, jagged non-animal intelligence, vertical LLM applications, local agents, vibe coding and visual interfaces. He describes an application layer that assembles context, orchestrates calls, provides human interaction and adjusts autonomy. He questions benchmark generalization when training optimizes nearby environments and distinguishes local access to an existing workspace from where model compute happens.
