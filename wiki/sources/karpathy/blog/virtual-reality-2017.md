## What this source says

Karpathy describes excitement about VR followed by low repeat use of his Vive. He contrasts impressive first demos with durable experiences, identifies setup and interruption costs, expensive shallow ports and nausea-producing errors, and favors creative, social and full-body interactions that exploit the medium. He predicts successful uses would address recurring human needs and offer value beyond existing devices.

Short exact evidence from the [archived text](../../../../raw/sources/karpathy-brain/raw/blog/md/medium/karpathy.medium.com_virtual-reality-still-not-quite-there-again-5f51f2b43867.md):

> What **is** difficult is making one that a person wants to come back to

## Interpretation and limits

A useful product criterion is repeat willingness under real friction, not an impressed first reaction. This is a 2017 personal observation and speculative forecast, not a current VR market survey, validated UX study or present buying recommendation. His percentages and price examples were not independently reproduced, and screenshots were not inspected. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article. Statements are attributed to the captured source; no changing external capability was verified live in this compilation.
