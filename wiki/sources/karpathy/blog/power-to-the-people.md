## What this source says

Karpathy argues that inexpensive broadly capable LLMs initially benefited individuals disproportionately by making unfamiliar tasks approachable, while organizations face integrations, coordination, risk and inertia. He describes a possible future tension between compute-intensive improvements that widen capability gaps and distillation that narrows them.

Short exact evidence from the [archived text](../../../../raw/sources/karpathy-brain/raw/blog/md/bearblog/karpathy.bearblog.dev_power-to-the-people.md):

## Interpretation and limits

Use broad assistance to extend an individual's reach while retaining checks for reliability and context.
