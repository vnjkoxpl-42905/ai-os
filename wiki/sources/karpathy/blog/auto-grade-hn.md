## What this source says

Karpathy describes collecting December 2015 Hacker News front pages, full articles and comment threads, asking a model to compare predictions with later outcomes, parsing fixed-format grades and interest scores, and publishing static HTML. He first tried one thread manually before expanding to 31 days of 30 articles. The article gives his prompt and reports 930 API queries at about $58.
