## What this source says

Karpathy matches ICLR 2017 submissions to arXiv by exact title and compares conference decisions with arxiv-sanity library additions. Many submissions do not match, and oral rankings diverge while broad acceptance/rejection shows agreement. He proposes inexpensive bottom-up interest as a useful signal, while acknowledging unequal exposure, publication timing, author prominence and rich-get-richer effects. A suggested future citation comparison is not completed here.
