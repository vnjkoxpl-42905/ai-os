## What this source says

Karpathy reflects on his computer-vision PhD: finding a worthwhile problem is a separate outer loop from solving it, and research taste depends on importance, feasibility, fertile follow-on work and transferable skills. He discusses advisor fit and fallibility, field service beyond publication counts, learning from good and bad papers, one contribution per paper, concrete paragraph points, controlled evidence and reproducible code. His thesis crystallized only in the last 1.5 years after exploration. Talks should excite listeners about a problem and leave actionable understanding.

## Interpretation and limits

This is a 2016 personal academic guide, not universal educational or career advice. The transferable communication pattern is explaining the problem, limitation, proposed mechanism and evidence rather than listing procedural steps. Academic formatting heuristics and opportunity-cost claims are context-dependent. Linked papers, videos and actual figures were not reviewed; his institutional judgments do not become Joshua policy. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article. Statements are attributed to the captured source; no changing external capability was verified live in this compilation.
