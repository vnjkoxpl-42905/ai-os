## What this source says

Karpathy describes ulogme, a local free/open-source activity collector plus web visualization, using active-window titles and typing frequency over nearly three months. He compares single-day and longitudinal patterns, annotates activity and notices fewer actual coding hours than his initial impression. He admits missing laptop activity, unimplemented causal analysis and that computer time alone would not establish productivity after breaks.

Short exact evidence from the [archived text](../../../../raw/sources/karpathy-brain/raw/blog/md/github-blog/karpathy.github.io_2014_08_03_quantifying-productivity.md):

## Interpretation and limits

Instrument a meaningful outcome and acknowledge coverage/proxy limits. Keystrokes and active-window time are activity proxies, not universal productivity metrics; his one-paper writing statistics are personal examples. This does not authorize installing monitoring software. The historical article says Windows was unsupported. Screenshots were not inspected. The companion HTML is a format counterpart, not an independent supporting article.
