## What this source says

Karpathy analyzes keyword incidence in an arXiv-sanity collection of 28,303 papers over five years. He normalizes mentions by paper totals, compares frameworks, ConvNet architectures, optimizers and researcher names, and ranks changing unigram/bigram frequencies. He explicitly notes that arXiv submission coverage changes over time, citations can mention a tool without using it, Adam collides with personal names, and Bengio combines two researchers.
