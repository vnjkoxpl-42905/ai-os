## What this source says

> Your very first run should always be to establish the baseline

The instructions fix the evaluation harness, restrict edits to train.py, compare validation bits per byte within a fixed five-minute training budget, and record keep/discard/crash outcomes. They explicitly prefer simpler code at equal performance.

## Interpretation and limits

These are project-specific research instructions, not permission to start indefinite training here. CPU/GPU compatibility, metric outcomes and the implementation were not executed.
