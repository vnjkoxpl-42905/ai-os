## What this source says

> the LLM identities are anonymized

The app gathers independent answers, anonymizes the answers for peer ranking, and asks a chairman to synthesize the result. It stores conversation JSON locally. The author explicitly describes it as an unsupported Saturday exploration. Setup uses OpenRouter and purchased credits.

## Interpretation and limits

Independent opinions and anonymization are useful patterns; agreement and ranking do not establish factual correctness. Nothing was installed and no paid calls were made.
