## What the demonstration requires

At 4:10 the narrator describes retaining raw evidence: "The raw files don't get touched". Prompt 2 asks for an index, log, source pages, principles, methods and a hot page under 500 words. Prompt 3 requires rules supported in at least two distinct places. Prompt 4 limits the teaching agent to five wiki pages and requires execution reporting. Prompt 5 scopes a stop gate to Karpathy work. The actual sixth visible command teaches a tiny byte-pair tokenizer; a separate numbered sixth card was not verified.
