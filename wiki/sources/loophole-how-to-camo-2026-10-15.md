## What the instructor says

- At 0:11–1:02, Camo produces a review list containing every missed question plus an undisclosed number of originally correct decoys. A second attempt separates conceptual gaps, misreads, self-doubt and self-confidence according to the two answer outcomes.
- At 2:12–3:30, she recommends waiting at least 24 hours before Camo, with a later review day as an alternative, to encourage a fresh attempt rather than rushing to a score.
- At 3:37–4:16, she places Camo at the end of that day's preparation to reduce reactive changes to the next section.
- At 4:24–5:26, she describes avoiding premature answer and score reveals in the then-current LawHub interface. This is an attributed source prescription, not a current LawHub UI verification.
- At 5:33–7:02, she asks for a genuine new attempt at the entire question, including Translation, CLIR, the stem and all answer choices. The aim is to answer every review question correctly rather than preserve an original choice out of loyalty.
- At 7:07–8:20, she recommends completing the Wrong Answer Journal alongside Camo while the questions are fresh, and discusses restarting the habit if a backlog prevents any journaling.
