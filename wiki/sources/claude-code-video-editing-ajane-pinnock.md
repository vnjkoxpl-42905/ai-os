# Ajane Pinnock's Claude Code video-editing workflow: iterative previews become reusable skills

Ajane Pinnock's YouTube video, ["How I Edit My Videos Using Claude Code (Fully Automated)"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vpAWIF6OZkU), was captured 2026-10-21. The inbox clip does not provide a publication date, so it remains unknown. The full description and timestamped transcript (0:00–27:14) were read. The creator links a paid editing-skills pack and describes her own workflow; the transcript is auto-captioned.

## What the source says

- **Build in stages (5:53–12:33):** the creator describes trying Remotion, Premiere Pro, then Hyperframes, and refining her workflow over several iterations. She says the workflow transcribes footage, makes a tight cut, adds word-timed captions and optional graphics, B-roll, and sound effects.
- **Give concrete editorial feedback (12:34–16:52):** examples include matching B-roll to cuts, adjusting caption length and placement, choosing source footage, and giving visual references. She says she refined the output through about 15 versions.
- **Turn accepted work into reusable skills (16:53–20:07):** she says she packages scripts, brand assets, style rules, and workflow steps into separate editing skills for different video formats.
- **Require a preview and review (20:08–21:33):** her stated workflow renders a lower-quality preview first, lets her leave timestamped notes, and renders a final file only after feedback.
- **Keep human review and storage safeguards (24:23–27:14):** she says taste and watching the draft still matter. She also recommends asking for a read-only storage analysis before moving or deleting files.

The demonstration uses tools including Claude Code, Hyperframes, Remotion, Premiere Pro and transcription services. Her preference for Hyperframes and claims about speed, quality, and automation are personal and not independently tested here.
