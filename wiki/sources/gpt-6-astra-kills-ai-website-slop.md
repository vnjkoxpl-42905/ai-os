# Slop is also a site built for the wrong visitor

Nate Herk's "GPT-6 Astra FINALLY Kills AI Website Slop" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhmhUgccaS0), published 2026-09-04, three days after his [Fable 5.1 video](fable-5-1-kills-ai-website-slop.md); clipped 2026-09-11). Full clip and transcript (about 8:35) read 2026-10-12. Evidence: [archived clip, provenance and hashes](../../raw/sources/clips/gpt-6-astra-kills-ai-website-slop/PROVENANCE.md), SHA-256 `2e8c7c2b2b0beaf660f61db25bd14198305adaeae0a067648dd94b453d69ae21`.

## What the video claims

- **He now prefers GPT-6 Astra for design (0:00 to 1:59).** Its one-shot sites felt 3D and immersive without being overwhelming, and varied by business type instead of repeating one template. He still rates his Fable-built site higher because he iterated on it about 15 times. All examples used his `scroll-craft` skill.
- **Motion graphics (3:04).** Two prompts and 20 to 30 minutes produced a HyperFrames promo with music sync plus a vertical version.
- **A 152 GB event recap (4:14).** Given a footage folder, Astra produced a one-minute sizzle reel in about 35 minutes, finding logos and brand assets itself. One wrong domain appeared on screen. He says Soul and Fable 5.1 did worse on the same task.
- **The second kind of slop (7:25).** Besides generic buttons, fonts and gradients, a site is slop when it feels made for someone else: a 3D scroll world is wrong for an older visitor buying medication and right for a teenager buying headphones. Hence the three P's (pain, person, promise) before any effect.
- **Method (8:04):** brand guidelines, copy, inspiration sites (godly.website, 21st.dev, Awwwards by category), the skill, then iterate by scrolling and naming what to fix.
