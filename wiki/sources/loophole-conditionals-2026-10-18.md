## Keep the verification limits visible

All final native caption cues end within **1.27 seconds** of measured media duration, with no suspicious trailing caption gap. This checks capture timing, not word accuracy. Caption grouping differs between the site-rendered transcript and native VTT. Their wording can differ slightly, and both versions are preserved. No word-by-word listening verification has been performed.

Frames sample at intervals of at most 45 seconds, plus 3%, 50% and 90% of runtime. They are a visual reference, not a guarantee that every brief whiteboard change was sampled. The full original videos remain available.

One historical response mismatch remains explicit: in the Unless quiz, question 2's earlier submitted-B record captured a selected-A response state. The raw history is preserved; the readable supplement labels that historical feedback attribution unverified. The independently observed final B success is valid, and all 78 final-answer records have successful site-response proof. This proves the site's responses, not independent pedagogical correctness or every alternative wrong-answer comment.
