# Use a taste vault, compare design families, then refine one direction

**Source:** This New Astra Web Design Workflow Is INSANE. Author: Chase AI. [Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XK6S1hbYv7I). Publication and capture dates unknown. Ingested 2026-10-24.

**Evidence:** [preserved complete clip](../../raw/sources/clips/astra-web-design-taste-vault-chase-ai/source.md), [provenance and hash](../../raw/sources/clips/astra-web-design-taste-vault-chase-ai/PROVENANCE.md). Status: captured and text-compiled, not independently tested.

## What the source says

- At 1:53–4:01 he collects screenshots into a taste vault grouped by design family. References include Pinterest, Twitter, Landbook and Dribbble. Specific visual examples replace vague adjectives.

- At 4:49–7:43 he compares several design families and separately compares baseline output with frontend-design, Impeccable and Taste. His demonstration uses five families times four skill configurations, not a mandated count for every project.

- At 8:22–11:25 he says baseline output also looked strong to him. He chooses a family, asks for distinct variations within it, and only then moves to small edits. The point is to judge visible output rather than assume more skills always improve it.

- At 13:17–15:23 he adds a customizable tweak panel for typography, imagery, grid, accent, footer and motion settings, with save/reset, entrance replay and comparison against an untouched original.

- At 15:54–17:24 he browses specific components on 21st.dev and adapts a component aesthetic across a coherent page. At 17:24–18:20 he describes temporary hosted sharing, explicitly distinguishing it from production.

## Application here, separated from source claims

This closely supports the requested design folder: keep visual examples, reasons, sources and design-family labels together while linking to existing skills and repos. It does not justify overriding Joshua’s accepted charcoal palette or generating twenty versions by default.

## Actionable proposals

- Create the requested navigable taste/reference library with screenshots, family tags, selection reasons and provenance.
- Compare a small number of meaningfully different compositions, then refine the chosen family rather than endlessly switching themes.
- Retain an untouched baseline and add optional tweak controls/replay for efficient comparison.
- Browse actual component demonstrations and adapt them to a coherent design instead of accumulating unrelated widgets.
- Existing Taste, Impeccable and frontend-design availability should be checked before proposing another installation.
