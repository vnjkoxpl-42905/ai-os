## What the source says

1. **Give a goal, not only a prescribed solution (0:31–3:18).** The creator argues that stating the desired outcome lets Claude suggest approaches beyond the user's first idea. He says specific tasks still have a place.
2. **Plan visually for complex work (3:20–6:39).** He recommends using an HTML page or artifact with questions, diagrams, and mockups when a project involves several choices. He says a short task can stay in chat and acknowledges the extra token cost.
3. **Provide access, context, and feedback (8:20–14:31).** He frames useful context as decision-making principles, not just facts, and recommends feedback loops alongside verification. His broad suggestion to expand access is not a blanket permission rule.
4. **Build and visualize task-specific harnesses (14:32–17:09).** He describes sub-agents and dynamic workflows as ways to structure work and recommends visualizing complex flows.

His closing caution is to stay willing to discard short-lived tools and workflows as technology changes (17:28–19:59). That is advice from the creator, not a sourced claim about the future.
