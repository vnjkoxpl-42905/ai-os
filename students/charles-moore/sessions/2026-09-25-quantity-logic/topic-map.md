## Source caution

Gemini says the quantity hierarchy “flows forward from some to most to all.” The transcript's valid implication runs **all ⇒ most ⇒ some**, assuming a nonempty starting group. A `some` claim can *allow* all; it never proves most or all. The recap uses this distinction. Joshua also describes a “some followed by most” extension loosely; no general inference follows from `some A are B` and `most B are C` alone.
