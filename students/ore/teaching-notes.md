## 2026-09-11 | Make argument structure automatic

Source: [[transcript-downloads/2026-09-11-ore-notes-by-gemini]]; [[sessions/2026-09-11-argument-structure/transcript]].

- Joshua emphasized finding the main conclusion before advanced assumption work. The reliable check is whether the claim is the author's opinion and another statement explicitly supports it. Premise indicators and pivot words are useful clues, but the support relationship decides the role.
- They distinguished main conclusion, intermediate conclusion, premise, opposing viewpoint, and background. An intermediate conclusion is both supported and supporting. This distinction matters when a later question asks where the argument's gap lies.
- Oreoluwa described an evening-study pattern that was leaving her fatigued. Joshua recommended a smaller, sustainable morning routine. This is a study-scheduling observation, not a medical judgment.
- Oreoluwa reported LSAT U explanation popup trouble and a LawHub Chrome login issue. The transcript also records platform feature promises. Track these separately from teaching assignments and verify their current status in the live system.
