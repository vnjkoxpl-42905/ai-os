# Kaden Forges — Teaching notes

Distilled from the single Gemini session note. Append only; never rewrite.

## [2026-05-19] Argument fundamentals

First-principles session on argument structure. The through-line: read
structurally, not interpretively.

- Premises versus conclusions identified by **structure**, not by personal
  reading. Whatever the stimulus asserts is accepted as true regardless of
  whether he agrees with it.
- **FABS** as the premise-indicator mnemonic: for, after all, because, since.
- Conclusion indicators drilled separately: therefore, accordingly, it follows
  that.
- Isolating the core argument from background noise, then rephrasing it in his
  own words without looking at the original. Rephrasing was the main skill
  being built.

## Goal and cadence set that session

- Target: a score in the **170s**.
- Study cadence agreed: **3 to 5 days per week**, with consistent platform
  engagement.

## What to know before any next session

Only one session on record, and it was foundational. If he returns, confirm
whether the FABS and conclusion-indicator lists actually got memorized and
whether the rephrasing habit stuck, before building anything on top.
