Conditional statement mechanics and diagramming
* Conditional statements act as hypothetical rules with sufficient and necessary conditions.
* Contrapositives require reversing the terms and negating both sides of the conditional arrow.
* Keywords like if, when, and every introduce sufficient conditions on the left.
* Keywords like only and only when introduce necessary conditions on the right.
* Conditional statements with unless require negating the attached term for the sufficient side.
