# Adriana Varea — Teaching notes

Distilled from the four Gemini session notes. Append only; never rewrite.

## The core diagnosis

Her problem was never comprehension. A 174 blind review against a lower timed
score means the understanding is there and the loss is happening under time
pressure, driven by confidence rather than knowledge. She was missing questions
she could solve correctly when the clock came off. Everything below follows from
that.

## [2026-05-11] Practice-test review, LR flaw and weakening

- Established the pattern above: efficiency and confidence, not ability.
- Flaw questions: isolate the conclusion first; watch for moral-concept
  mismatches, which were the recurring trap.
- Taught a filtration method for eliminating answer choices quickly rather than
  evaluating each on its merits.
- Argument dissection: evidence indicators locate premises and conclusions, and
  that structure filters invalid options by scope and by definitive language.

## [2026-05-18] Reading Comprehension, inference, pacing

- Passage structure and viewpoint mapping, using a vertical annotation system
  to hold focus.
- Inference questions must be grounded in text. Eliminate answers with excessive
  scope or unsupported conditional claims.
- **Key heuristic, decided this session:** prefer the answer with the lowest
  burden of evidence. Claims needing more textual support than exists are
  disqualified.
- Pacing work: run a set while tracking metrics to find her optimal speed, and
  watch that precision does not fall as speed rises.

## [2026-05-25] Necessary assumption, comparatives, symmetry breakers

- Comparative arguments: identifying the symmetry breaker is what weakens the
  comparison.
- Recurring difficulty isolated: net-effect reasoning. Practice was pointed at
  symmetry-breaker application specifically.
- **Decision (logged as ALIGNED in the notes):** treat outlier level-5 questions
  as acceptable lost points and keep pacing, rather than burning time on them.
  This is the pacing fix applied as strategy.

## [2026-06-02] RC framework shift, final pre-exam session

- **Moved her off ViewStamp to a Comparison / Explanation / Evaluation /
  Recommendation framework** for RC. Clarifies authorial intent and makes
  paragraph categorization fast.
- Applied it live to practice passages.
- Correct answers usually turn on identifying authorial *evaluation*; the trap
  is confusing correlation with causation.
- Avoid absolute language; watch test-maker sensitivities.
- Established the study plan for the final days before her exam.

## What to know before any next session

She is a high-ability student whose entire gap was pacing and nerve. If she
returns, do not restart from fundamentals. Pick up at the CEER framework for RC
and at symmetry breakers and net-effect reasoning in LR, which were the live
edges when the sessions stopped.
