### Summary

**Conclusion Identification is Key**  
Failure to accurately identify the exact conclusion is the main source of error, requiring mastery of the conclusion's precise meaning beyond just the argument's general feeling. Evidence is taken as true, and the focus must be on whether this evidence guarantees the conclusion without a conceptual restatement.

**Locating and Rephrasing Conclusions**  
The main conclusion is an opinion supported by at least one other statement, often appearing early or in a rebuttal structure that must be positively rephrased. Analysis of the hardware store question confirmed the conditional logic supported the conclusion that no store would open. The earthquake question conclusion identified a gap in the current theory because the expected post-quake heat was not detected.
