LSAT Argument Structure and Logic Fundamentals
* Joshua defined an argument as the relationship between a conclusion (the author's opinion) and a premise (supporting points).

* Answer choices containing "not" or "cannot" are strong indicators for the correct response in necessary assumption questions.
* Joshua noted that if a new concept appears in the conclusion but not the evidence, the correct answer likely links that specific concept.
