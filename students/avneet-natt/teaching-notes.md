# Avneet Natt Teaching Notes

## [2026-09-08] Necessary assumptions

- Avneet distinguished sufficient assumptions, which guarantee the conclusion when added, from necessary assumptions, which the argument requires to survive. Joshua separated linking assumptions from defender assumptions that rule out an objection.
- On the art-judgment question, the missing link was between passing judgment and the stated choices of praising or dismissing; a new term in the conclusion supplied a target for the assumption. She first selected A and then worked through the link.
- On the pheromone and economic-reduction questions, she identified causal structure and selected B and A respectively after considering objections.
- On the tillage question, the core risk was reverse causation: erosion might lead farmers to till deeply. Answer C's denial of other viable tilling methods was unnecessary because the conclusion did not require ruling out every alternative. The negation of A threatened the proposed causal direction. Joshua used cough drops and coughing as a parallel example.
- Avneet answered most of the practice questions correctly. Next, check whether she can independently name the conclusion, predict the missing link or objection, and use the negation test without making an answer stronger than necessary.
