## 2026-09-17 | Diagnose the argument before choosing

Source: [[transcript-downloads/2026-09-17-gianna-notes-by-gemini]]; [[sessions/2026-09-17-session/transcript]].

- On a campaign-mailing question, she first selected B. Joshua had her restate each claim, distinguish a factual report from a claim about the campaign's purpose, and ask what explicitly supported the author's claim.
