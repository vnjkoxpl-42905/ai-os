**Four moves an author makes**

- Comparing, explaining, evaluating, recommending. Evaluating and recommending usually carry the main point, with recommending taking precedence. The side the passage spends more space on is usually the author's.

**Tulip-market passage (Mackay and Garber)**

- RC answers are Must Be True statements: 100% provable from the text, softer language preferred.
- Analogy questions can be arbitrary; do not overspend on them. Celia chose the correct analogy (a publisher paying a high price for a novel and selling cheap copies).

- Question stems are defined, semi-defined, or undefined, which guides how to predict.
- Answers match meaning, not wording. A verbatim restatement is a predictable trap.
