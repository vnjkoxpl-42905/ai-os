Sufficient Assumption Methodology
* The primary goal for sufficient assumption questions is to construct a logical bridge between premises and the conclusion.
* Strategy involves identifying the specific subject and attributed judgment in the conclusion, then defining the criteria to validate that judgment.
* Utilize the 'POC bridge' technique by linking the final premise concept directly to the new concept introduced in the conclusion.
* Prioritize answer choices with strong, universal language (e.g., 'must', 'all') over weak qualifiers like 'can' or 'may'.
