Conditional logic session covering sufficient and necessary conditions through rules and examples.

**What a conditional is**

- Joshua introduced conditionals as rules or laws: a sufficient trigger guarantees a necessary outcome. A trigger is one guaranteed way to the outcome, not necessarily the only way.
- The contrapositive is the only other logically equivalent statement. Find it by flipping the two sides and negating both.

**Reading the arrow**

- Only the left side yields a deduction. Knowing the right side happened says nothing about the left.
- Indicator words place concepts: "if", "every", "any", "all" mark the sufficient side; "must" and "required" mark the necessary side.

**The five-step process**

- Identify the indicator, label the two concepts, apply the left and right rules, form the contrapositive, state the relationship back in English.
- Sandra practiced it on the Rwanda passport rule, "All apples are sweet", an Ethiopia colonization sentence, a negative requirement for becoming a doctor, and flamingo and penguin categories.
