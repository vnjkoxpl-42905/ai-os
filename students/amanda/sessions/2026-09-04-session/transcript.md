Necessary assumption question strategies
* Assumptions are defined as implicit premises required for an argument's conclusion to be logically valid.
* Positive linking assumptions represent ideas the author must affirm; negative defender assumptions rule out potential objections.
* Prioritize positive assumptions for conditional logic and repeating concepts; prioritize negative assumptions for causal or comparative arguments.
* New concepts appearing in the conclusion are critical indicators; the correct answer almost always includes them.
* The negation technique is highly effective for testing defender assumptions, particularly when answer choices include not or cannot.
