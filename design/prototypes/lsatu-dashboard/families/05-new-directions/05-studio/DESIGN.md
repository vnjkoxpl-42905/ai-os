# The study studio

Light ambient workspace for a student at a desk during the day. Warm porcelain canvas, cobalt active practice, coral tutoring, lilac reference library, ink text. Expressive product design with one sculptural focal point.

Space Grotesk headlines and numerals, Manrope controls, restrained Newsreader italic for editorial notes. Three scale levels, contrasting white and colored surfaces, shared alignment with asymmetrical two-column composition. Native controls and dialogs; local SVG data marks and original canvas sculpture. 180-240ms interactive transitions, one short artwork arrival, no continuous decorative animation.
