# Product

## Register
product

## Users
LSAT U students returning to assigned reasoning and reading work, preparing for a session with Joshua, retrieving questions and reviewing practice. Established in the inspected 04-refined HTML, app.js and session handoff.

## Product Purpose
Make the next useful action immediately available. Local interactive demonstration, no live account or test bank connection. Student and teacher experiences remain distinct.

## Brand Personality
Confident, inventive, thoughtful. The current user explicitly rejects monotony and gives full creative control over palette, hierarchy and layout.

## Anti-references
The previous gray editorial page, identical thin-lined sections, tiny supporting copy, beige luxury templates, and visual spectacle that hides the actual work.

## Design Principles
One strong focal point. Color communicates work types. Retrieval and resuming require few steps. Every displayed control has a real local interaction. Demonstration data stays labelled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, visible focus, readable contrast, touch targets, reduced-motion alternatives and responsive single-column phone layouts.
