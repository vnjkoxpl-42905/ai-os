# Find a product screen by platform and use case

Use this supplied sample to compare a specific interface decision, such as onboarding, a list, checkout, or a dashboard. Search this page for the platform, use case, or app, then open the matching image. The use-case and app labels below are derived from the supplied filenames, not independently verified descriptions. These are still screenshots: they do not establish interactions or complete flows. Claude and Codex can both read this page and open the same local files. For design work, start with [the design hub](README.md) and its relevant principles.

The supplied [archive](../raw/sources/mobbin-sample-pack-100/mobbin-sample-pack-100.zip), [README and rights note](../raw/sources/mobbin-sample-pack-100/README.txt), and [provenance](../raw/sources/mobbin-sample-pack-100/PROVENANCE.md) are preserved. The README says these are Mobbin-curated screenshots of other companies' interfaces for personal design reference and inspiration only; do not resell or republish them as your own work. The pack supplies no individual source URLs or separate licence. It is a reference collection, not an installable component library.
