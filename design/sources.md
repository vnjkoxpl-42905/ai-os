# Follow a clip to its preserved evidence

The [catalogue](catalog.generated.md) collects canonical source summaries, design references and local research. Each ingested source points to immutable evidence and its SHA-256. Read its limitations before treating a video claim as a verified capability.

The 2026-10-24 batch covers the design-relevant inbox clips, including changed generations and exact duplicates. [Batch report and exclusions](../research/2026-10-24-design-hub/REPORT.md) records which were new, already complete, revised, or unrelated. Inbox originals remain in place; presence in the inbox does not mean pending ingestion.

New sources use the existing [ingest skill](../.agents/skills/second-brain-ingest/SKILL.md). Proposed actions remain in the [AI queue](../references/ai-workspace/action-queue.md) or [LSAT U queue](../wiki/lsatu-idea-queue.md), with source links rather than a third queue here.
