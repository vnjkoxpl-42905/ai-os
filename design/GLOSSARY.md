# Design glossary

Shared words for visual direction, agreed with Joshua. One meaning per term. Add a term only once he has agreed it.

## Layout and pieces

**Widget**
A self-contained piece that answers one question at a glance through a picture or one prominent value, rather than rows of text to read.
_Avoid_: card (when used for any box), panel, section

**Hero instrument**
A large, live, purpose-built visual that shows where things stand (time, progress, place in the day) and leads to an action. The eclipse on teacher Home is the reference example. Valued for being innovative and genuinely useful, not decorative.
_Avoid_: hero banner, hero image, decoration

**Bento grid**
A layout where widgets of different sizes fit together in one grid; size signals importance.
_Avoid_: dashboard (too vague), stacked sections

**Score bars**
One small bar per question, filled for correct, so a section's shape is visible as well as its score.
_Avoid_: progress bar, sparkline

**Tactile**
Pieces read as physical objects: a lit top edge, a soft contact shadow, lift on hover and press in on click.
_Avoid_: skeuomorphic, glossy, 3D

**Dock**
A floating bar pinned to the bottom of the screen holding the most-used jumps, with one lit primary action.
_Avoid_: toolbar, bottom nav, tab bar

## Relationships

- A page has a **Bento grid** of **Widgets**; the largest compartment holds a **Hero instrument**.
- **Tactile** describes how every **Widget** and the **Dock** feel, not a separate piece.
