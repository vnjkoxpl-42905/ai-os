# Unless Drill

**Four unless sentences, against the clock. Repeat it until you clear 5 seconds.**

This is the warm-up for everything we did on conditionals. You can't link rules or apply them to facts if turning the sentence into a rule is slow.

The rule from our session: **choose either idea, negate it, and put it on the left. Take the other idea and put it on the right, exactly as written.** Either choice is correct. One gives you the rule, the other gives you its contrapositive.

The trap this drill catches is **negating both ideas**. Only one gets a "not." The other stays exactly as it was.

One habit: say the finished rule as an if-then sentence in your head before you lock it in. If it doesn't sound like something the original sentence says, fix it.

Clear 10 seconds first, all four correct in one round. That unlocks 5 seconds.
