# Joey Luckett, 2026-03-04

## Teaching moves
- **Pulls live data before diagnosing anything.** As a 7Sage tutor, Joshua asked Joey to paste his 7Sage analytics URL into the chat, then screen-shared the account and read his PT history back to him in real time.
- **Normalizes a low starting point immediately**: "you're just getting started. There's nothing that's out of the norm from what I see. You're not uniquely good or bad."
- **The unknown unknowns framing**, his central diagnostic idea and the argument for tutoring: "it's not so much about what you don't know, because you might say I'm not good at this specific thing and that's normal, you can target that. The biggest issue people have with the LSAT is the unknown unknowns. It's what you don't know that you don't know. If it's not even on your radar, good luck."
- **Sells the value as time saved, not score gained**: "I would have gotten there within a third of the time, just not wasting my time floundering."
- **Honest about the platform he tutors on.** On 7Sage's paid core curriculum: "it's questionable. It's usually pretty generic. It gives you something to do, but is it efficient? I would say probably not." Confirmed they can work entirely on the free tier and offered to compile practice test PDFs.
- **Cuts a price concession unprompted and explains why in personal terms**: extends every one-hour session to an hour and twenty minutes at the one-hour rate, "I come from a pretty big family myself, so I know financially things aren't always easy... the LSAT costs money, applying to law school costs money, that can be quite burdensome."
- **Volunteers verification before being pressed hard**: offered his score report, a former student reference, or a student TikTok video. Screen-shared the report during the call and emailed the PDF.
- **Deliberately withholds information to avoid overwhelm**: mid-sentence on accommodations, "you know what, I won't just spit out a bunch at you now, because I don't want this to overwhelm you." Defers a full accommodations walkthrough to session one.
- **Praises initiative specifically**: "you did your work up front, which is good, because a lot of students don't even know that there are accommodations. They come to me and say I have ADHD and I say, did you look into the accommodations? You did all that."
- **Names consistency as the operative variable**: "consistency is going to be your best friend. Deviating takes a lot away."
- **Sequences the plan out loud**: deep dive into analytics, then likely core-curriculum foundations ("that usually goes by pretty quickly"), then a drilling phase with practice sections.
