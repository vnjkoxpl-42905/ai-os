Logical reasoning question types and strategies
* Most Strongly Supported and Must Be True questions consist of statement lists lacking conclusions.
* Softer and weaker claims such as at least one respect are more easily supported than specific claims.
* Method of reasoning questions require identifying specific argument forms like conditional, analogical, or causal reasoning.
* Parallel flaw questions require matching the exact conclusion type and logical structure of the stimulus.
* Argument part questions require distinguishing between refuting an underlying tactic versus refuting an individual motivation.
