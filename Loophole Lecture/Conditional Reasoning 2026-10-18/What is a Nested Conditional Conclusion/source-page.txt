Ellen's Notes

Nested Conditionals Inside a Conclusion

If there's a conditional inside the conclusion, don't Loophole the stimulus by negating the sufficient condition of the conclusion. The author is only claiming the conclusion as rule that must be followed in a world in which it gets activated.

If the sufficient condition is not happening, the conclusion would just be dormant, but it wouldn't be disproven. Instead, show that even in the world in which the sufficient is activated, the necessary condition doesn't have to follow. This is what would actually disprove the conditional relationship.

Example Conclusion: If I go to the moon, I will have to wear space shoes.

The Loophole should point out a way for me to go to the moon and not have to wear space shoes. The Loophole should not point out the possibility of me not going to the moon.
