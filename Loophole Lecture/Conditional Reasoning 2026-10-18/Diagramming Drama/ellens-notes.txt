Unofficial Diagramming Marathon

Work backwards from your most recently used sections looking at all the stimuli that allow an opportunity for diagramming. 

Reattempt these questions with a new mindset. 

Try diagramming a few borderline stimuli just so you can learn the line of where diagramming is appropriate. 

The marathon is over when you no longer feel any hesitation diagramming a stimulus or answer choices, diagramming seems boring and rote, and there is no fear left!
