## Ten separate notes blocks preserve examples and exact procedures

- **Main Point:** takeaway standard, author opinion, comparative-passage instruction and conclusion-test comparison of two answers.
- **Tone:** definition, reading-for-tone pointer and instructor's linked “I Should Disappear” material.
- **Function & Organization:** sample stems and functions, plus the full piece-by-piece organization-answer procedure. The notes explicitly require vetting all text of the chosen answer.
- **Purpose:** stems asking about the passage, paragraph, analogy or mention.
- **Specific Reference:** sample stems and a later platform-format caveat that differs from the spoken video.
- **Audience and Source:** several example stems showing the exact scope of each task.
- **Parallel & Principle Conform:** examples, including comparative requirements.
- **Strengthen, Weaken and Resolution:** examples preserving the argument or tension the question targets.
- **Inference & Author Agreement:** examples and an explicit Inference EXCEPT classification.
- **Answer Choice Translation Primer:** three written sections, then three aloud sections; different sections for each phase; no stimulus/stem reading or combining with other drills.

The first question-typing lesson has no separate notes block in the captured page. Its notes-status file records this observation. HTML retains captured links and list structure, while text retains readable wording. A linked resource is a pointer unless separately downloaded and checked.
