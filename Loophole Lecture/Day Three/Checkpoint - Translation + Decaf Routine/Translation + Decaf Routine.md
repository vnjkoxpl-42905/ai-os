## GUIDANCE

To progress through Decaf RC Translation quickly, do at least three Decaf RC Translation Drill sections (no questions) in a row each day. Do not take breaks in between sections or passages.

I know this level of volume is going to feel really hard in the beginning. It may even feel like your brain is melting. It is not melting: it is improving. Stay strong. This is where you're breaking out of your cocoon. You are forging more than a decade of reading habits right now.

Your Law Student Future Self is already thanking you because they're going to be able to do their reading now without feeling like this. If you don't put in this work now, you're going to put it in later when your grade in Torts is on the line.

**Hack to Maintain Your Retention Standard:** Roll a die on Google and if you roll a six, do a full recap of the passage off time after your Translation. This will always keep your Translation strong since you don't know if you'll have to do the full recap.

**The time goal for Translation + CLIR Drills is 20 minutes out loud.** The accommodated time goals for Translation + CLIR Drills are 25 minutes for 53-minute accommodations and 30 minutes for 70-minute accommodations. But remember, meeting this time goal is only valid if you properly RTQ every stimulus out loud and you have a CLIR for every stimulus.

Once you hit the Translation + CLIR time goal, ideally while maintaining accuracy of -5 or better against the CLIR Answer Key, you can move on to the AC Primer + Decaf Routine. We move to the Answer Choice Primer next so we can bring your newfound Translation skills into the answer choices.
