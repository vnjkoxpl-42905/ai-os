Cappuccino RC Translation Drill

NOTE: Cappuccino is similar to Decaf, except instead of recapping all of your Translations after finishing with each paragraph, you’ll be looking back at the text to confirm your Translations.

  1. Read, translate, and quip paragraph 1. Do it just like LR.

  2. Look away from paragraph 1 and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of paragraph 1 silently.

  3. Take a few seconds to confirm your Translation with the paragraph you just read.

    1. In this step, you are not rereading from scratch. 

    2. You're confirming the Translation you already have is right. 

    3. This style of confirming your Translation with the text applies to every confirmation you ever do on RC.

  4. Read, translate, and quip paragraph 2. Do it just like LR.

  5. Look away from paragraph 2 and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of paragraph 2 silently.

  6. Take a few seconds to confirm your Translation with all the text you've read of the passage so far.

    1. This confirmation step will include all the paragraphs you've read so far.

  7. Repeat this pattern for all remaining paragraphs.

  8. OPTIONAL: Look away and recap the FULL TRANSLATION of the entire passage silently.

    1. This step is optional, but you should do it when you first begin Cappucino RC Translation Drills and periodically afterward, just to confirm that you can.

Notes for a Successful Cappuccino RC Translation

  • If you have a 70-minute time accommodation or a 53-minute time accommodation, you can use Cappuccino RC Translation on test day. You do not have to master Cappuccino RC Translation (35 Version) or Caffeinated RC Translation for use on test day, if Cappuccino RC Translation offers superior Translation retention.

Cappuccino RC Translation Drill (35 Version)

  1. Read, translate, and quip every sentence. Max every two sentences if they are really simple. Exactly how you would translate in LR. 

  2. At the end of each paragraph, pause to review the Translation while looking at the paragraph and confirm with yourself that you really know it. 

    1. In this step, you are not rereading from scratch. 

    2. You're confirming the Translation you already have is right. 

    3. This style of confirming your Translation with the text applies to every confirmation you ever do on RC.

  3. After each paragraph, relook at all the paragraphs that you've read so far to confirm they are placed in your memory. 

  4. Continue on until the end of the passage with the same process.

  5. At the end of the passage, do a final review by relooking at the entire passage and reconfirming your Translation. This should take about 30 seconds.
