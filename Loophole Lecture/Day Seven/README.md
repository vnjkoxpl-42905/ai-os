## Ten notes blocks accompany the videos

**How to Translate Comparative Passages** includes a substantive written procedure: finish Passage A including its main point, conceptually highlight A's topics while reading B, relate each B paragraph to A with a quip, and avoid assuming a preset disagreement relationship.

The passage-translation video and all eight Q20–Q27 talkthroughs carry the same separate instruction to try the question first, then watch and compare processes. These nine short notes are source instructions, not additional transcript speech. No notes block was captured on the two other comparative-strategy pages; their status files record this observation.

## Keep the two topic groups distinct

The first three videos discuss comparative-passage technique. The remaining nine videos translate and answer questions on **PT140.S4 Passage 4**, about mirror explanations, observers and mental constructs. That worked passage is not an A/B comparative example. The sidebar groups these sources, but the adjacent ordering does not make them a single comparative demonstration.

The question videos preserve instructor selections and reasoning. They are not an independently verified official answer key. Saved captions may omit or misrecognize parts of stems, choices and explanations; use the source video/audio for review.
