Comparative RC Methods

  1. Highlight the topics that are mentioned in Passage A while you're reading Passage B.

  2. Have at least one quip on each paragraph of Passage B that mentions Passage A in some way (whether it's Passage A as a whole or something mentioned in Passage A).

  3. Do your whole passage process on Passage A before moving to passage B, including inferring out a main point.

  4. Don't assume that there are a pre-set number of ways the comparative passages can relate to one another. They don't have to disagree and you will run into trouble if you assume they do. They might be a general theory and an example; they might be a similar theory discussed in two different contexts; they might be related in any number of other ways as well. They are just two excerpted pieces so they can relate in any way that two pieces of writing can relate to each other.
