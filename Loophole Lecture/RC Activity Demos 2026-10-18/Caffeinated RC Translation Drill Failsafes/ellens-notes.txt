Caffeinated RC Translation Drill FailSafe

First Day:

Do a Caffeinated Translation Drill on a passage with a stopwatch to establish your baseline natural pace.

All Other Days:

Adopt the timing pattern that you earned the previous study day.

Set a countdown timer for your baseline natural pace plus/minus the 20-second adjustment you have earned.

Read, translate, and quip the paragraph silently.

At the end of the paragraph, relook at the paragraph to make sure you have everything and nothing is left out.

Repeat the process on the subsequent paragraphs.

At the end of the passage, take ~30 seconds to review the passage.

Stop the time and note the time down.

Off time, recount the Translation of the entire passage. Check to see if there are any major gaps in your Translation. 

If there are gaps twice in a row, move the time up by 20 seconds.

If there are no gaps twice in a row, move the time down by 20 seconds.

The Standard: If you couldn't look back at the passage, you would still feel comfortable that you have a shot at perfect accuracy in the questions.

Eventual goal is 3:30 to 4:30 timing.
